What a non-standard parcel is
Non-standard parcels are the parcels that, for example:
exceed the maximum weight
exceed the maximum dimensions
are packed in a way that makes it impossible or hard to automatically sort them
are of irregular shape.
We can charge you additional fees if a given carrier considers your parcel non-standard.
When you will need to pay additional fees

We may charge you an additional fee when:
the parcel exceeds the maximum dimensions and weight:
if you dispatch it with a courier
if you dispatch it to parcel lockers and pick-up points
the parcel contains a product enclosed in an external packaging made of metal or wood
the parcel shape is irregular, oval, cylindrical, circular, or tubular — for example, it contains a barrel or tire — and is not fully enclosed in a dispatch container made of corrugated cardboard
the way the parcel was packed makes automatic sorting impossible or difficult.

How you can avoid a surcharge
To avoid an additional fee:
pack the parcel correctly
pack a product of irregular shape into a rectangular cardboard box that does not exceed the maximum dimensions for a given carrier
adjust the thickness and type of cardboard, filling, and protection to the product you want to dispatch — consider its dimensions, shape, weight, and fragility
place the shipping label on the biggest side of the parcel so that is fully visible and not folded.
How to pack problematic products
Parcels containing products such as tires or liquids must be appropriately packaged. Learn how to correctly and safely dispatch this type of orders to avoid surcharges.
How to pack tires if you dispatch them with DPD
Tires must be:
packed sperately
wrapped with tape to which you stick the label.
Place the label so that:
the barcode is on the tire shoulder
it is not covered with tape
it is not folded — especially in the barcode area.
How to pack liquids if you dispatch them with InPost
Liquids must be:
protected from spilling — wrap the bottle cap with tape or foil
sent in a maximum capacity of 5 liters per parcel
of the substance type that is approved for dispatch.
