Additional parcel protection
The parcel price includes insurance up to 500 EUR. However, if you dispatch parcels through Ship with Allegro and enter a value other than 0 PLN in the Insurance value field — we will charge you an
additional insurance fee
. It also applies if you dispatch parcels through the API.
If you want to take advantage of the
insurance included in the price
— leave 0.00 PLN in the
Insurance value
field. It is a
default
value and you do not need to change it. Then, the parcel will be automatically insured under the carrier's liability — up to the amount indicated in the
DHL General Terms & Conditions of Carriage
.
If the value of your parcel
exceeds the value
indicated in the
DHL General Terms & Conditions of Carriage
and you want to benefit from
additional insurance
— enter the amount you want to insure the parcel for in the
Insurance value
field. Then, we will charge you an additional fee according to the
price list
.

If the buyer asks you to change the delivery address, you can do so while dispatching the parcel. However, you cannot change the delivery option — for example, from delivery to a pick-up point to delivery to a parcel locker.

What if the buyer does not collect the parcel
If the buyer does not collect the parcel, and it is returned to you, for each such return, you will pay:
18.99 PLN — for purchases made on allegro.pl
3.99 EUR — for purchases made on allegro.sk.

We cover the costs of returning parcels uncollected by the buyers. If the buyer does not collect the order made on allegro.sk and the parcel is returned to you, you will pay only a symbolic amount for each such return — 0.25 EUR, which equals 1 PLN.

How to file a complaint
You can file a complaint in 2 ways:
on your own to the carrier
― if the parcel was damaged or lost
through Allegro
― when the complaint concerns deliveries you dispatched via the Ship with Allegro tool without an agreement with the carrier.
In that case, contact us (via chat, email, or phone) to file a complaint concerning:
delivery service fee
fees for additional services
damage or loss of the parcel.
If you file a complaint and the fees turn out to have been improperly charged, the carrier sends us a correction, which we will later add to your account. If the complaint concerns damage or loss of the parcel and you file it without Allegro's involvement, the correction will go directly to the bank account number you have indicated.
