Our international delivery option is carried out via the DPD carrier as part of the
delivery options settled within Allegro Standard
. This allows you to dispatch products from Poland to 23 EU countries quickly and conveniently.
We want you to reach as many buyers as possible not only in Poland, but also abroad.
Allegro DPD Courier — dispatch abroad from Poland
will make shipping abroad as easy and convenient as shipping within Poland.

You want to know more about dispatching products abroad? Visit our page about
foreign marketplaces
.

How much you will pay for Allegro DPD Courier international parcels
Types of service within the Allegro DPD Courier international dispatch:
basic service fee
― applies to parcels up to 10 kg. It is a fixed fee for a given destination country covered by the buyer,
additional service fee
― applies to parcels between 10 and 20 kg, or between 20 and 31.5 kg. The seller covers the additional service cost ― the buyer pays the price for the basic service that the seller sets in the price list.

What the maximum parcel dimensions and weight are
The maximum dimensions and weight of parcels dispatched with the Allegro DPD Courier — dispatch abroad from Poland option:
maximum weight (dimensional or actual weight) —  10 kg
maximum length of the longest side of the parcel — 175 cm
sum of sides (2 x width + 2 x length + height) = max. 300 cm
Parcel weight is calculated according to the higher parameter:
actual weight — how much the parcel weighs when placed on a scale or
dimensional weight — related to the size of your parcel. We calculate it with the following formula: [height (cm) x length (cm) x width (cm)] / 5,000.

On January 1, 2024, we changed how we calculate the dimensional weight of parcels dispatched with the Allegro DPD Courier — dispatch abroad from Poland delivery option. The new formula we use to calculate the dimensional weight: [height (cm) x length (cm) x width (cm)] / 5,000.

How parcel returns work
If the buyer does not collect their parcel or the courier does not find the recipient at the indicated address, the parcel will be sent back to you at your cost.
For returning an uncollected parcel, you will pay:
18.99 PLN — for purchases made on allegro.pl (regardless of the country of delivery)
115 CZK* — for purchases made on allegro.cz
3.99 EUR* — for purchases made on allegro.sk
1,490 HUF* — for purchases made on allegro.hu.
If the parcel is heavier than 10 kg, we will charge you the additional service fee according to the
price list
.
If the buyer collects the parcel and decides to return it, they should arrange the return of the product at their own expense. You will not be charged any additional fees.

We cover the costs of returning parcels uncollected by the buyers. If the buyer does not collect the order made on allegro.cz, allegro.sk, or allegro.hu, and the parcel is returned to you, you will pay only a symbolic amount which equals 1 PLN for each such return — 5 CZK, 0.25 EUR, or 90 HUF.
