Maximum parcel dimensions and weight
Maximum dimensions
the longest side — 150 cm
parcel size — sum of length and circumference (length + 2 x height + 2 x width) — max. 300 cm
Maximum weight
basic weight — maximum parcel weight without additional fees — 10 kg
maximum parcel weight you can dispatch through Ship with Allegro — 31.5 kg
actual weight — how much the parcel weighs when placed on a scale — 10 kg
dimensional weight (also called volumetric or volume weight by some carriers) — it is calculated based on your parcel's dimensions and the formula used by the given carrier.
For the Allegro DPD Courier delivery option, we calculate it with the following formula: [length (cm) x width (cm) x height (cm)] / 6,000.
We base the settlement on the higher of those values.
When you dispatch a parcel, the carrier takes two weights into account: actual and dimensional. If your parcel exceeds any of them, you cannot dispatch it.

What if the buyer does not collect the parcel
If the buyer does not collect the parcel, the carrier will send it back to you at your expense. For returning an uncollected parcel, you will pay 14.99 PLN.
If the buyer collects the parcel and decides to return it, they will do it for free
within Allegro Smart!
or at their own expense. We will not charge you any additional fees.
How to dispatch parcels
You can dispatch parcels
only
through our free tool —
Ship with Allegro
— or through integrating external tools (for example, Base) with the Ship with Allegro API.
You can dispatch parcels
just as you would with other delivery options

Read
our articles on Ship with Allegro

You can dispatch parcels:
during a one-time courier pick-up — the courier will collect the parcels from the address you provide in Ship with Allegro. The service itself costs 4.99 PLN, and if you hand over at least 4 parcels to the courier — the pick-up order is free of charge. We will add these costs to
Settlements with Allegro
on your account, in the month following the month in which the service was performed.
during a fixed courier pick-up — the courier will collect the parcel for free from the address you give to the carrier
in DPD Pickup parcel dispatch points
at DPD parcel lockers.

If the buyer asks you to change the delivery address, you can do so only when dispatching the parcel. However, you cannot change the delivery option — for example, from delivery to a pick-up point to delivery to a parcel locker.

DPD can also pick up your parcels on Saturdays and deliver them to buyers on Mondays. You can use the service
for free

form

You can order fixed pickups by 12:00 p.m. on Friday before the Saturday you want the fixed pickups to start.

Additional Parcel Protection value
field — we will charge you an
additional insurance fee

field. It is a default value, and you do not need to change it. Then, the parcel will be automatically insured under the carrier's civil liability.
If the value of your parcel

additional insurance
— enter the amount you want to insure the parcel for in the
Additional Parcel Protection value

price list

How to dispatch a parcel outside Ship with Allegro
Besides Ship with Allegro, you can dispatch parcels with the following delivery options:
Allegro DPD Courier
Allegro DPD Courier payment on delivery.
If you want to settle these delivery options directly with us, you have to:
provide the correct email address in the carrier's dispatch tool
have a login and password to the carrier's dispatch tool
set the correct maximum prices in the shipping price lists.

If you want to settle other carrier's delivery options with us,
you have to
dispatch parcels with Ship with Allegro.

Provide DPD with the correct email address
For every order, provide the correct email address of the buyer. The carrier's dispatch system recognizes that you are dispatching a parcel based on the customer's email address, and you will be able to settle it directly with us.
When dispatching a parcel, always enter the full and correct email address of the customer. A full email address consists of two parts, connected by a "+" sign, in the @allegromail.pl domain. You can find it in sales notifications and order details.
If you provide an incorrect buyer's email address or do not provide it at all for a given order:
you will settle this order with the carrier, according to the rates specified by your agreement with them
in case of an Allegro Smart! parcel — you will not receive a payment from the buyer or us, so you will have to pay the full delivery cost charged by the carrier.
Login and password to the DPD dispatch tool
If you want to generate labels and dispatch parcels outside Ship with Allegro, contact DPD directly.
The parcels you settle with Allegro under your agreement with DPD:
are included in the parcel pool within your subscription — you will settle the subscription directly with DPD
are included in the parcel limit
are not included in the
system readiness fee
(available in Polish).

If you use DPD services through a third-party provider:
find out whether the provider is going to charge you additional fees for parcels dispatched with Allegro delivery options
check if the provider exchanges information with us to find out which parcels you are settling directly with us.
