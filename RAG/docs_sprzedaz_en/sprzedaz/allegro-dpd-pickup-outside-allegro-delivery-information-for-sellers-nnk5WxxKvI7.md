The maximum parcel dimensions and weight
The maximum dimensions and weight of parcels you can dispatch with the Allegro DPD Pickup delivery option
parcel weight (actual or dimensional) = max. 20 kg
length of the longest side of the parcel = max. 150 cm
sum of the height and the circumference (2 x length + 2 x width) = max. 300 cm.
Parcel dimensions
are the same for every dispatch point
. The parcel weight is calculated according to its actual or dimensional weight. We consider the higher value for settlements.
actual weight — how much the parcel weighs when placed on the DPD scale
dimensional weight — related to the size of your parcel. We calculate it with the following formula: [height (cm) x length (cm) x width (cm)] / 6,000.

You dispatch a parcel that weighs
3.5 kg
. Its dimensions are
40 cm x 30 cm x 20 cm
— meaning the dimensional weight is
4 kg
.
DPD will account for the higher value — 4 kg — while settling the parcel.

Note
Always check the dimensional weight of the parcels you dispatch. Pay particular attention to it when it comes to large lightweight parcels.
A minor difference in the dimensions of the parcel can make you pay the prices in the higher weight range.
How to dispatch a parcel

Among the documents available for download from the
carrier’s page
, you can find the instructions on how to properly pack a parcel.

You can dispatch parcels:
during a one-time courier pick-up — the courier will collect the parcels from the address you provide in Ship with Allegro. The service itself costs 4.99 PLN, and if you hand over at least 4 parcels to the courier — the pick-up order is free of charge. We will add these costs to
Settlements with Allegro
on your account, in the month following the month in which the service was performed.
during a fixed courier pick-up — the courier will collect the parcel for free from the address you give to the carrier
in DPD Pickup parcel dispatch points
at DPD parcel lockers.
You can dispatch parcels with
Allegro DPD Pickup
and
Allegro DPD Pickup payment on delivery

You do not have to conclude an agreement with a carrier to use Allegro DPD Pickup and dispatch parcels.

Complaints
You can file complaints about:
the delivery fee
fees for additional services
parcel damage or loss.
There are two ways of filing a complaint:
on your own, through our complaint form in the
Shipments and pickups
tab. Select the order you want to file a complaint about, open the order details, and click [complain about delivery]. Use this form if your complaint concerns parcels you dispatch in the Ship with Allegro tool without a separate agreement with the carrier.
directly through the form on the DPD website — you will find it in the My DPD tab.
Make sure to include our power of attorney when filing such a complaint.
All you need to do is change the language settings to Polish and click [pobierz pełnomocnictwo] (download the power of attorney) in the Reklamacje dostaw (Delivery complaints) section.
If you file a complaint and the fees prove to be improperly charged, we will settle them on your account.
If the complaint relates to damage or loss of a parcel, and you file it without our involvement, the correction will go directly to the bank account number you indicated.
