Check the rules of packing parcels dispatched with Allegro International delivery options.
Do not ship
forbidden products
.
Adjust the packaging to the value and type of the product you ship. Make sure it is packed securely and insured, especially if it is of high value.
Adjust the packaging to the product size. Make sure it does not move inside the packaging. You can fill empty spaces with cardboard, bubble wrap, or special airbags.
Ship cuboid-shaped parcels. Parcels with irregular shapes or protruding elements are more prone to damage.
Products you ship with the
Allegro International
delivery options must come in the original and sealed packaging that protects the product from:
spilling or leaking
accessing its content freely
opening without visible signs on the packaging
mechanical damage and free movement of the product inside the packaging
dirt.
A parcel should be cuboid-shaped — you must not ship parcels:
with irregular shapes
with protruding elements
packed in metal or wooden shipping containers.
You should stick the shipping label on the parcel's visible side — it should be in whole, not folded. Stick the label in a way that protects it from removal or accidental tearing.
How to prepare shipping labels
Printing the shipping label
You can print the shipping label in the Ship with Allegro form or the
Shipments and pickups
tab. Depending on the printer, select the file format in the
Label printing — Ship with Allegro
settings:
ZPL
PDF and the paper size: A4 or A6.
The shipping label
has to
be clear and contrasting and
cannot
contain white parts or elements that do not stand out from the white background.

Labels that are damaged or unclear can cause a delay in delivery or even result in returning the parcel to the sender.



Placing labels on parcels
The shipping label
should
be placed on an even surface of the parcel, and it
cannot
be folded:

None of the barcodes on the label can be covered by other labels/stickers.
Other codes, graphics, or text cannot cover the barcodes.
The shipping label
cannot be inside a sheet protector
or covered by transparent or colored sticky tape.

The maximum dimensions and weight of parcels
Check the maximum parcel dimensions and weight in
articles about specific Allegro International delivery options
.
Forbidden products
You must not ship
forbidden products
.
You cannot ship, for example:
products obtained illegally
money, securities, or other payment instruments
valuables, such as jewelry, works of art, antiques, numismatics, etc.
weapons whose purchase and possession require a permit, a registration, or a registration card, under Polish law
ammunition, except for blank ammunition with a caliber of up to 6 mm
chemically and biologically active items
agents that are explosive, inflammable, oxidizing, irritating, caustic, sensitizing, cancerous, mutagenic, or harming fertility, and that may pose a threat to human health or life, or cause property damage to the logistics company
other items whose features may pose a threat to health or life upon contact with them, or that may damage other parcels
narcotics, psychotropic substances, or so-called designer drugs
products with short shelf-life
products that require special transport conditions (including products that smell or leak)
dead or live animals and plants
human corpses, human or animal organs.
