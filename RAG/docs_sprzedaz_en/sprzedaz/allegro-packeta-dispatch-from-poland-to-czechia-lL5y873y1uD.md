How to hand over parcels for dispatch
If Poczta Polska is the carrier:
during courier pick-up — if you have an agreement with Poczta Polska for the collection of Business Mail, or you use
fixed pickups
at any Poczta Polska post office.
If ORLEN Paczka is the carrier:
only
during a free ORLEN Paczka courier pickup. You cannot dispatch those parcels at the ORLEN Paczka pick-up points or parcel lockers.

An ORLEN Paczka courier can also collect
Allegro Delivery
parcels from you. However, they will not collect Allegro Packeta parcels handled by Poczta Polska. Hand your Allegro Packeta parcels over to a Poczta Polska courier — as part of your agreement for parcel pickups.

You will find information about the carrier handling the parcel on the shipping label.
No matter how you dispatch your parcels — Poczta Polska or ORLEN Paczka will forward them, and Packeta will deliver them to buyers in Czechia.

Maximum dimensions and weight of parcels dispatched to pick-up points
Maximum parcel dimensions:
length of the longest side of the parcel — 120 cm
the sum of 3 sides' length — up to 150 cm (for example, 60 x 40 x 50 cm).
Maximum parcel weight — 15 kg.
We determine the parcel weight based on the actual weight. That means we consider how much the parcel weighs when placed on a scale. We do not calculate the dimensional weight.
Maximum dimensions and weight of parcels dispatched to parcel lockers
Maximum parcel dimensions — 35 x 45 x 60 cm.
Maximum parcel weight: 15 kg.

If you dispatch a larger parcel — and its dimensions do not exceed the limit for the Allegro Packeta delivery options —
it will be delivered to the nearest pick-up point
.

Complaints
You can file a complaint in two ways:
with the carrier on your own ― if the parcel was damaged or lost:
through the form
(available in Polish) ― if you have an account on the Packeta website
by email:
reklamacje@packeta.pl
through Allegro ― when the complaint concerns deliveries you dispatched via the Ship with Allegro tool without an agreement with the carrier.
In that case, report to us (via chat, email, or phone) a complaint concerning:
the delivery fee
fees for additional services
damage or loss of the parcel.
If you file a complaint and it turns out that the fees are miscalculated — the carrier will send us a correction we will later include in your bill. If the complaint concerns damage or loss of the parcel and you file it without Allegro's involvement, the correction will go directly to the bank account number you have indicated.
