What the program is all about
Every day, we use the
Allegro Prices
algorithm to select products that are recommended for a discount. We choose products whose sales may increase if we reduce their price appropriately — they will then be much more attractive to buyers.
We select the best offer for each of those products and automatically reduce its price.

The discount percentage always depends on the price in that offer and the market prices. You will find current information on your participation in the program in the
Allegro Prices
tab.

If your offer is among those selected for a price reduction and the buyer takes advantage of the lower price and orders from you — we will give you
compensation equal to the discount value
. We will refund you the difference between your price and the reduced price — in the form of a discount on our services. You will find it on your account a few minutes after the sale:
in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab
in the
Funds and Operations History
tab as an
adjustment in the Allegro Prices program
.
We select offers for automatic discounts based on
specific criteria
. If you want to
increase the chances
of your offer being selected — you can
declare that you will also cover part of the reduction of the base price
. If our algorithm selects such an offer, we will cover the discount cost together — in a proportion of at least 1:2. It means that our contribution will always be at least twice as much as yours. For every 1 PLN by which you reduce the price for the buyer, we will reduce it by at least 2 PLN more at our expense.

Example 1: when we cover the entire discount cost (without your declaration)
Your base price is 100 PLN.
We automatically reduce it by 5%.
After the automatic price reduction, the buyer pays 95 PLN.
The discount value is 5 PLN (100 PLN – 95 PLN) — of which we refund you the entire 5 PLN in the form of a discount on our invoice.
You do not participate in the discount cost. You receive 95 PLN from the buyer, and we compensate you for the entire discount value — 5 PLN. You lose nothing, because you still receive 100 PLN: the product price (95 PLN) plus the compensation amount (5 PLN).
Example 2: when you cover the discount cost together with us (based on your declaration)
Your base price is 100 PLN.
You declare that you can offer a maximum discount of 3%.
We automatically reduce the price by 6% — of which we cover 4% of the discount, and you cover 2% (within the limits of your declaration).
After the automatic price reduction, the buyer pays 94 PLN.
The discount value is 6 PLN (100 PLN – 94 PLN), of which:
we compensate you for 4 PLN in the form of a discount on our invoice
you cover 2 PLN yourself.
You receive 94 PLN from the buyer, and we compensate you for ⅔ of the discount cost. Your price has been reduced by 6 PLN, so we will refund you 4 PLN. The only cost you cover will be the remaining 2 PLN.

Declarations are
optional
and do not affect your participation in the program. Offers without a declaration may still be selected for the automatic price reduction. In such situations, we will always cover the entire discount cost — like in example 1 above.

How much it costs to participate in the Allegro Prices program
By default, participation in the Allegro Prices program is free
. You incur costs only if three conditions are met for a given offer:
you declare an additional discount in that offer yourself
our algorithm selects this offer and automatically reduces its price for the buyer
the buyer orders from you in that offer.
You will only incur costs when a sale is made. If no one places an order in the offer with your declaration and the automatic discount — you will not incur any costs.
In such a situation, it is up to you to decide how much you are willing to pay. You declare the maximum discount percentage you can offer yourself. If our algorithm selects your offer,
your participation in the costs will never exceed your own declaration
. It may even be lower — if our analysis confirms that a smaller discount will be sufficient. If we decide to significantly reduce the price in your offer — for example, by 25% instead of 2% — your participation will be the declared 2%, and we will cover the rest.
You will always receive the refund as a discount on our services. You will find it in the
Funds and Operations History
tab as an
adjustment in the Allegro Prices program
.
How to declare the maximum discount you can cover
Go to the
Allegro Prices
tab.
You will see the list of all offers you can manage within the program. Next to each offer, in the
Max. declared price reduction % from you
column, we will display your current settings for that offer. It is set to
0%

If you want to manage offers in another marketplace, select it in the
market
filter.

Declare a price reduction for that offer. Click the pencil icon next to the discount percentage you want to edit.
Enter the maximum discount percentage you want to declare in that offer. You can enter a number between 0 and 100. To set a decimal number, enter it with a period — for example,
3.5
to set 3.5%.
Click [enter] to confirm.
Done! In the
Max. declared price reduction % from you
column, you will see the updated percentage. In the
Max. declared price reduction from you
column, we will convert it into an actual discount amount based on the current offer price.
You can change your discount declaration at any time. You can also make these changes to multiple offers at once. To do this, select the offers on the list and click [declare a price reduction].

The maximum discount percentage from you is just a declaration. The offer price will not change the moment you set it. It only serves as information for us — with how much you are willing to reduce the price for the buyer further if we select your offer and cover part of the discount.

How to edit your maximum declared discount
In the
Allegro Prices
tab, we display all offers in which you have declared a discount of more than 0%, in the [with a declared price reduction] view. You can edit the declared discount percentage there at any moment — for example, increase it or set it at 0%. You will do it
in the same way as when you set the discount for the first time
.
What the recommendations with a suggested reduction declaration value are
For offers with the highest chance of getting a price reduction, we are preparing recommendations with a suggested value for your declaration. You can find them next to offers in the
Allegro Prices
tab. Thanks to them, you do not have to analyze prices yourself.

You will see recommendations next to offers that do not yet have a price reduction declaration, but could still have a real chance of winning the next qualifications thanks to them. We can also add them next to offers with a declaration whose value is too low to win qualifications.

How to use recommendations
Click [apply] next to the selected recommendation. Our system will automatically take this value into consideration during the next qualifications. You decide whether to use our hints or not. You can enter your own reduction value at any time.
