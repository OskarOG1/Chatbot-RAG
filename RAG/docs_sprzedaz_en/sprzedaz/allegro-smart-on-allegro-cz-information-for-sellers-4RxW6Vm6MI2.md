Your offers can also be Smart! on allegro.cz. Just like on allegro.pl, we will mark your offers with Smart! badge if you meet specified requirements. However, conditions on allegro.cz will differ from those on allegro.pl. We have adapted them to the needs of Czech customers — to make offers with Smart! badge even more appealing to them.
When we will mark offers with Smart! badge
We will mark your offers on allegro.cz with Smart! badge if:
you enable in your offers the following delivery options:
Allegro International Courier Czechia
and
Allegro International Pick-up Point Czechia
or
Allegro International Parcel Lockers Czechia
the
sales quality level
on your account is higher than Neutral,
or
it is at least Neutral on all of your accounts
you enable at least 1 courier delivery option in your offers
and
at least 1 delivery option to a pick-up point or a parcel locker
you enable at least one option with payment in advance
and
at least one option with payment on delivery in your offers
you provide an address for returns in Poland or Czechia in your offers
in the
Delivery Settings
tab, you provide prices in the required ranges for
options you enable
.

If you sell products that cannot be dispatched with the Allegro International Czechia delivery options or do not fit into a parcel locker or a pick-up point — for example, due to their size or weight. If you list offers with such products and do not enable delivery with Allegro International Czechia or delivery to a pick-up point and/or a parcel locker — you do not need to meet those requirements. We will mark your offers with Smart! badge if you meet the conditions regarding sales quality and return address.
If you sell custom-made products. If you list offers with such products, you do not need to enable payment on delivery options in them.

How we settle Allegro Smart! parcels
Buyers who place an order within Allegro Smart! do not pay for delivery. That means you will receive:
only payment for the purchase — if they paid in advance
or
payment for the purchase and additional fee for the payment on delivery option:
29 CZK — for deliveries to pick-up points and parcel lockers (except for DPD delivery options)
39 CZK — for courier deliveries and all DPD delivery options.
We split the delivery cost of such orders between you and us. We charge fees depending on the order value. We take into account 5 thresholds of order value:
from 179 CZK to 268.99 CZK
from 269 CZK to 378.99 CZK
from 379 CZK to 588.99 CZK
589 CZK to 878,99 CZK
879 CZK or more.

When buyers can make a Smart! purchase
On allegro.cz, buyers can get free delivery and returns within Allegro Smart! if:
they have an active Allegro Smart! membership on their accounts
the total price of purchase they make from one seller is at least:
299 CZK — for pick-up points and parcel lockers
499 CZK — for courier deliveries
the delivery cost from one seller does not exceed 1,000 CZK.
If the buyer selects payment on delivery, we will add the following amount to the amount from the table for the additional payment on delivery service:
in the case of parcel lockers and pick-up points (except for DPD delivery options) — 29 CZK (apart from the payment for your purchase, you will receive from the buyer an additional 29 CZK for that option)
in the case of courier delivery and all DPD delivery options — 39 CZK (apart from the payment for your purchase, you will receive from the buyer an additional 39 CZK for that option).
When you will be refunded for Smart! parcel fees
If your customer:
returns the purchase as part of withdrawing from the agreement (they report the return through the return form on their Allegro account and dispatch it with a return option available in Allegro Smart!)
or
does not collect the parcel
we will automatically
refund you the fee for delivery of the Smart! parcel
. You do not have to apply to get a refund.
Only the basic fee will be refunded — not the fees for additional services. You can see it as a discount in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab.
We will refund you the fee once the status of the return parcel says that you have either collected it or refunded the order cost.

Your customer wants to return a Smart! order worth 350 CZK delivered with Allegro International Parcel Lockers Czechia.
They send it back to you using a free Smart! return. You collect the return parcel.
We refund you 18.49 CZK.

If a buyer returns a part of an order, we will recalculate the order value — this time without the returned products. If the new order value is low enough to qualify for the lower price range, we will refund you the difference.
