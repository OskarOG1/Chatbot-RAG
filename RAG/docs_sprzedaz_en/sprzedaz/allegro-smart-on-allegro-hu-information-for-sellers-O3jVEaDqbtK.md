When we will mark offers with the Smart! badge
We will mark your offers on allegro.hu with the Smart! badge if:
the
sales quality level
on your account is at least
Good
or at least
Neutral
on all of your accounts
in your offers, you enabled at least 1 courier delivery option
and
at least 1 delivery option to a pick-up point or a parcel locker
in your offers, you enabled at least 1 delivery option with payment in advance
and
and least 1 with payment on delivery
in the
Delivery Settings
tab, you provide prices in the required ranges for the options you offer
you do not list offers in
categories excluded from Allegro Smart!
you have set the
returns terms
, including an address to which the buyer can send the product back. The return address can be located in Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, or Hungary.
When you do not need to meet all the requirements
If you sell products that cannot be placed in a parcel locker or delivered to a pick-up point — for example, due to their size or weight. If you list offers with such products and do not enable delivery to a pick-up point and/or a parcel locker — you do not need to meet that requirement. We will mark your offers with the Smart! badge if you meet the other two requirements.
If you sell custom-made products, you do not need to enable payment on delivery options.

How we settle Allegro Smart! parcels

only the payment for the purchase — if they paid in advance
or
payment for the purchase and an additional fee of 490 HUF for the POD option.
We split the delivery cost of such orders between you and us. We charge fees depending on the order value. We take into account 5 thresholds of order value:
from 2,800 HUF to 4,199 HUF
from 4,200 HUF to 5,999 HUF
from 6,000 HUF to 9,199 HUF
from 9,200 HUF to 13,899 HUF
13,900 HUF or more.

When buyers will make a Smart! purchase
On allegro.hu, buyers can get free delivery and returns within Allegro Smart! if:
they have activated the Allegro Smart! service on their account
they purchase from a single seller for a minimum of:
5,000 HUF — for deliveries to pick-up points and parcel lockers
9,000 HUF  — for courier deliveries
the delivery cost from one seller does not exceed 20,000 HUF.
When you will be refunded for Smart! parcel fees
If your customer:
returns the purchase as part of withdrawing from the agreement (they report the return through the return form on their Allegro account and dispatch it with a return option available in Allegro Smart!)
or
does not collect the parcel
we will automatically
refund you the fee for delivery of the Smart! parcel
. You do not have to apply to get a refund.
Only the basic fee will be refunded — not the fees for additional services.  You can see it as a discount in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab.
We will refund you the fee once the status of the return parcel says that you have either collected it or refunded the order cost.

Your customer wants to return a Smart! order worth 10,000 HUF delivered with Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Hungary — Packeta Parcel Lockers.
They send it back to you using a free Smart! return. You collect the return parcel.
We refund you 700 HUF.

If a buyer returns a part of an order, we will recalculate the order value — this time without the returned products. If the new order value is low enough to qualify for the lower price range, we will refund you the difference.
