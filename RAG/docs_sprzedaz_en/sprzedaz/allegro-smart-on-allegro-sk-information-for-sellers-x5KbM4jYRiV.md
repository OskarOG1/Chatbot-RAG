Your offers can also be Smart! on allegro.sk. Just like we do on allegro.pl, we will mark your offers with the Smart! badge if you meet specified requirements. However, requirements on allegro.sk differ from those on allegro.pl. We have adapted them to the needs of Slovak customers — to make offers with Smart! badge even more appealing to them.
When we will mark offers with the Smart! badge
We will mark your offers on allegro.sk with the Smart! badge if:
the
sales quality level
on your account is higher than Neutral or it is at least Neutral on all of your accounts
in your offers, you enable at least 1 courier delivery option and at least 1 delivery option to a pick-up point or a parcel locker from the
list of qualifying delivery options
you enable at least one option with payment in advance and at least one option with payment on delivery in your offers
you provide an address for returns in Poland, Czechia, or Slovakia in your offers
in the
Delivery Settings
tab, you provide prices in the
required ranges
for the options you enable
you list offers in categories that allow them to be marked with the Smart! badge Check the
list of categories in which offers cannot be Smart!

If you sell products that cannot be put in a parcel locker or delivered to a pick-up point — for example, due to their size or weight. If you list offers with such products and you do not add delivery to a pick-up point and/or a parcel locker, but you meet other conditions — we will mark your offers with the Smart! badge.
If you sell custom-made products. If you list offers with such products, you do not need to enable payment on delivery options in them.

How we settle Allegro Smart! parcels
Buyers who place an order within Allegro Smart! do not pay for delivery.
That means you will receive:
only payment for the purchase — for orders paid in advance or
payment for the purchase and 0.99 EUR for the additional payment on delivery service — for orders with payment on delivery.
We split the Allegro Smart! delivery cost between you and us. We charge fees depending on the order value.
We take into account 5 thresholds of order value:
from 6.90 EUR to 10.39 EUR
from 10.40 EUR to 14.99 EUR
from 15.00 EUR to 22.99 EUR
from 23.00 EUR to 34.49 EUR
34.50 EUR and more.

When buyers make a Smart! purchase On allegro.sk, buyers can get free delivery and returns within Allegro Smart! if:
they have an active Allegro Smart! membership on their accounts
they shop from a single seller for a minimum of:
12.90 EUR — for deliveries to pick-up points and parcel lockers
21.90 EUR — for courier deliveries
the delivery cost from one seller does not exceed 50 EUR.
When you will be refunded for Smart! parcel fees
If your customer:
returns the purchase as part of withdrawing from the agreement (they report the return through the return form on their Allegro account and dispatch it with a return option available in Allegro Smart!)
or
does not collect the parcel
we will automatically
refund you the fee for delivery of the Smart! parcel
. You do not have to apply to get a refund.
Only the basic fee will be refunded — not the fees for additional services. You can see it as a discount in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab.
We will refund you the fee once the status of the return parcel says that you have either collected it or refunded the order cost.
