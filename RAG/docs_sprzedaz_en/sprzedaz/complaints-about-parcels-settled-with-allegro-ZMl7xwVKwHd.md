Who and when can file a complaint about the parcel
General rules
As a seller
dispatching
the parcel to the buyer, you can
always
file a complaint if the parcel has not been delivered to the buyer yet.
The buyer may file a complaint about the parcel only after it is successfully delivered to them, that is, a courier hands it over to them, or they pick it up at a pick-up point or from a parcel locker.
If the buyer files the complaint after the parcel has been delivered, you cannot file another one.
In case of a delayed, damaged, or lost parcel
If the parcel is in delivery, as the seller, file a complaint directly with the carrier under the
power of attorney
.
If the parcel is delivered, the buyer may file a complaint with the carrier as the parcel owner or contact you. Then, on their behalf, you can file a complaint only if the buyer sends you a waiver of claims.
In case of a parcel with a consumer return
When the buyer sends you a return parcel, and it is delayed or damaged
If the return parcel is delivered to you, as the seller, you have the right to file a complaint with the carrier.
If the parcel with the returned products has not been delivered to you yet, the buyer, as the sender, has the right to file a complaint. As the seller, you may file a complaint with the carrier on behalf of the buyer only based on the waiver from the buyer:

What the complaint should include

Sender and recipient's details, as well as information on who is filing a complaint.
The ID number of a document confirming the dispatch or the tracking number.
A complaint description, in which you should give the reason for filing the complaint and the compensation amount — in case you demand it.
The bank's name and account number to which the compensation should be transferred.
All the documents confirming the transaction conclusion (email correspondence, sales agreement confirmations, the link to the offer on Allegro, transfer confirmation, a receipt, or a VAT invoice).
The original damage report — if the parcel has been damaged.
In the case of an insured parcel — insurance information.
If the complaint is filed by the recipient — a statement in which the sender relinquishes the right to vindicate a claim in favor of the recipient.
If the buyer collected the parcel and it is the sender who files the complaint — a statement in which the recipient relinquishes the right to vindicate a claim in favor of the sender.
If the parcel is damaged — the recipient's statement of damage filed at an InPost facility directly after receiving the parcel (or within 7 days of the parcel pick-up in case of damage that is not visible at pick-up).
