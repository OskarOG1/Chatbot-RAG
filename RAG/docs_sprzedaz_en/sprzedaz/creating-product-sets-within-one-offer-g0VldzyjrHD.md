You can list
multipacks (product sets)
using the
listing form
or through the
API
.
You can also use sets created by the manufacturer, in the Allegro Product catalog. In that case, you do not need to add every element of that set as a separate product. Sets created by the manufacturer come with their own GTIN (EAN) or manufacturer's codes.
Additionally, you can list
more than one product
in an offer in selected categories. See the
category list
.
