You can enable additional services within your offers. You can manage them in the
Additional Services for the Offer
tab and through the
Allegro API
.
What additional services you can offer
giftwrap
home delivery
home delivery and set-up
home delivery and placement
installation/assembly
home delivery and installation/assembly
home delivery, installation/assembly, and set-up.
If you offer additional services, buyers will see relevant information on the offer page. According to
Appendix no. 4
to the Allegro Terms & Conditions,
we do not charge any fees for offering additional services
.

You can use
our calculator
to check how much you will pay the carriers whose services you settle through Allegro Mix for the carry-in.

If you offer additional services, buyers can see this information on the offer page.
To see the details, click the arrow on the right, in the
Additional services
section.
A buyer can select additional services while buying with the buy now option or via cart.
How to enable additional services in your offer
Go to the
Additional services
tab.
Click [add service].
Select the additional services you want to offer to your customers.
Enter the service price and description.
Choose the delivery option for which you want to enable the selected service. You can select only those which you have added to the delivery price list for a given offer.
If you select delivery options which you do not have in the price list, buyers will not be able to benefit from the additional services you offer.
Click [add package].
You can add the assembled package to your offers:
through the
My Assortment
tab
in the listing form.

In the Additional services tab, next to each package of additional service, you can find a badge informing for which marketplace a given package was created.
You can only add additional services for a single offer in one marketplace at a time. If you have added additional services to the offer on allegro.pl, you cannot add them to the offer you share in a foreign marketplace — or the other way around.
If you want to list an offer with additional services only in a foreign marketplace:
Sign in to that marketplace.
List a new offer.
Add additional services.

If you change the service price after adding the package, we will update all the offers where this service is enabled.
How to add additional services to multiple offers at the same time
Go to the
My Assortment
tab.
Select the offers in which you want to enable an additional service package.
Click [edit] and then [additional services].
Select the service package you created and click [save].
Done! The customers are now able to select an additional service on those offers.

in the
delivery and payment form
completion notification
in REST API in the
GET/order/checkout-forms

in the
Orders
