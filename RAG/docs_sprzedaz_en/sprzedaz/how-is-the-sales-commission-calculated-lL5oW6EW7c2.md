What the sales commission amount depends on
The sales commission amount is not the same for all offers. It depends on:
the final price for which you sell the product — that is the highest bid in an auction or the buy now price
the category in which you list a given product
the number of products sold in a given offer — in multi-product offers, multiply the sales commission for 1 unit by the number of sold units
whether the buyer pays for the delivery.
When charging the sales commission amount, apart from the price of the product, we
will also take into account its delivery cost
. If the buyer does not pay for the delivery — because it costs 0 PLN or the purchase is made within Allegro Smart! — we will not add its cost to the sales commission amount.
If the buyer makes a purchase within Allegro Smart! and selects the POD option — they will pay
4.99 PLN
for each parcel in the order. We will take it into account while charging the sales commission.

You sell a product worth 100 PLN from a category covered by a 6% sales commission. Your customer chooses delivery for 18 PLN. The 6% sales commission of 118 PLN is therefore 7.08 PLN.

You sell a product worth 100 PLN from a category covered by a 6% sales commission. Your customer chooses delivery to a pick-up point within Allegro Smart! The 6% sales commission of 100 PLN is therefore 6 PLN. The delivery option selected by the buyer is free of charge — that is why the sales commission will include only the product cost.

You sell a product worth 100 PLN from a category covered by the 6% sales commission together with a product worth 50 PLN from a category covered with the 8% sales commission. For those two products, your customer chooses delivery for 18 PLN. The delivery cost will be divided between the purchased products 9 PLN each. The 6% sales commission of 109 PLN is 6.54 PLN. The 8% sales commission of 59 PLN is 4.72 PLN.

When we charge the sales commission
We charge the sales commission
at the moment when the customer makes a purchase in your offer
— that means when they win an auction or use the buy now option. We round up the sales commission to 0.01 PLN.
Sales commission in featured offers
If you sell a product in an offer with the active Feature, Flexible Feature, or Promo Package — we will charge an
additional sales commission
.
Check the details
.
Categories
We do not charge the sales commission in the following categories
: Cars, Motorcycles and Quads, Other Vehicles and Boats, Trailers, Semitrailers, Construction Machines, Real Estate, Services, Vacations, and Live Animals. It means that in those categories you will
only pay for listing an offer
.

What transaction rebate (sales commission refund) you will get if the buyer returns a part of the order
If the buyer returns a part of the order and you meet the
conditions
, we will grant you a transaction rebate for the returned products and the appropriate share of delivery costs.

You sell a product worth 100 PLN from a category covered by the 6% sales commission together with a product worth 50 PLN from a category covered with the 8% sales commission. For those two products, your customer chooses delivery for 18 PLN. The delivery cost is distributed between the purchased items at 9 PLN each. The 6% sales commission of 109 PLN is 6.54 PLN. The 8% sales commission of 59 PLN is 4.72 PLN. However, the buyer returns the first product — that is why we grant you a 6.54 PLN transaction rebate.
