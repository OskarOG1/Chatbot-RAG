The Assistant is a tool that provides you with hints regarding sales. It will remind you about important tasks that require your attention. It will also suggest how to grow and develop your sales.
The Assistant analyzes your account data and your sales results on a daily basis. This makes its suggestions unique and customized for your account.
Where to find the Assistant
Go to the
Sales Center
.
Click the bulb icon in the top right corner.
On the right side of the screen, we will display reminders and hints from the Assistant.
What hints the Assistant can give you
Urgent
— this is information on what you need to do as soon as possible to avoid account suspension or maintain your sales quality level.
Growth
— this is information on what you can do to grow and develop your sales.
How to manage hints from the Assistant
For each hint, you can:
mark it as done
reject it if you do not find it helpful
save it for later — then, we will keep it for 30 days.
You can also go directly to the tab mentioned in the hint.
