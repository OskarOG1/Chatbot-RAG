If you hold a regular account, you will not be able to use the
Sales break
option.

How the Sales break option works
Thanks to the
Sales break
option,
you can terminate all your active offers with one click
— it applies to both the offers on allegro.pl and those shared in other marketplaces.
When you suspend offers using the Sales break option,
buyers will not place new orders until you end the sales break
. Thanks to that, you can be sure that when you are absent, no new orders will appear or negatively impact your sales quality if you do not handle them.
The sales break on your account will end:
automatically — after 30 days
once you end it manually in the tab — within 30 days.
After that time,
we will relist all your offers suspended within the sales break
.
See how it works
.

If you are using the One Fulfillment service, you can suspend your offers on your own. You can do it by editing offers in bulk in the
My Assortment
tab.

How to set the Sales break
Open the
Sales break
tab.
Go to the
Start of the break
and select whether you want to suspend sales now or on a selected date. Set the selected starting date in the calendar.
Click [request a break].
Confirm that you want to change the status of your offers.
Done! In a few (up to several dozen) minutes we will suspend all your offers. If you specify the starting date of suspension, we will start suspending offers at 0:00 a.m. on the day you select.
If there are some that cannot be terminated, you will see a notification with their list and a link to edit them.
Rescheduling the Sales break
If you want to call off or reschedule the Sales break, click [abort suspension]. You will be able to schedule it again.

The Sales break option only applies to managing offers in the Sales Center.
We do not enable this option through the API.

What is next
All your active offers will be terminated. We will not display them for buyers until you restart sales.
During the Sales break, we will display the message on your seller card and your offer list:
Active auctions will be terminated with the result obtained at the moment that you suspend sales —
according to the rules of auction
.
Sales break does not apply to
offers with a scheduled listing date
. Such offers will be activated on the date you set — even during your sales break.

fulfill orders placed before the break
reply to messages and open Discussions.
If you fail to do that, your sales quality score may drop.

After you set the Sales break option, you can still edit offers and add new ones.
the offers you add after setting or scheduling the Sales break will not be suspended automatically
. To suspend newly added offers you can:
terminate them manually — if you do that, you will have to activate them manually as well (disabling the Sales break will not relist such offers)
disable the ongoing Sales break and set it again for all offers.
How to resume sales and disable the Sales break
You can relist terminated offers within 30 days. This way, you will keep their original URLs and
proportional popularity
. We will automatically relist your offers after 30 days.
To end the sales break and relist all offers, open the
Sales break
tab and click [activate sales]. All the offers and offer bundles active before the sales break will be activated again. As with the sales suspension, we will let you know if we cannot relist some of your offers.
You cannot relist a
classified ad
.
Remember to
link your offers to the Allegro Product Catalog
before resuming sales. Also, check
if your offers do not lack any obligatory parameters
. If you do not link your offers to the Catalog or if you do not complete the obligatory parameters, your offers will not relist.

Suspending offers with the Sales break option affects the popularity of your offers just like suspending them manually in the My Assortment tab. Popularity is the number of purchases made from a given offer within the last 30 days. If you relist an offer before 30 days have passed since it was suspended — you will maintain its popularity proportionally. For example, if you relist an offer after 20 days, we will calculate its popularity from the last 10 days of its duration.

We calculate the estimated delivery time based on data from the last 30-90 days. That means, the break will not affect the estimated delivery time provided in offers.

We calculate your sales quality score based on data from the last 30 days, for at least 1 active offer. That is why, 30 days after all offers are terminated, you will see no score in the
My Sales Quality
dashboard. We will recalculate it once you activate your sales again.
If your break is shorter than 30 days, we will calculate the score in the My Sales Quality proportionally based on your last actions before suspending sales. Remember that in the dashboard we display the score calculated for the previous day.

We want the sellers to enjoy their free time when on holiday! Sellers who used the Vacation option still had to fulfill orders buyers placed — despite the information about the absence. In a similar way, not answering a query about a listed product could lower the rating in the My Sales Quality dashboard or lead to losing the
Super Seller status
.
The new option solves this problem. After setting Sales break, you will not receive new orders nor queries about suspended offers.  Therefore, you do not have to fulfill orders at once to maintain your sales quality. Sales break also solves another problem that had been reported frequently — the necessity to resume single offers in the My Assortment tab one by one. Now, you can do it with one click.
We will keep developing the Sales break option and adding new features — to make scheduling and using it even more convenient.
