In the listing form, you can indicate the product with which you will combine your offer. This way, you can list it faster, because we will complete the product parameters for you.
If you do not find a product that is compatible with the one you want to sell, you can immediately add it to
the Allegro Product Catalog
.
What to pay attention to when you want to add a new product
When you add a product to the Catalog, you co-create it and influence the quality of the data we display to buyers. Therefore, add the product data correctly in the listing form.
In particular, pay attention to:
category
— select the category that is the most compatible with your product. This makes it easier for buyers to find exactly what they are looking for. If possible, do not list your Offers in the
Other
category.

A properly listed product is exactly described with parameters available in the chosen category. The product name:
is the same as category name, or
fits the assortment sold in this category.
Pay attention to the main use of the product you are selling. Determine whether it is a part of a set, an accessory, or a standalone device. Finally, check at what price your product is available in other offers linked to it.

parameters
— complete them in the
Products in offer
section. Thanks to the parameters, customers can make a well-considered purchase decision. In addition, in most categories, we automatically create
a name for the product
based on them.
Learn more about how to correctly complete the product parameters.
photos
— they should best present the product you offer. Learn more about
the product image policy
.
description
— may only refer to the features, properties, and functions of the product. Here you can specify, for example, its color, material, or weight.

In accordance with
Article 5.5 of the Allegro Terms & Conditions
, in the Allegro Product Catalog we may use all the information you provide about the product — such as logos, descriptions, or photos.

Except that:
add a separate product to the Catalog for each variant you sell.

If you sell the same dress model in several colors, add each color to the Catalog as a separate product.

do not add a non-original product to the Catalog — we will reject it.
You will not be able to add items to the Catalog if you list them in the Services category — for example, online courses or custom-made furniture.
How to add a new product to the Catalog
Go to
the listing form
, enter the product name, and click [Search].
Click [continue without selecting a product] under the product suggestions.
Select the category in which you want to list the offer.
Add the remaining information in the form and click [List Item].
If you fill in the parameters correctly and add photos,
we will automatically add a new product to the Catalog
.
We constantly check the quality of data in the Catalog and the products it contains. If the product does not meet
the requirements specified in the Terms & Conditions
, we will remove it from the Catalog and remove the Offers from it. We will let you know by email. In the email we will indicate what you need to correct in order to add the correct product to the Catalog.
