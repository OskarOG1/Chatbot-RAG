To save time, you can make changes to
up to 1000 offers at once
. You can find the management in bulk function in the
My Assortment
tab.
In order to make changes to multiple offers at once
Go to the
My Assortment
tab.
Select the offers you want to modify.
Click a specific action on the green bar and select what you want to change:
price
quantity
duration
dispatch time
shipping price list
wholesale price list
returns terms
complaints terms
warranty information
invoice options and vat rate
offer tags
size chart
additional services
update with product data.
Make changes and click [save]. Done!

You can download the file with offer details regardless of whether you
list offers from a file
, through the
form
, or through the API. This way, you can manage them more efficiently and edit
up to 10,000 offers
at the same time. Find out
how it works
.
