Sign in to your account.
Click your username in the upper right corner and then [account].

In the Sales Center

Sales Center

you use Allegro and list offers
we will send you emails and documents.
Click [change]. Done!

For example, if your account language is set to Polish, it will be the base language for your offer and you need to provide the offer description in Polish. If there is an offer completed in English and you want to add a description in Polish — you need to create a new offer or translate it into Polish on your own. Once you open the listing form for a given offer, you cannot change the language in the form.
