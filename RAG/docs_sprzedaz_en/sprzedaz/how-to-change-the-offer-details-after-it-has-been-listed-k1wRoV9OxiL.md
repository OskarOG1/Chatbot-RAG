In order to change the offer details, go to the
My Assortment
tab, select [akcje] (actions) and click [edytuj] (edit).
The changes you make will not lower the listing fee.
If there are already bids in your auction, you cannot change the descriptions of ongoing offers. If there is a
sales block
on your account, you cannot change the details of ongoing offers.
Check what changes you can make after your offers have been listed:

You can change the offer parameters only if it does not change the product properties, as specified in sub-paragraph 5.5 of the Terms & Conditions.

Category change
Details
At any time — regardless of how much time has passed since the offer's listing or whether a purchase has been made in it. Offers you want to move to the categories such as Other or Parts and Accessories are an exception. In their case, the category change is not possible. For offers linked to the
Allegro Product Catalog
: if the new category matches the product category — the link remains the same. If the new category does not match the listed product (it is not possible within the Allegro API) — you lose the link.
Changing the offer duration and the listing date
Details
Not possible.
Changing the automatic offer relisting option
Details
You can enable or disable this option anytime and decide whether we should relist the offer with a full set of items after it has ended.
Terminating the offer
Details
You can terminate it early if there are no purchase offers. You can end an
until items are exhausted
offer, but you are obliged to complete all orders that are placed before the offer ends.
Adding and editing a delivery price list
Details
You can edit the price list at any time. Each time you edit your price list in the settings, we will update it automatically in the relevant offers.

At any time.

At any time.

Changes
Details
Price change
Details
You can change the starting, minimum or kup teraz (buy now) price if no one has made a purchase offer. You can add the minimum or kup teraz (buy now) price if no one has made a purchase offer yet. You can add the kup teraz (buy now) option up to 12 hours after listing an offer. You cannot delete the minimum price if someone has made a purchase offer. The change of the starting or minimum price may influence the listing fee (according to the price list). If you change the starting or minimum price to 50 PLN or more, we will charge you the difference in the listing fee.
Title change
Details
You can change the offer title if no one has made a purchase in it or placed a bid yet.
Description change
Details
You can edit the description as long as there are no bidders.
Parameter change
Details
You can change the offer parameters only if it does not change the product properties, as specified in sub-paragraph 5.5 of the Terms & Conditions. You can do so as long as there are no bidders.
Changing / adding image
Details
You can add or edit the image if no one has made a purchase offer or purchased the item.
Category change
Details
You can change the category only if no one has made a purchase offer or purchased the item and up to 12 hours after listing an offer.
Changing the offer duration and the listing date
Details
Not possible.
Changing the automatic offer relisting option
Details
You can enable or disable this option anytime and decide whether we should relist the offer after it ends without someone winning the auction.
Terminating the offer
Details
You can terminate an auction early even if someone has made a purchase offer – in this case, the winner is the highest bidder at the moment of the offer termination.
Adding and editing a delivery price list
Details
You can edit the delivery price list if no one has made a purchase offer.
Changing the number of available units in the offer
Details
Not possible.
Adding the option to issue a VAT invoice
Details
You can add this option even to the offers in which someone has made a purchase offer.
Offer promotion options
Details
You can enable and disable them if no one has made a purchase offer.
Payment and delivery
Details
You can change it if no one has made a purchase offer.
