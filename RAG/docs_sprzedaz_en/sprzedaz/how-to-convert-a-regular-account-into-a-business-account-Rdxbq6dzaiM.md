Use the business account if you are an entrepreneur and want to sell on Allegro.

You already hold a regular account but consider launching professional sales? Open another account — a business one! That way, you can conveniently do private shopping on your regular account, and engage in business activities on the business account. This is the solution many sellers on Allegro opt for.

(available in Polish).

How to convert a regular account into a business account
Open the
Account details
tab.
In the
Account details
section, click
Change to Business Account
.
Check whether the details, such as contact number or first and last names are valid.
Create your own login
if your account name comes in the Client:123456789 format.

If
in the last 24 months
,
there were no sales
on your regular account, you will convert your regular account into the business one specifically for purchases on
Allegro Business
. You only need to provide your NIP (Tax Identification Number) and complete the business activity details.
Check how it works
. At any time, you can also activate sales on the business account you use for purchases.

Complete the NIP (Tax Identification Number) and click [company details]. We will then download your company details from the Central Statistical Office.

You can have the same NIP (Tax Identification Number) on several business accounts.

Check whether your company details are valid. If not, you can change them at once. If the details are correct, click [convert your account to a business account].

In the form, you can indicate a
sole proprietorship
or a
limited liability company if you are its only associate
. In other cases, register with a new business account.

Confirm your bank account with a quick online payment or standard transfer.
Send us the documents of your company — as photos or scans Prepare the files in the JPG, PNG, TIF, or PDF formats — with sizes between 1 MB and 10 MB.
Make sure that:
all corners, edges, and pages of the document are visible (if it has more than one page)
the photos are well-lit, without shadows
the photos and scans are clear
the photos and scans are in color
no data is anonymized (for example, covered up), and the file has not been graphically edited.
Click [send all documents]. That is all! We will inform you by email once we convert your account into a business one.
