Within a flexible bundle, you can offer customers all the
product variants
you sell. It means buyers will see a single bundle with variant selection, rather than over a dozen very similar bundles. When they buy a bundle:
they have a full selection of variants to choose from — instead of searching for separate offers, they can easily select a variant that suits them best from your assortment
they can buy only those offers from a bundle they are interested in.

You determine which product groups go together in a bundle. Customers can select only the products you offer in your assortment — they cannot select a bundle made of offers from other sellers.

With flexible bundles, you can:
offer your customers a better and wider selection
increase the visibility of your offers ― also the less popular ones
make your bundles stand out with discounts and attract buyers with competitive prices
decide which specific products within a bundle will be discounted.

A
flexible bundle
consists of various offers. A customer can buy the entire bundle
or
choose a product and different offers from the same category.
As part of
multipacks (product sets)
, you sell
multiple units of a product
within a single offer. A product set can consist of:
different products or
multiple units of the same product – meaning a
multipack
. 
A customer can only buy the entire product set. Find out more about product sets (including multipacks) in
this article
.

How to create a flexible bundle
Go to the
Offer bundles
tab.
Click [add new].
Click [add offers] and select the offers the bundle will consist of. One bundle can consist of a
maximum of 6 positions
— a group of offers from the same category.

You cannot create a bundle that consists of offers listed in
categories with age restrictions
.

Click [save]. If you want, you can add a discount to the bundle or selected offers.
Decide on which offer's page you want to display the bundle — check or uncheck the
show bundle in this offer
option. For example, you want to display the following bundle only on the iPhone's page — iPhone + phone case + charger — because the customer might be willing to buy cheaper accessories along with it.
Check the summary and, if everything is correct, click [save bundle]. Finished!

We group offers into consecutive positions based on their category. If you add more than one offer to the selected position, the customer will be able to personalize the bundle and select another offer when making a purchase.
You can change the order using the arrows and move offers between the positions, to present your bundle in the best possible way.

How we display bundles in other marketplaces
We will display your bundle to the buyers from allegro.cz, allegro.sk, or allegro.hu, if you list
at least 2 offers
from that bundle in foreign marketplaces.
If you list
only one offer
from a bundle in a foreign marketplace, your bundle will not be visible to the buyers from allegro.cz, allegro.sk, or allegro.hu.

On allegro.pl, you have a flexible bundle: iPhone + gray phone case + green phone case. You list an offer with an iPhone and a gray phone case on allegro.cz. It means that buyers on allegro.pl can buy a phone case in a selected color, and buyers on allegro.cz — only the gray one.

If an offer from your bundle ends, we will stop displaying it as available to buy in a bundle. We will show it again only if you resume it. If only one offer is active — we will stop displaying such a bundle to customers. We will show it again if you resume the terminated offers.
You can check the offer's status in the
My Assortment
tab. To check which offer is available to purchase in a bundle in a selected marketplace, go to the
Flexible bundles
section and the bundle editing page. We mark the availability of an offer for purchase in a bundle with a flag — for example, we will display Polish and Czech flags for a bundle available on allegro.pl and allegro.cz.
If an offer is not available for a purchase in a given marketplace — the flag will be grayed out, and we will display a message next to it.

How to set a discount for the whole bundle
Indicate the discount percentage for the whole bundle. You can do it separately for each marketplace or copy it for other marketplaces.
Select the number of various products in a bundle that the customer must purchase for the discount to apply.
Click [save]. We will deduct the indicated percentage from each offer's price. The discount for the whole bundle will amount to the sum of what was deducted.

You add a 10% discount to a flexible bundle: iPhone (2,200 PLN) + phone case (100 PLN) + charger (80 PLN). You select the option with information that the discount will be available if the customer buys at least two different products from the bundle. The customer selects iPhone + phone case. The price of the entire bundle with the applied discount is: 1,980 PLN + 90 PLN = 2,070 PLN.

How to set a discount for selected positions
Add the percentage discount for the selected positions. You can do it separately for each marketplace or copy it for other marketplaces.
Click [save]. We will apply the discount if a customer buys an offer with a discount within the bundle.

You have a flexible bundle: iPhone + phone case + charger. You add a discount to the offer with the charger. The customer buys a bundle of an iPhone + phone case, but without the charger. We will not apply the discount in such a case.

How to edit the bundle discount in bulk
Go to the
Flexible bundles
section in the
Offer bundles
tab.
Select the bundles you want to edit the discount for.
Click [edit discounts].
After you introduce changes, click [save].

To remove the discount in bundles in bulk, enter 0 in the discount value field.

If you want to remove the discount from a bundle, go to the bundle's edit page and click the trash can icon.
Automatic combination of offers into flexible bundles
We can automatically combine selected offers into a bundle. We verify whether the offers match, among others, based on the selected parameters of the products they are linked to. We also consider sales in a given offer and the product popularity. Bundles will be visible to buyers at once.

You have 4 offers: shampoo for curly hair + conditioner for curly hair from X brand + conditioner for curly hair from Y brand + mask for curly hair. You complete the
Hair type
parameter with
curly
in all offers. We automatically create a flexible bundle out of them and we group both conditioners under the appropriate position.

Flexible bundles we created automatically do not receive a discount. You decide
yourself
whether to add it.
How to manage flexible bundles
Go to the
Flexible bundles
section in the
Offer bundles
tab. You can manage your own bundles as well as those we created automatically. In order to search for the latter, select the
automatic
filter.
In the
Flexible bundles
section:
you can create and edit your own bundles
– you decide yourself which product groups make a perfectly tailored, complete order for your customers
you can assign and change percentage discounts
– add a new discount or edit the existing one
you can delete the selected bundle
– if you decide that any bundle (including those created by us automatically) does not align with your sales strategy, you can remove it anytime. In such a case, we will never automatically recreate such a bundle. If you change your mind later, you can group the same offers into a bundle yourself at any time.
You can also find your sales statistics here. You can check how many flexible bundles you have sold in the last 30 days.
How to remove selected bundles in bulk
Go to the
Flexible bundles
section in the
Offer bundles
tab.
Check selected bundles.
In the menu at the bottom of the screen, click [remove bundles]. Finished!
Flexible bundles and other promotions and programs
How bundles with a discount work when an offer participates in a campaign
If an offer that belongs to a flexible bundle participates in a campaign that lowers its price, then such an offer can still be purchased in a bundle — but the bundle discount does not apply. Its price will be updated to match the campaign prices.
How bundles with a purchase limit work
If an offer participates in a campaign with a buyer limit or a unit number limit, and the limit is
lower or equal
to the number of offer units set in the bundle — then the offer will not be visible in the bundle when the limit applies. The bundle will be visible to buyers if it consists of other offers.

A bundle consists of the following offers: lamp + LED bulbs (4 units) + regular bulbs (4 units).
An offer with the LED bulbs participates in a campaign where the
limit is 2 units
. We display the following bundle: lamp + regular bulbs (4 units).
The offer with LED bulbs participates in a campaign where the
limit is 10 units
. We display the bundle unchanged: lamp + LED bulbs (4 units) or regular bulbs (4 units) to select from.

How bundles work with other campaigns and promotions
If an offer participates in a campaign for a specific group — for example, Allegro Smart! customers — the offer will be visible in a bundle only to the buyer who meets all the campaign conditions.

One of the offers in a bundle participates in a campaign only for buyers with Allegro Smart! Buyers will see this offer in a bundle if they have the Allegro Smart! subscription.
