Some products may be sold not only separately, but also as offer bundles. With bundles, you can:
sell multiple products in one transaction
increase the visibility of your offers ― especially the less popular ones
make your bundles stand out with discounts and attract buyers with competitive prices.
You can create bundles in the
Bundles
tab and the
Allegro API
. In the
Bundles
tab, you can also find your sales statistics. There, you will check how many offer bundles and offers with discounts you have sold in the last 30 days.

You will not create a bundle with offers you list in the categories with age restrictions.

If all offers in a bundle meet
the sharing conditions
, we will automatically share the bundle in foreign marketplaces.

What offer bundles are
If you list an offer bundle, customers can buy either one of the offered products of the whole bundle:
You can make offers within a bundle more attractive by granting additional
discounts
. This way you increase the chances that buyers will buy more than one product from you in one transaction.
How to prepare attractive offer bundles
Select products that match your offers best. Example: if you sell a laptop, add a mouse and a cover to the bundle.
Pay attention to the prices of the products in the bundle. Do not offer a premium laptop cover with a budget laptop.
Consider discounts for customers. Buyers are more eager to buy bundles with discounted prices.
Add a few different bundles to let your customers select the one that suits them best. Example: when you sell a mobile, add a protective film and a power bank.
Do not create bundles with too many offers. If the price of the bundle is too high, it may discourage buyers.
How to create an offer bundle

From June 10, 2026, you will not be able to create new offer bundles. Use the flexible bundles.
Learn more
.

Go to the
Bundles
tab.
Click [add new].
Click [add offer] and decide which offers the bundle will be displayed in.
Select only the offers you list with the buy now option
. The bundle will consist of all the offers you select.
You can add a discount for the purchase of the whole bundle.
You can set any discount for every marketplace you share your offers in. You can also grant a discount only in selected marketplaces. Click [convert to other markets] to automatically calculate the discount amount. The discount amount will be converted to other currencies at the current exchange rate
After completing all information, click [save bundle]. Done!

A bundle can consist of up to 6 products.
One offer can be a part of up to 7 bundles.

If you share offers in foreign marketplaces, your bundles will also be available there.

We automatically combine selected offers into bundles
We may automatically combine select offers into a bundle. We verify whether the offers match, among others, based on the selected parameters of the products they are linked to. We also consider sales in a given offer and the product popularity.

You sell shampoos in one offer and hair conditioners in a different one. You complete the
Hair Type
parameter with
curly
in both offers. We automatically create a bundle with those offers. A buyer can buy the whole bundle
or
buy only in one offer.

Bundles will be visible to buyers at once.

The bundles we create automatically do not have discounts added. If you want to make your bundle stand out, you can add a discount on your own.

How to check which offers are included in a bundle
You will find the bundles in the
Bundles
tab. You can check there how many bundles you have sold, change the discount, or delete an entire bundle ―
also an automatically created one
.
How to manage offer bundles
In the
Bundles
tab, you can:
see the number of sold bundles
edit the bundle discount
create a similar offer bundle based on the bundle you already have
remove selected bundles ― also those created automatically.
We will not create the same bundle automatically again.
If you want, you can combine those offers into bundles again on your own.
To edit discounts assigned to a bundle, click [change discount], and edit the price or remove the discount for that bundle.
How to create a similar offer bundle
Go to the
Bundles
tab
Select the offer bundle you want to copy and click [create similar].
You can freely edit the new bundle — for example, add or change the offer, or set a discount.
Click [create bundle] — the new bundle is ready!
How to edit the bundle discount in bulk
Go to the
Bundles
tab.
Select offer bundles in which you want to edit the discount.
Click [change the discounts] in the menu at the bottom of the screen.
We will display a window with the list of bundles. Provide the percentage-based or fixed-amount discount for all the bundles and click [apply to the bundles below].
On the list of bundles, in the Applied discount field, you will see both the amount and the percentage of the discount.
If you want to change the discount amount, you can do it separately for each bundle in the Applied discount field.
Click [change discount] to confirm the changes.

Bulk editing of discounts is possible only in the Polish marketplace.

How to remove selected bundles in bulk
Go to the
Bundles
tab.
Select bundles.
Click [remove bundles] in the context menu at the bottom of the screen.
Offer bundles and terminated offers
If only one active offer from a bundle is terminated, the entire offer bundle becomes inactive. That means buyers cannot purchase products in that bundle. When you relist those offers, we will activate the bundle automatically.
How we display a discount for offer bundles
In the Buy together section, we display information about the price of products within and outside the bundle.
How we calculate a discount on products in a bundle
The discount amount on a bundle you provide is divided proportionately among all products included in the bundle.

You sell a bundle that consists of 2 products ― the first one is worth 900 PLN and the second one ― 100 PLN. Together they cost 1,000 PLN. You grant a 100 PLN discount ― 10% of the starting bundle price. We will lower the price of both products by 10% ― as a result, the first one will cost 810 PLN and the second one ― 90 PLN after the discount.

How we calculate the sales commission in a discounted bundle
We charge you the sales commission for every product included in the bundle. If you sell a bundle with a discount, we charge you the sales commission accounting for the discounted product price.

If a product you sell normally costs 100 PLN and after a discount ― 90 PLN, the sales commission for that item is calculated from 90 PLN.

Offer bundles and other promotions and programs
How bundles with a discount work if an offer is in a campaign
If a bundle
with a discount
contains an offer that you submit to a promotional campaign, such as Allegro Prices or Black Friday, we will
not display
such a bundle. In the Sales Center, you will see the
suspended
status when the campaign price applies.
If you remove the discount, we will start displaying the bundle with offers that will have campaign prices.
How bundles without a discount work if an offer is in a campaign
If a bundle contains an offer that you submit to a promotional campaign, such as Allegro Prices or Black Friday, we will
display
such a bundle and set the offer prices to the campaign prices.
If you add a discount to the bundle during a campaign,
we will stop displaying it
. In the Sales Center, it will be marked with the
suspended
status when the campaign prices apply.
How bundles with a purchase limit work
If a bundle contains an offer that has a limit on purchases per buyer or unit number in a campaign, and:
the limit is
higher than or equal to
the set unit number of the offer in a bundle — the
bundle will be visible
the limit is
lower than
the set unit number of the offer in a bundle — the
bundle will not be visible
as long as the limit applies. The bundle will be visible as active in the Sales Center.

You are selling a bundle of an offer for a lamp and another offer for 4 bulbs.
If the bulb has a purchase limit of 2 units at the campaign price
, we will
not display
such a bundle, because the bulb purchase limit (2 units) is lower than the bulb unit number in the bundle (4 units).
If the bulb has a purchase limit of 6 units at the campaign price
, we will
display
such a bundle, because the bulb purchase limit (6 units) is higher than the bulb unit number in the bundle (4 units). We will display this bundle until the stock of the bulbs is sufficient.

How bundles work with other campaigns
If a bundle
does not have a discount
, and one of its offers participates in a campaign for a specific group, for example, Allegro Smart! customers or buyers in the app, the
bundle will be visible
when a buyer meets all the conditions set for those offers.

One of the offers in a bundle participates in a campaign only for buyers with Allegro Smart!, and the other one — for buyers in the app. A buyer will see this bundle if they have Allegro Smart! and use the mobile app.
