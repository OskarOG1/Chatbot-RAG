If you hold a
business account
, you can list additional offers only for entrepreneurs who make business purchases. This allows you to offer a range of products that you can only sell to companies — with an invoice.
How it works
If you want an offer to be visible only on Allegro Business — when listing it, make sure to:
select: VAT invoice, margin VAT invoice, or non-VAT invoice in
Invoice options
select: business customers only in the
Who can purchase the offer
section
for VAT invoices — complete the VAT rate.
We will then display such an offer only on Allegro Business (
business.allegro.pl
or
business.allegro.cz
).
Business offers are directed only at customers who make business purchases and request invoices. They are marked with a special badge:
b2b offer
.
You will see it:
on the product list
on the offer page.
You decide which offers will be available to all buyers — regardless of account type — and which will allow only for business purchases with an invoice.
How to make your offers available only to business customers
Go to the
listing form
.
Add invoice information and a VAT rate. Check
how to do that
.
In the
Who can purchase the offer
section select
business customers only
.
List the offer with the buy now sales format. Done!
