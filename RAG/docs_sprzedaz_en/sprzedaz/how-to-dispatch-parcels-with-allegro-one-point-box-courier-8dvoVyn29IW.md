Allegro One is the name of our service.
It contains the following delivery options:
Allegro One (Point, Box) — delivery to pick-up points and parcel lockers across Poland. Parcels are delivered by DPD.
Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) — delivery to pick-up points, parcel lockers, and to the buyer's address in selected cities. Parcels are delivered by Allegro One Courier.
Delivery options:
Allegro One Point — delivery to the Kolporter convenience store, epaka.pl, Furgonetka, Auchan, or Pakersi pick-up points
Allegro One Box — delivery to parcel lockers
Allegro One Courier — courier deliveries within the Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option
Allegro One Courier — payment on delivery — a courier POD delivery carried out within the Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option.

How to enable the Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option
You can enable this delivery option only in a
package with other options from the Allegro Delivery program
.
How to resign from using Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option
To resign from using Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option, disable the whole package of Allegro Delivery options in your price lists in the
Delivery settings
tab.

After you do that, your Smart! offers will lose the Smart! badge.
You have to enable the delivery options from the program if you want your offers to keep the Smart! badge
.

How to settle delivery costs
You will settle delivery with Allegro One within the
Allegro Standard
method. It means you will pay for parcels and additional services within the monthly invoice you get from Allegro.
Each time, we add delivery cost to

on your account:
we add the fee for parcel dispatch the month the service was performed
we add the fee for additional services (e.g., additional insurance, oversized parcel) the month following the service's performance.

How to order a fixed courier pick-up
To order fixed pick-ups for the Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) delivery option, fill in the
form
.
Provide
all the necessary information
in the form:
your Allegro account login
company name and NIP (Polish Tax Identification Number)
parcel pick-up address:
street, building and apartment number
postal code
city
the phone number of the person who handles dispatches
the time by which the courier can pick up parcels
days of the week (from Monday to Saturday) when the courier will come to collect parcels
preferred pick-up time (hourly time frame)
estimated number of parcels to be picked up by the courier
additional information for the courier (optional) — for example, information on how to get to the warehouse.

You can order scheduled Allegro One Courier arrivals if you plan to hand over a
minimum of 4 parcels at each pick-up — at least 3 times a week
.

If you want to order a fixed courier pick-up for Saturday, you can only do it for the
next-day delivery
option. In that case, the courier will pick up your parcels on Saturday and deliver them to buyers on the next working day — Monday.
You cannot order a fixed courier pick-up for Saturday for the
same-day delivery
option.
The courier's arrival time depends on the postal code.
We will let you know by email once we launch the scheduled pick-up order.
We will launch it only after you have received the email confirmation from the carrier
. From then on, select the [without pick-up by courier] option for parcels you dispatch with
Ship with Allegro
.
Before the scheduled arrival is enabled, you can:
dispatch parcels free of charge in Allegro One Box parcel lockers or at brick-and-mortar Allegro One Point points, such as Kolpolter branches, epaka.pl, Furgonetka, Auchan, and Pakersi points
or
order a
