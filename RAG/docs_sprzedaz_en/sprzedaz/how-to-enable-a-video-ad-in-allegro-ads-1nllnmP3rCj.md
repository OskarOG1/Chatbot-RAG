This ad format consists of a video and promoted offers you select. Thanks to that, you can advertise your product with a short video that can help you increase interest in your offers.
How to enable the ad step by step
Sign in to your account in the
Allegro Ads
dashboard.
Click [add campaign].
Select
Video ad
and click [configure campaign].
Name the campaign and ad group, and click [next step].
Specify the display locations and click [next step].
What display locations you can select
Search results — middle and bottom
— your ad will appear between ad belowe the search results the buyers are browsing at the moment. Thanks to that, customers interested in a given category will see your offer.
Select that location if you want to reach the customers interested in a specific product category.
Search results — left column
— your ad will be visible on the left-hand side of the page, under the menu on the search results page, regardless of what products the buyers are browsing. We adjust offers in the ad to buyers' previous searches and how they use the platform.
Select that location if you want to reach a larger group of customers with varied interests.
Click [create a new ad].
Name your ad and add a video.

Add a static poster. It is a graphic we will display to the buyers who cannot play the video. We will display the first frame of the film if you do not add the graphic.

Add the offers we will display in the ad along with the video. You can add between 1 and 15 offers to each ad. We will display the offers that match the buyer's query best. Along with the video, the buyer will see one of the selected offers.

You can find offers to your ad:
by the title
on a list of all your offers
with the filters in the advanced options ― filtering by category, price, or offer ID.

In the next step, set the ad reach —
precise or extended
.

If you want to improve the efficiency of the video ad and reach a narrow group of buyers most interested in your offer — select the precise reach.

Enter the
keywords and negative keywords
. Thanks to them, we will display your ad in response to the buyers' selected queries and limit the impressions that do not match your offer.
Click [ad preview] to see what the final version of your ad looks like. If you want to change something, click [edit ad].
Determine your daily budget, the
CPM
rate, and display dates. You can also set your total budget.
Click [create campaign]. Done! We will verify your ad. If it complies with our rules, we will soon start displaying it. You will see the
on
status next to the ad.

We will display your video ad on the offer search list.

We may also reject the ad — in that case, we will ask you to edit it and introduce the changes we have described. Correct the ad and send it for verification again.
In the Allegro Ads dashboard, you will find hints on how to prepare the video ad well — the technical requirements, recommended file format, and content guidelines. Click [expand complete specifications].
What you need to keep in mind
The video should be understandable without the sound.
The content of the video needs to be closely linked to the product or offer and direct to a relevant page.
The video needs to comply with the rules and standards of advertising on Allegro. We reserve the right not to accept the prepared content.
