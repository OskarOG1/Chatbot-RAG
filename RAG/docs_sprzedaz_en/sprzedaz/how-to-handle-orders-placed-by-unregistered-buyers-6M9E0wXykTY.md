Unregistered buyers cannot use certain features — for example, Allegro Smart!
Check the key differences from a buyer's perspective
.
If a buyer places an order without registering,
you cannot ask them to
pay a surcharge for their purchase
. Apart from that, you can handle an order placed without registration just like any other.

All orders on allegro.pl, also those with delivery to Czechia and Slovakia, come from registered buyers.

How it works
If a buyer decides to place an order without creating an account, they can select the [pokračovat jako host] (buy as a guest) option. Next, they continue like buyers with registered accounts — enter shipping details and select a payment method and a delivery option. Regardless of whether your customer holds an Allegro account —
we will provide you with all the information necessary to fulfill the order
.
We contact unregistered buyers via email. If you want to ask the buyer a question, contact them via your
Message Center
. We will send your message to the email address the buyer provided while making the purchase. You will see their reply in your Message Center as well.

Among others, unregistered buyers cannot:
benefit from
Allegro Smart!
make purchases through the app
use
coupons
pay a surcharge for their order
rate you as a seller — they can
only rate the product they bought
benefit from the additional post-purchase protection —
Allegro Buyer Protection
. However, after registering an account, the buyer can apply for compensation regarding new orders as well as the old ones — placed before the registration.
check their purchase history
use tools such as the Message Center or Favorites list.
