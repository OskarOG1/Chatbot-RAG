Listing a charity offers is easy:
Use the
listing form dedicated to charity
offers.
Select the fundraising you want to support:
the sale format ― you can list it an offer with the kup teraz (buy now) option or an auction
offer duration ― your offers can last until the fundraiser deadline set by the organizer
delivery options ― only payable in advance or through PayU are available.
The charity offers you list are visible on
allegro.pl
. Learn
how buyers can find charity offer
s.
You can also
list a charity offer through the API
.
Helping does not cost a thing ―
we do not charge you the sales commission or listing fees in charity offers
.
When somebody buys an item in your charity offer, the payment for the item will be transferred directly to the bank account of the charity whose fundraiser you support. You will receive the amount equal to delivery cost.
If you want to promote your charity offers, we will charge you according to the Allegro price list.
How to prepare an offer — good practices
When you list a product
For
personal pickup or delivery
, provide the deadline by which the buyer can collect the item. Explain what you will do with the item after the deadline — for example, whether you will relist it.
If you
list an item with an autograph
, you can attach a proof of its authenticity. Add a certificate of authenticity or a photo of the item with the person who has signed it. It will help you gain more trust from buyers.
When you list an event or a meeting
If an offer involves a
meeting with a public figure
:
in the meeting description, emphasize that you will decide on the date together with the winner
indicate the dates when the person is not available.
Describe the
details of the event
, such as place and time. In offers with a specified date, emphasize the deadline by which the event must take place. Explain what will happen after that date — for example, whether the meeting will be canceled.
If participation in the event involves
additional costs
— for example, transportation or accommodation — indicate who will cover them.
Mention
potential limitations
— for example, age restrictions or accessibility barriers for people with disabilities.
List the events in
Special Moments
in the
Culture and Entertainment
category.
