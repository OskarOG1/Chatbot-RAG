Use the
listing form for charities
.
You can list an offer in
buy now format or as an auction
. Your offers will last until the fundraiser ends at the latest ― this year it ends on February 16, 2026. If you want to list an auction with a duration different from the one available in the listing form —
contact us
. At your request, we can extend the auction's duration, but not longer than until the fundraiser finishes — February 16, 2026.

Note that we cannot extend the duration of an active offer. Contact us
before
you list your offer.

Listing an auction for WOŚP
Use only those delivery options where the buyer can pay in advance through PayU and Allegro Finance.
In the listing form, decide whether:
you are listing a
product from the Catalog
— in that case, use ready images and descriptions that you can edit while listing the offer
you are listing a
unique product
— one of a kind, unique, and not available anywhere else, such as a book signed by the author.
If you want to list a unique product that is one of a kind or cannot be found anywhere else, select the
Special Moments
category in the listing form. This way, you will not have to complete the parameters in the offer. Moreover, products from this category will not be linked to any other product on Allegro — customers browsing products on the platform by category will not see them.
Complete the following:
offer title
category
parameters
price
sales format
duration
delivery terms.
Add images.
Add a description — provide accurate information about the condition, size, and other features of the product that may affect the buyer's decision.
Set payment in advance.

Use only the graphics
prepared for the 34th WOŚP Grand Finale, and not the WOŚP heart logo itself, which is protected by law.
When you list an offer for the 34th WOŚP Grand Finale, we will
automatically add the appropriate logo
to your first image (thumbnail). We will choose the best place for the logo.

After you list the auction, share a link on your social media. This will greatly boost the chance of making a sale. Tag Allegro and WOŚP. All ready!
