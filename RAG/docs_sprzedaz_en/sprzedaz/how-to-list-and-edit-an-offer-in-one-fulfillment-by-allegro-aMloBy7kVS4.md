How to prepare an offer correctly
Link your offer to a product from the
Allegro Product Catalog
or create an offer and a new product in the Catalog. In One Fulfillment by Allegro we handle
only
products in offers linked to a product from the Allegro Product Catalog.
You can only have one
active
offer for a single product. However, take into account its different variants. If you sell a single product in different variants (for example, in different colors or sizes), create a
separate offer
for each of them — even if those products have the same
EAN (GTIN code)
.
Link offers with different variants of a given product to corresponding products from the Allegro Product Catalog.
If you cannot find a specific variant of the product in the Catalog — create a new product in the Catalog and link it to your offer
. Do it also if different variants of a single product have the same EAN (GTIN) code.
If you want to create an offer for a given product, you can do it right after the previous offer for that product ends. If you do that, we will automatically assign all products from the previous offer that are stored at the warehouse to the new offer.
Check whether details of the product from the Catalog you want to link your offer to are correct. If they are incorrect —
report incorrect product information
. When you do, save the offer and wait until we verify your report. You can check its status in the
Product Change Requests
tab. If we make the changes you suggest, you will be able to link your offers to that particular product from the Catalog. If we do not make changes, create a new product in the Catalog — one that will completely match the item from your offer.
When you create a new product in the Catalog, enter its correct name — the name provided by the manufacturer, not the title of your offer. Add information that will allow to identify product variant — for example, size or color.  Make sure you enter all information correctly — without typos or unnecessary capital letters.
When you create offers for manufacturer sets (products that consist of several units), make sure to link them to the product from the Catalog with the appropriate EAN (GTIN) code. Single units of products usually have different EAN (GTIN) codes than manufacturer sets that consist of the very same products.
Check whether the EAN (GTIN) code you provide matches the EAN (GTIN) code encrypted as a barcode on the item. To do that, scan the item barcode.
Some of the products from the Allegro Product Catalog may be marked as
Pending verification
. Those are unverified products for which a seller reported an error in the
basic product parameters
. We verify it within 2 working days  — until we do, you cannot list the offer with such a product. If you want, you can create a draft version of the offer linked to an unverified product. You can only list it after we have verified the product. If we reject the request for the change in product parameters, we will delete the unverified product and unlink it from the draft version of your offer.  You can check the status of product errors you have reported in the
Product Change Requests
tab.

Click the three vertical dots next to the offer you want to edit. Then, click [edit].

cannot change
