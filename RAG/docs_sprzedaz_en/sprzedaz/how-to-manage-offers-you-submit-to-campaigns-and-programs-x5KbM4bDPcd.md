In the
Campaigns and Programs
tab, you can see promotional campaigns available for a given Allegro marketplace. For each campaign and program, you can:
see its terms and conditions
check if you meet participation requirements
choose and submit offers to it
view the submitted offers and download their list.
If it is in line with the terms & conditions of a given campaign or program, you can also:
change the price of a participating offer
withdraw selected or all offers from the promotion.
What you can find in the Campaigns and Programs tab
You can find two sections in the
Campaigns and Programs
tab:
in the
Submit
section, you can browse promotional campaigns and submit your offers to them
in the
Manage participation
section, you can see and edit your offers participating in campaigns and programs.
You will see information related to your registration marketplace. Choose another marketplace at the top of the page to see all the campaigns available on it.
In both sections, there are additional filters that help you customize the displayed content.
How to use the Submit section
In the
Submit
section, you can find the list of all campaigns and programs you can currently join. Click a program to select it.
This way you will be able to see all the details below. They include:
campaign name
badge displayed in participating offers
date when we start accepting submissions
duration
link to the terms & conditions
participation requirements
participation benefits
participation costs.
Below, you can see the list of your offers with information on whether a given offer meets the conditions of a chosen campaign or program. You can select and submit offers to this promotional campaign.

If your account does not meet the chosen campaign's or program's conditions, you will see a notification instead of the list of offers. We will also inform you which conditions you do not meet and how to change that.

How to submit your offer to a campaign or program
Go to the
Submit
section.
Look at the top of the page and make sure you have selected the marketplace where the campaign you are interested in takes place — for example, allegro.pl. If not, select the correct marketplace.
Select the promotional campaign you want to join.
Select the offers you want to submit. You can select them manually or click [select ready to submit]

Only offers you can submit to the selected campaign or program will be displayed on the list by default. To see all the offers, disable the
See offers that meet the conditions
filter.
Clicking [more filters] will enable more filtering options, including the category, product number, and price. You can also look up offers using an EAN, a title, or a number.

Complete the selected offers with details related to the campaign, such as the
promotional price
, unit number limit per buyer, or — for some campaigns — quantity in the campaign. You can do it:
separately for each offer
for all selected offers at once — to do that, click [edit price] or [edit limit] at the bottom of the screen.
Click [submit offers].
How to submit an offer in multiple marketplaces
In the case of some campaigns, such as Allegro Days or AlleDiscount, you can see the additional
Submit these offers to other markets
overlay. We will display it if at least part of the submitted offers meets the conditions of this campaign also in other marketplaces.
Thanks to this option, you can save time and submit your offers to the campaigns in Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary at the same time. See how to do that.
In the
Submit these offers to other markets
overlay, select the marketplaces where you also want to submit your offers to the given campaign. You can select allegro.cz, allegro.sk, allegro.hu, and local marketplaces for business purchases, such as business.allegro.cz.
Next to each marketplace, you can see the local campaign name and information on how many offers meet the conditions there.
We will calculate the
promotional price
for a foreign marketplace based on the promotional price from the base marketplace, using the European Central Bank conversion rates. In the
Price management
column, you can set the percentage by which we will additionally change the promotional price after the conversion. You can add a maximum of 10% of the price or deduct a maximum of 99.99% of the price.
If the original submission has limits on the unit number per buyer, the limits for other marketplaces will be the same as the ones set for the base marketplace. At this stage, you cannot change them anymore.
Click [submit to selected markets] when everything is ready.
If you want to submit offers just to one marketplace, click on [skip] on this overlay. If you change your mind, you can go through the described process once again and manually submit offers to a campaign in another marketplace.
You can submit offers to campaigns and programs also through the API.

How to use the Manage participation section
In the
Manage participation
section, you can find the list of campaigns and programs from the last 2 months. Next to the list, you can see two filters:
Only active
and
Only with offers
. You can enable and disable them to customize the list.
Both filters are enabled by default. It means that you will see the list of ongoing campaigns your offers participate in.

Both the AlleDiscount campaign and the Allegro Prices program are always visible, regardless of whether your offers participate in them.

Click a promotional campaign to select it.
When you select a campaign or program, you will see a summary of the most important information and the list of your offers that are participating or have participated in it.
Next to each offer, you can see:
the details related to your participation in that campaign, such as the promotional price you have set or the unit limit — depending on the campaign rules
the status of that offer in the campaign:
participating
pending verification
rejected
expired.
There, you can withdraw an offer from the campaign or edit its details related to that campaign.

To get the list of offers in a CSV file, click [download file with offers]. It will include all data filtered according to your current settings. If your filtered view has more than one page, the file will include them all.
This way, you can save time instead of manually browsing the view — especially if there are a few pages of offers from a given campaign in the tab. You can also easily import the data from a CSV file into spreadsheets. It will allow you to generate reports and analyze the efficiency of your offers' participation in campaigns and programs.
AlleDiscount
and
Allegro Prices
are exceptions —
you cannot download the list of offers
in their case.

How to withdraw an offer from a campaign or program
Go to the
Manage participation
section.
Select the promotional campaign you want to resign from.
Find the offer you want to withdraw.
Click the three vertical dots by the selected offer.
Click [resign].
Confirm your decision in a pop-up window — click [resign] again.
You can resign from all campaigns and programs
for free
.
How to change the offer price in a campaign or program
Go to the
Manage participation
section.
Select the promotional campaign in which you want to edit offers.
Select the offer in which you want to change the price.
Click the three vertical dots by the selected offer.
Click [change price].
Enter the new price for the relevant program or campaign.
Click [save].

Learn more
about the differences between base and campaign/program prices.

What quantity in the campaign means
You can determine and submit a specific quantity of products for a given offer to some campaigns on Allegro. It means that once customers buy all the units at the promotional price, the offer will stop participating in the campaign and become available outside it again.
You provide the quantity you want to sell in a given campaign when submitting offers. You will see the
Quantity in the campaign
field. There, you can enter the number of units you want to sell in the selected campaign.

You can declare more units than you have in your offer at the moment of submission, but the quantity must fall within the range specified for that product. You can find the information on ranges in the
Campaigns and programs
tab — below the field with your declared quantity.
For example, you declare 100 units in a campaign with a range of 80–100 units for that product. You have 90 units in your offer — and you can submit 90 units to that campaign.

A few days before the campaign's start, we will reserve the quantity you have provided
. It will be separated from your main stock. This way, you can be sure you will not run out of the specified quantity before the start, and you will begin the campaign with this exact number.
You can also edit the declared quantity when the offer has a pending verification status. In some campaigns, you will also be able to edit the quantity when the offer has an accepted status.
If in a given offer, buyers:
buy all the units before the campaign's end — we will automatically remove this offer from the campaign. It will become available for sale outside the campaign again — at the base price with the main stock.
do not buy all the units, and the campaign ends — we will return the remaining supplies reserved for the campaign to the main stock.
