Before you pack products for shipment, make sure they are properly labeled and secured.
Unit packaging
Products you ship to the Allegro Warehouse must come in the original and sealed packaging that protects from:
spilling or leaking
having free access to its content
mechanical damage
dirt.
In addition, when you ship to the Allegro Warehouse products whose packaging is the integral part (for example, jewelry in decorative packaging) — secure it as well. You can use, for example:
shrink wrap
foil bags sealed with barcode labels
seals.

If you ship to the warehouse a product in unit packaging and additional protective packaging (for example, in a plastic bag), we will not unpack it before measuring. We will measure it in the packaging and consider those dimensions valid.
This may make the dimensions of such a product larger enough to qualify it for a different price category
. Learn more about

You can
conditionally ship
lithium-ion, lithium polymer, lithium metal, sodium-ion batteries, and devices containing such batteries to the Allegro Warehouse. Before you do it, pack and label them correctly.
Use packaging that protects them from damage and prevents them from moving within the packaging (this does not apply to batteries placed in devices). Then, put them in a durable and rigid bulk packaging.
Both batteries and devices containing batteries should be packed in packaging that withstands a fall from a height of 1.2 m (in any position) — without damage, battery displacement, or release of contents.
Make sure that the weight of batteries with packaging does not exceed 30 kg.
If you ship devices with batteries, secure them so they cannot turn on during transport.
Secure batteries against short-circuiting or possible contact with electrically conductive materials (if shipped in the same packaging). This applies particularly to batteries that were not pre-packed. Pack such batteries yourself - for example, in a plastic bag.
Place an appropriate sticker on each packaging.
Provide the UN number (starting with "UN") applicable to the batteries in the packaging in the spot marked with an asterisk. You can check them in

.

Pack glass and fragile products in cartons that cover them completely. If possible, secure them also with bubble wrap and paper.
If you ship bulk packagings containing glass or fragile products, indicate the upper part of the packaging.

Pack textile products — which are products made, for example, of woven or knitted fabric — in transparent polyethylene bags or foil. Do it even if products are already in original, textile packagings.
Pack leather products into cartons.
Do not ship clothes on hangers to the Allegro Warehouse. You can only ship products with mini hangers — hangers that are a part of the packaging.

Liquids should be packed in airtight containers with a double-sealed screw cap. In addition — if liquids have to be stored vertically — their height cannot exceed 40 cm.

We handle products with a minimum shelf life of no less than 6 months from the date of acceptance to the Allegro Warehouse.
If a product has a specified expiration date, it should be visible on the product or the unit packaging. If it is not visible — we will not accept the product for storage.
Pack products with the same expiration date and batch number into one carton. Do not pack products with different expiration dates and batch numbers into one carton.
If your products expire in less than 60 days — we will send them back to you at your cost. In addition — other of your products stored in our warehouse are unfit for sale — we will send them all back to you in a single shipment.

EAN (GTIN) code
GTIN is an EAN or other code — learn more about it in
this article
. With that code we can identify products you ship to the Allegro Warehouse. That is why:
the code should be valid and come from a legal source Learn how to check the validity of EAN below
each of your products should be marked with this code or have a label with this code. If a product comes in a protective packaging, the EAN (GTIN) code should also be placed on it.
the code should be visible, clear, and scannable. It is best to place it on a flat surface — not on the edges and folds
each of your products should be marked with only one code. If a given product has additional codes, cover them or black them out with a marker.
How to check the validity of the EAN (GTIN) code
Go to the
GS1 Poland
or the
GS1 Global
website and enter the EAN (GTIN) code provided on the product. That way you can check whether this EAN (GTIN) code has been registered and whether it really corresponds to a given product. You can determine it, for example, based on the manufacturer's name assigned to the EAN (GTIN) code in the GS1 database.
Check whether the EAN (GTIN) code you provide matches the EAN (GTIN) code encrypted as a barcode on the item. To do that, scan the item barcode.
The product EAN (GTIN) code should not begin with:
020–029, 040–049, or 200–299 — EAN (GTIN) codes that begin with those digits are not used on products intended for sale
950, 951, or 960–969 — EAN (GTIN) codes that begin with those digits are used only by GS1
980 — EAN (GTIN) codes that begin with those digits are used for return receipts
981–984 or 990–999 — EAN (GTIN) codes that begin with those digits are used for coupons.
If you product is not a book, a magazine, or a periodical, make sure that it is not marked with ISSN or ISBN — GTINs that begin with: 978, 979, or 980.

How to pack products for shipment
Place all products in cartons. Use durable cartons that will not tear or brake.
Pack products in a way that will prevent them from moving inside the carton. Fill any remaining empty space in the carton. You can, for example, use air cushions, bubble wrap, sheets of paper, or corrugated cardboard. If you pack small items in a large carton, make sure that objects you used to fill empty spaces do not hinder the unpacking and identification of items.
Seal every carton — you can, for example, use a tape or a stapler.
If you ship cartons to the Allegro Warehouse via a courier company, mark them according to their requirements.
If you ship your products in recycled cartons, remove any old labels and stickers or black them out with a marker.
Do not cover the cartons completely with tape and do no wrap them with stretch foil. You can wrap cartons with stretch foil only if you place them on a pallet collectively.

How to place labels correctly
When you
create an ASN
for a shipment in boxes or pallets you can decide — place labels on your own, or let us do it for you. If you want to place the One Fulfillment labels on your own — follow the steps below.
The label should be legible — do not add or cross anything out.  If the information on the label is not legible — generate and print it out again.
Place labels on the side of the carton. If possible, place them on a flat surface — not at the joint of the box flaps.
If you plan to place the cartons on a pallet, then — if possible — place the label in a way it can be scanned without unloading the pallet.
If you place the label in an incorrect place or if the label gets damaged in transport, we may print out and place a new label on accepting your shipment for storage at the warehouse. In that case, we will charge an additional fee — in accordance with the [price list](
