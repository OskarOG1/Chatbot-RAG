Are you starting to sell on Allegro? We have a welcome package for you!
Learn more
.

Choose a business account if you are:
an entrepreneur and want to sell on Allegro or plan business purchases
a board member, a proxy (an employee) of the company, an organizational unit entered into the commercial register (a foundation, an association) and you want to sell or purchase in its name and on its behalf.
The business you want to register on Allegro has to be active — if you have suspended your business, you will not pass the verification.

You can create another account using the same business details if:
the tax identification number of your business was issued in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, Ukraine, or the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland or
the registered office, registered business, or the actual place of conducting business activity is registered within the area specified above.
Creating another account
using the same business data
is simple. All you need to do when you register a new account is provide the NIP (Tax Identification Number) of the business that is already in our system. You will register another account with the same tax identification number only after we have finished verifying the documents of the business linked to your accounts.
If you already have a verified account and want to create another one, you can do it:
in a standard way
through fast registration.
Learn more
.

You can set up your business account in a few simple steps.
How to register a business account
Go to the
registration page
and complete all the fields. If you have any doubts, read the hints under the fields.
Click [download company data] ― we will download the business details from the Central Statistical Office of Poland (
GUS
), the Central Registration and Information on Business (
CEIDG
), or the VAT Information Exchange System (
VIES
). If it is not possible, you will see a relevant message in the form. In this case, provide the business details yourself.

The NIP number (the Polish tax identification number) you provide in the form must match the address of the company's registered office.

Click [sign up] at the end of the registration form.
We will send an email to the email address you provided. Open the email and click [confirm registration].

Check how to register and activate a business account if you have a business:

in a country from outside the European Economic Area.

Remember that you can change your mind
If you change your mind after the registration whether to sell or just buy on your business account, do not worry.
If you choose to have a business account only for business purchases, click [just want to buy?] on the bank account number verification form.  This way, you will not have to make a verification transfer, and your account will be dedicated to business purchases.
However, if you decide you want to sell on Allegro, you can
activate sales on your account
.
