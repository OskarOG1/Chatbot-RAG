What offer sharing is all about
Our rule is:
list once — sell everywhere
. If you want to sell in foreign marketplaces, you do not need to list new offers. All you need to do is submit them for sharing. Once you do it, we check whether they meet all requirements. The verification process usually takes a couple of minutes. If you submit several offers at the same time, we may need more time to process them.
In foreign marketplaces, you can share offers
listed on a business account with at least Neutral
sales quality level
that do not contain products that cannot be sold in a given marketplace
that are available to customers with regular accounts
that come in the
buy now
format
that are not charity offers, auctions, or classifieds
that are linked to the
Allegro Product Catalog
or are product sets
where you enabled international delivery options — at least one for each marketplace you want to share them in
that have been automatically or manually
translated into the language of a given marketplace
— except offers listed in
those categories
where you provide prices in the currency of a given marketplace
that are listed in categories available in the particular foreign marketplace.
How you can meet the requirements
You have already met some of them — as they are the entry requirements for joining One Fulfillment.  They involve holding a business account, the sales format of offers, and linking products to the Product Catalog. You can meet the remaining requirements through the options we enable on Allegro or within One Fulfillment.
To make your offers available to customers with regular accounts, go to the listing form and tick the
all customers
option in the
Who can purchase
the offer section.
When you decide to make an offer available in foreign marketplaces, we will check whether it has been translated. If not, we will translate it automatically.
While sharing offers in a foreign marketplace, you will decide whether to enter your price manually or enable the
Price Converter
.
To sell in foreign marketplaces, add a shipping price list with international delivery options to your offers. You only need to select the
One Fulfillment International
shipping price list when creating or editing offers in the
Delivery and Payment
section.
What international delivery options we will add to your offers
No matter if you decide to share your offers in Czechia, Slovakia, or Hungary — we will automatically add all international delivery options available in One Fulfillment to your offers. Those are:
Allegro DPD Courier Czechia
Allegro DPD Courier Slovakia
Allegro DPD Courier Hungary
Allegro International Courier Czechia.
In addition, if you list offers with products that can be dispatched to a pick-up point or a parcel locker, we will add to them the following delivery options:
Allegro International Pick-up Point Czechia
Allegro International Parcel Lockers Czechia
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Czechia — Packeta Pick-up Point
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Czechia — Packeta Parcel Lockers
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Slovakia — Packeta Pick-up Point
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Slovakia — Packeta Parcel Lockers
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Hungary — Packeta Pick-up Point
Allegro Dispatch from Poland to Hungary — Packeta Parcel Lockers.

We will add the delivery options to parcel lockers and pickup points to offers where the products do not exceed the maximum parcel dimensions:
Allegro Packeta
: 36 x 45 x 61 cm, actual weight: 15 kg
Allegro International Czechia
: 58 x 44 x 48 cm, actual weight: 15 kg.

Once we have added those delivery options to your offers:
Czech buyers will see your offers on allegro.cz
Slovak buyers will see your offers on allegro.sk
Hungarian buyers will see your offers on allegro.hu
buyers on allegro.pl will place orders with delivery to Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary.
Each of those options is available within Allegro Smart! on allegro.cz, allegro.sk, and allegro.hu.

How to share offers in a foreign marketplace
You can do it:
in the listing form, or
in the
My Assortment
tab.
In the listing form
You only need to select the
One Fulfillment International
shipping price list in the
Delivery and Payment
section. Then, we will add delivery options to Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary to that offer. It means that if the offer
meets the requirements
, we will display it on allegro.cz, allegro.sk, and allegro.hu.
When you select the
One Fulfillment International
shipping price list, you will see the additional part in the
Price
section. There, you can set the price in the relevant currency. Set the price in PLN first.
The
Price Converter
option is checked by default. If you want to, you can set the price manually. To do that, uncheck the
Price Converter
option and enter your own price.
Next, finalize listing the offer in a standard way.
In the My Assortment tab
To submit offers for sharing:
check selected offers
on the bottom bulk editing bar, select
Sales policy
, and then
Shipping price list
select the
One Fulfillment International
shipping price list
click [next], and then [save and share offers].
We will automatically submit offers for sharing in foreign marketplaces and enable the default
Price Converter
pricing rule in them.
To check the verification status, use the
market
filter and select the name of the marketplace where you share a given offer. If the offer verification status is
rejected
, you will see a message
complete missing information
displayed below it. Click it and see detailed tips on what you can improve.
How we handle orders placed in foreign marketplaces
We handle orders placed in foreign marketplaces just like orders placed on allegro.pl. Learn more about it in
our article
.
It means when you sell in a foreign marketplace, most One Fulfillment functionalities, tools, and processes work as usual.  You can create ASNs, withdraw products, or check your stock in a regular way. To make selling in the foreign marketplaces easier for you, we will also add a few new functionalities to your One Fulfillment account.  Learn more about them below.
What we will change on your account when you start selling in foreign marketplaces
Payments from buyers
Buyers pay:
in CZK — for purchases made on allegro.cz
in EUR — for purchases made on allegro.sk
in HUF — for purchases made on allegro.hu.
When you share offers in foreign marketplaces, in
Allegro Finance
, you will see payments from buyers both in the Polish and foreign currencies.
Returns
We handle returns made in foreign marketplaces just like the ones
made on allegro.pl
. To make it easier for you and foreign buyers:
we will add to your account
returns terms
in the language of a given marketplace — based on the returns terms you specified in Polish
we will issue refunds in the relevant currency. You should always have enough funds in Czech korunas, euros, and forints ― so that we can use it to refund the buyers. For that purpose, you can
hold money for refunds in Allegro Finance
.
go to the
Payment Refunds
tab to check refund amounts in the currencies of all the marketplaces you share offers in.
Settlements
When you sell in foreign marketplaces, you settle with us:
in PLN — for the
One Fulfillment services
and selling on allegro.pl
in CZK — for selling on allegro.cz
in EUR — for selling on allegro.sk
in HUF — for selling on allegro.hu.
You can check detailed information about the fees for our services in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab. You will check settlements for each currency separately.
Compensation
Regardless of the sales market, we will issue compensation for damaged products in PLN — based on the net prices in the offer in the Polish marketplace.
Contact with buyers
We handle buyers from foreign marketplaces like customers on allegro.pl. It means that
messages from them are distributed between you and us
. We respond to messages distributed to us in the language of a given marketplace. You can use the
translation option available in the Message Center
.
My sales tab
Here, you can check sales statistics for each marketplace.
Summary of produced waste
When we pick and dispatch orders to your customers, we produce waste that should be registered. When you sell abroad, the waste you generate reaches other countries. You can generate a separate report for each delivery country in the
Waste inventory
tab.
Learn more about issues related to waste and packaging in
Czechia
,
Slovakia
, and
Hungary
.
How to prepare products for selling in the foreign marketplaces
To ship your products to Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary, adapt them to the requirements of those countries. They may differ — depending on the type of product. Requirements concern mostly labels, leaflets, or markings available in the language of a given marketplace.
More information is available:
in
our articles
on our
page about foreign marketplaces
at
trade.gov
.
Frequently asked questions

If possible — wait until products destined for the Polish marketplace are sold out. When it happens, ship to the Allegro Warehouse products adapted for sale in the foreign marketplaces, and share your offers.

The delivery time for all delivery options to Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary we offer within One Fulfillment is 2 working days.

You do not need to do it. If you clone offers already shared in a foreign marketplace to your One Fulfillment account, we will automatically add international delivery options and display them in that marketplace.
