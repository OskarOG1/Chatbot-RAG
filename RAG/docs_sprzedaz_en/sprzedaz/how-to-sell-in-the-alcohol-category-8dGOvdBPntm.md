Preliminary information

You can only sell alcoholic beverages on Allegro after signing a separate agreement with us. To do so, please contact us.

If you want to sell in the Alcohol category, you must:
have a business account
have the appropriate authorizations, which we will verify
operate a brick-and-mortar retail outlet
offer the option of in-person pickup at your point of sale
and
a special DPD delivery method:
Allegro courier for age-restricted products
.
During delivery, the courier will check the sobriety and age of the recipient of the shipment and the PIN code that the buyer will receive from the carrier. Age verification is based solely on a physical document (passport, driver's license, ID card) – it is not possible in the mObywatel app.

You can sell alcohol on Smart! and outside of the service. For a customer to be eligible for Smart! delivery, the order value from a single seller must be at least:
65 PLN — if they activated Allegro Smart! before March 2, 2026
49.90 PLN — if they activated Allegro Smart! on or after March 2, 2026.
Learn more
.
You can also
set up free delivery for orders above a certain amount
.
The commission on sales in the Alcohol category is 5% net (6.15% gross).
The Alcohol category is only available on allegro.pl.
It is not possible to ship orders containing alcohol from Poland abroad or from abroad to Poland.
You can withdraw money from sales from the Funds and transaction history tab once the courier has collected the parcel from you or once you have marked in the Orders tab that the customer has collected the order in person. This applies to the amount for the entire order containing products from the Alcohol category.
Separate rules apply in the Alcohol category with regard to sales support offers.

What licenses do you need and how will we verify them
To sell alcoholic beverages on Allegro, you must have a license to sell the type of alcohol in question at your brick-and-mortar point of sale. The license must be issued in Poland.
Type of authorization
Scope of authorization
A
Scope of authorization
beverages containing up to 4.5% alcohol and beer
B
Scope of authorization
beverages containing alcohol above 4.5% to 18%, except for beer
C
Scope of authorization
beverages containing more than 18% alcohol
Once we sign the agreement, we will email you a form in which you will:
add a scan of your license or licenses – to sell all types of alcohol, you must have 3 types of licenses – add each type of license separately
you will add a scan of the license fee
you will confirm that you meet the requirements of the law and our terms and conditions.
We will inform you of the verification result by email within 2–3 days.

30 days before your license expires, we will ask you to send us a new one by email. Please send us your new license at least 14 days before your current one expires. If you fail to do so, we will automatically terminate all your listings in the Alcohol category and block you from posting new listings in this category until your new license has been verified. You will then be able to easily resume your terminated listings.
In accordance with the agreement, we are entitled to randomly check your permissions even after positive verification – when you are already selling alcohol. We may therefore ask you to resend your documents.
If you want to expand your product range to include alcoholic beverages that require a different license than the one we have already verified, please fill out the same form again. While we verify your new license, you can continue selling as usual.

Offers listed in the Alcohol category:
can only be in the buy now format
cannot be listed on Allegro Local
cannot be listed as charity items
cannot be listed on Allegro Business
must have a delivery price list with the option of personal collection and the
Allegro courier method for age-restricted products
.
You cannot list:
beer and cider in bottles with a capacity of less than 300 ml
alcoholic beverages with an alcohol content of more than 4.5% and a capacity of less than 400 ml
alcoholic beverages with an alcohol content of over 50% – regardless of bottle capacity
products that are listed in Appendix 3 to the agreement we have signed.

The content of offers involving alcoholic beverages must be formulated in accordance with the law and the principles of social responsibility
. Alcohol offers may not:
be directed at minors or depict them in any form
suggest that alcohol consumption improves physical performance
suggest that alcohol consumption does not affect driving ability
contain information that alcohol has medicinal, calming, or stimulating properties
encourage excessive alcohol consumption
portray abstinence or moderate consumption in a negative light
emphasize high alcohol content as a feature that improves the quality of the beverage.
You are fully responsible for the content of your offers.

How does personal pickup and delivery of purchases work?
For offers in the Alcohol category, you must add a delivery price list:
with the option of personal pickup (payment in advance or cash on delivery)
and
with the
Allegro courier method for age-restricted products
(payment in advance only, payment to the DPD courier is not possible). This is the only courier delivery method that you can offer for alcohol products. We do not deliver alcohol to collection points or parcel machines.

Once you save this delivery price list, it will no longer be possible to edit it.

Personal collection
For offers involving alcohol, we require a single personal collection point. This must be the address of the point of sale for which your alcohol sales license was issued. Add this address in the
Personal collection point
tab.
When picking up an order in person, your customer is subject to the same rules of sobriety and age as when purchasing alcohol in a regular store. The goods can only be released to the person who placed the order. You cannot release an order containing alcohol during local prohibition hours. Mark the pickup in the
Orders
tab.

When sending parcels, we will automatically add mandatory additional services:

Return documents (DZ)

We add these services to meet the formal requirements for alcohol delivery and to ensure that the shipment is delivered to a person authorized to receive it, who is of legal age and sober.

Orders containing alcohol cannot be sent to workplaces, including government offices, schools, or hospitals.

Allegro Smart!
The conditions for the Smart! label for offers involving alcoholic beverages are different from those for other offers. Your offers in the Alcohol category will be Smart! if you offer personal pickup at your brick-and-mortar store and the
Allegro courier method for age-restricted products
, and if you meet the
terms and conditions of sale
.
The maximum amount you can set in your price lists for this delivery method to qualify for the Smart! label is
PLN 21.99
.
Customers will benefit from Smart! delivery if they make a purchase from one seller for a minimum of:
65 PLN — if they activated Allegro Smart! before March 2, 2026
49.90 PLN — if they activated Allegro Smart! on or after March 2, 2026.
Combined orders
A combined order, i.e., one that includes alcoholic beverages and products outside the Alcohol category, must be fulfilled in accordance with the rules for orders containing only alcoholic beverages.

The customer places a single order with you for:
100 g of pitted green olives
0.75 l of dry white wine.
The buyer chooses courier delivery. Due to the fact that the order contains alcohol, you must send the shipment using the
Allegro courier method for age-restricted products
. In order for us to fulfill the above order, this method should be used for both offers.

If you add the
Allegro courier delivery method for age-restricted products
to all your offers—including those outside the Alcohol category—you will be able to fulfill combined orders correctly. Buyers will only see this method in offers with alcoholic beverages. To add this method to other offers, create a separate price list.
Delivery costs in Smart! and outside the service
You will pay the same amount for shipments sent using the
Allegro courier for age-restricted products
method in Smart! and outside the service. You will settle your account once a month directly with us on the basis of an invoice.
The maximum amount you can set in your price lists for this delivery method is PLN 22.99, but
to qualify for the Smart! label, you can set a maximum of PLN 21.99
. These prices apply to parcels with the following weight and dimensions:
up to 10 kg of actual or dimensional weight
longest side – maximum 150 cm
sum of width, length, and height – maximum 300 cm.
If the parcel exceeds 10 kg, we will charge additional fees in accordance with the price list.
The above prices already include the fee for mandatory additional services:
delivery to the recipient's hands
return receipt.
The courier will make two attempts to deliver the parcel, after prior contact with the buyer.
Maximum weight and dimensions of shipments
Maximum actual or volumetric weight: 31.5 kg. Volumetric weight is calculated using the following formula: width cm x length cm x height cm / 6000.
Maximum dimensions:
length of the longest side: maximum 150 cm
total length of sides: maximum 300 cm.

before receiving the shipment

withdraws from the purchase agreement – this automatically means that they are also withdrawing from the delivery agreement.
Add information about withdrawal from the purchase agreement and delivery agreement to your offers.

We have disabled automatic refunds for customers in the Alcohol category. You can only
request a refund yourself in the Orders tab
.

The same return policy applies

Withdrawal from the alcohol delivery agreement
Your customer may withdraw from the delivery agreement without giving a reason
until the shipment is delivered

Orders

If the customer has purchased several products in one order and has only withdrawn from the purchase agreement for some of them, the agreement for the delivery of alcohol remains in force. The customer cannot withdraw from the delivery agreement for only part of the order.
Alcohol sales and your sales ratings
We can remove negative ratings for transactions in the Alcohol category if they relate to situations beyond your control, i.e., problems with the courier delivering the shipment to the buyer, for example, when the buyer:
does not show proof of identity to the courier
is underage
is intoxicated
does not have the PIN code to receive the shipment.
In other cases, we treat ratings the same as outside the Alcohol category.
