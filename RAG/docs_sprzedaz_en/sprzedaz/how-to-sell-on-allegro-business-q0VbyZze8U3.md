Allegro Business is available to everyone at:
business.allegro.pl
— in Poland
business.allegro.cz
— in Czechia.
On Allegro Business, we share offers of sellers who meet specific requirements. There, you will find the
same offers that are visible on Allegro, and all the tools you use daily
.
On Allegro Business, you can offer convenient solutions for businesses:
business discounts — wholesale discount, order discount, and turnover discount
displaying net and gross prices, and VAT rates
support for intra-community transactions — 0% VAT
payment methods for businesses
easier invoice management — for example, bulk download of invoices from a selected period
easier purchase management — the purchase history is divided into private and business.
What business discounts you can set
On Allegro Business in Poland:
order discount
wholesale discount
.
On Allegro Business in Czechia:
wholesale discount
turnover discount
.

If you add a wholesale discount to an offer on business.allegro.pl that qualifies for sharing on business.allegro.cz — it will be visible to business customers from Poland and Czechia. You cannot set a wholesale discount only for a selected marketplace.

Thanks to Allegro Business, your offers will be visible to more buyers in Poland and Czechia. You do not bear any additional costs. All sales rules on Allegro Business are the same as on allegro.pl and allegro.cz.

The conditions for qualifying offers on the business version of the platform are
the same
for the Polish and Czech marketplaces. We will display your offers on Allegro Business if you meet all the requirements below:
you hold a
business account
your business is registered in one of the EU countries
you ship products from the countries where Allegro runs its sales platform (Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, or Hungary) or you use
One Fulfillment by Allegro
we have
verified your offer and published it on allegro.pl
or
shared it on allegro.cz
your offers are linked to the Allegro Product Catalog
you issue VAT invoices, and you have
set a VAT rate in the offer
that is applicable in the country you dispatch the goods from
you list offers with the
margin VAT invoice
and
non-VAT invoice
parameter where the VAT rate is not required
if your company is located outside Poland, we will also check whether your EU VAT number is active in the
VIES database

display those offers
to both regular buyers making private purchases

, and to business customers on

How we display the price in the offer
If you declare the VAT rate applicable in the country you dispatch the goods from, we will use it to automatically calculate the net value of the goods.
We will display the net price as the main price to entrepreneurs purchasing within Allegro Business
. The net price is not the basis for settlement between you and the buyer — the gross price is. However, VAT rates and net prices in offers make it easier for customers to make business purchases.
You can use the Price Converter in your offers submitted for sharing on allegro.cz. It allows you to update prices in sales currencies in the My Assortment tab with just one click.
See how it works
. The price is the same for regular buyers on allegro.pl and allegro.cz, and for business customers on business.allegro.pl and business.allegro.cz. You cannot set different prices.

The price in an offer on allegro.pl is 123 PLN. You dispatch the order from Poland and have set a 23% VAT rate in that offer. That is why, on business.allegro.pl, we will show the
100 PLN net price as the main price
, together with the 23% VAT rate and the 123 PLN gross price.
If you also share that offer on allegro.cz and use the Price Converter, the price in CZK in that offer will be 703.33 CZK (price at the moment of conversion). That is why, on business.allegro.cz, we will show the
571.81 CZK net price as the main price
, together with the 23% VAT rate and the 703.33 CZK gross price.
