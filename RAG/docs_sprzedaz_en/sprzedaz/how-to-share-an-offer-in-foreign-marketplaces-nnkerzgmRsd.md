You do not have to list your offers again to sell in foreign marketplaces. If you already sell on allegro.pl, add to them international delivery options and adjust only the necessary details. That way, buyers from all the foreign marketplaces you select can buy your products
in one offer
.

If you sell on a regular account, you will not be able to share offers in additional marketplaces. What is more, you will not have access to options related to foreign marketplaces in the Allegro tabs and tools.
Check how to switch to a business account
.

What the offer sharing is all about
Our key rule is:
list once — sell everywhere
. You do not have to list a separate offer in each marketplace. Nevertheless, buyers from the foreign marketplace will see that offer in their language and local currency. Learn how it works.
You list an offer in the marketplace where your account is registered — that is, on allegro.pl.
You add to it at least
one delivery option to the country where you want to sell your products
— we will automatically consider such an offer
already submitted for sharing in foreign marketplaces and enable the default
Price Converter
pricing rule in it
.
We verify whether a given offer meets the
sales policy of a given marketplace
.
If not — we will let you know what exactly you should add or correct.
If yes (or if you make the necessary adjustments) — done! From that moment, buyers from a given marketplace can also buy in your offer.

If you enable
Allegro International Czechia
— the delivery option to Czechia — in your shipping price list and add that shipping price list to your offers, we will automatically submit them for sharing on allegro.cz. If they meet the

If you enable international delivery with DPD Courier to Czechia and Slovakia in your shipping price list — we will automatically submit offers with that shipping price list for sharing on allegro.cz and allegro.sk. When those offers meet the requirements, we will display them to customers in Czechia and Slovakia.

What requirements the offers in foreign marketplaces need to meet
Sales rules differ slightly between countries. In addition, some features and categories you are familiar with in the marketplace of your registration, may not be available in the foreign marketplaces. On the other hand, some features and categories unavailable in the marketplace of your registration may be available in the foreign marketplaces.
That is why
we will verify every offer that comes with delivery options to the country where you want to sell products
. We will check your offer and let you know whether it meets all the requirements of a given marketplace. The verification usually takes just a few moments. If you
edit shipping price lists in bulk
, we may need a little more time.

What is more, you can share offers on allegro.cz, allegro.sk and allegro.hu only if you dispatch your products from the EEA (the EU + Norway, Iceland, and Liechtenstein), the UK, Ukraine, or Switzerland. Otherwise, offers in which you add delivery options to Slovakia or Hungary will not pass the qualification process — we will reject them and will not share them in those marketplaces. Such offers will be displayed only to buyers in your registration marketplace — allegro.pl.

When we verify whether your offer meets the requirements
We do it every time:
you add to your shipping price lists at least one delivery option to the country where you want to sell products
you edit price lists that contain a delivery option to the country where you want to sell products in bulk
you
relist
the offer you shared — automatically or manually
you
edit
the following in the offer you shared:
product
category
title — in the language of the marketplace it was listed in
description — in the language of the marketplace it was listed in
shipping price list — if you remove a delivery option to one of the foreign marketplaces.
If your offer does not meet the requirements of a given marketplace anymore — we will stop displaying it there.

In that case, we will reject every new offer that can be shared. If your sales quality level remains below Neutral for 7 days,
we will hide your offers in all foreign marketplaces

How to share offers in foreign marketplaces
All you need to do is add to your shipping price lists at least one delivery option to the country where you want to sell products and link that shipping price list with offers. If you do that, we will automatically consider such offers
already submitted for sharing in foreign marketplaces and set the
Price Converter
pricing rule in them by default
.

If some of your offers used to be shared in foreign marketplaces but you decided to withdraw them from sharing — they will remain like that. If you want, you can share them on your own — in the My Assortment tab.
Learn more

If you want to share many offers in the foreign marketplaces at once, you can use the

Go to the

Go to the Shipping price lists section and click [add a new price list]. Name your shipping price list — this will help you identify it more easily in the future.
Check the international delivery options — depending on the marketplaces you want to sell in.

Price list settings summary

information on the marketplaces where we will display offers with that shipping price list —  if the verification is positive
delivery options you checked — divided into marketplaces
an additional section called [Check the prices of offers on markets] — if you edit a shipping price list already linked to offers.
Click [save].
If you edit a shipping price list that is already linked to offers, you can also check the
prices of your offers in all marketplaces
.
When you edit the shipping price list,
we will redirect you to the Delivery Settings tab
and inform you that we have ordered to share offers in an additional marketplace — if you edited a shipping price list already linked to offers

Check the selected offers.
Expand the
Sales Policy
option you can see on the green bar. Next, click

A window will pop out. Select the shipping price list that contains delivery options to a foreign marketplace and click [next].

Price Converter

If you submit several offers to be shared, it may take us a moment to verify them — the more offers you submit, the longer it will take. In the case of increased traffic on the platform, that may take up to an hour.

How to manage your shared offers
In the listing form
If you enable at least one delivery option to the country where you want to sell products, go to the
Delivery and payment
section of the listing form.
There, you can check the marketplaces we will display the offer in after you add  a given shipping price list to it.
The
Price
section allows you to edit offer prices in foreign currencies. It will be valid only for buyers in the marketplace the given currency applies to.
To edit that section, set the price in the currency of the marketplace of your registration first.
For the foreign marketplaces, we will set the Price Converter rule by default. If, for a given marketplace, you want to:
opt out of setting the pricing rule — uncheck the box next to the rule's name. Then, we will unlock the box where you can manually enter the price.
change the default settings — click [change rule]. Then, you can select one of the saved rules or go to creating a new one.
When converting the prices,
we round the result
— so that the final prices align with standard market practices and denominations in use for a given currency.
Check how we round the prices during conversion
.
In the cost summary section, you can find:
a table with the summary of costs divided into sales markets
— separately for each marketplace, in the currency that applies there
the summary of fees and sales commission
— click [details] to see the exact fees and commission amounts for a given market
a reminder
that you can settle sales on each market in a corresponding currency. That means that for sales in a foreign marketplace we will charge you commission and fees in the corresponding currency — the same one in which buyers in that marketplace will pay for their purchases.
In the My Assortment tab
This tab allows you to:
edit the details of the qualified offers — for example, edit the price in foreign currency or the translation of the offer description
share or withdraw an offer by editing shipping price lists in bulk
permanently withdraw offers from selected marketplaces —  in that case, we will not automatically submit them for sharing, even if you add to them a shipping price list with delivery options to a respective marketplace.
Check all functions this tab offers and learn how to manage the offers you share effectively.
How to permanently withdraw selected offers from foreign marketplaces

You can permanently withdraw only offers that have been submitted for sharing before — for example, by adding to them shipping price lists containing a delivery option to a given marketplace.

To withdraw several offers from the marketplace:
Select offers, click
Availability
, and then
Withdraw from the additional market
.
We will display a window. Select the marketplace from which you want to withdraw offers and click [save].

If the offer you submitted does not meet all the requirements of a given foreign marketplace, we will reject it and we will not display it to buyers in that marketplace. In the
My Assortment

Go to the

Use the filters. They will help you quickly find the offers submitted for sharing in the selected marketplace that we rejected. Select the following filters:
