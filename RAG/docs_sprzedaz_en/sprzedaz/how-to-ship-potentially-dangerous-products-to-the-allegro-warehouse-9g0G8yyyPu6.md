Before you ship dangerous products to the Allegro Warehouse, take a look at our glossary. It contains the definitions of basic terms that you may find useful while preparing products for dispatch. If you need more information, contact the ADR advisor or use the materials prepared by the relevant entities — for example, the
Polish Ministry of Infrastructure
.
Glossary

Dangerous products
are objects, materials, or substances that can pose risks in storage or transport. This includes threats to health, the environment, and property. Such products can be, for example, corrosive, flammable, poisonous, or pressurized. Those also include some products commonly used in households — such as spray deodorants, adhesives, paints, laptops, and cell phones.

The ADR agreement
is the international agreement concerning the carriage of dangerous goods. It comprises provisions for road transport, cargo security, or labeling of dangerous substances
Learn more
(available in Polish).

The ADR special provisions
constitute
chapter 3.3 of the ADR agreement
. They concern selected chemical substances and dangerous materials.  Thanks to them you will know whether a product marked with a specific UN number requires, for example, special parcel marking or is exempt from the ADR regulations.

The CLP regulation
is the EU regulation under which substances and mixtures are classified. The main purpose of this document is to identify potentially dangerous substances and mixtures. The regulation also informs how to:
pack and mark such substances and mixtures before introducing them on the market
store, transport, and dispose of such substances and mixtures
prevent and counteract possible risks associated with these substances and mixtures.
Learn more
(available in Polish).

The safety data sheet
(SDS) is a document that informs about the risks posed by a given substance or mixture. Based on that information, we decide whether to accept a given product for storage at the Allegro Warehouse. The safety data sheet also informs how to store the products in question and what to do in case of an accident or leak.
In the context of One Fulfillment, the most important SDS information can be found in:
Section 2, which contains information about the type of hazard (H-phrases)
Section 14, which contains information about the transport, UN number, packing group, and any special regulations.
The manufacturer, importer, or supplier is responsible for providing the product's safety data sheet. The safety data sheet can come in a paper or electronic form. It should be available in the language of the country where you sell a given product. It means that if you sell within One Fulfillment also in foreign marketplaces, we may ask you to present the safety data sheet in the respective languages.

The UN is a 4-digit identification number used to mark dangerous substances and products. The full list of UN numbers is available in the ADR agreement.
If a given product is subject to the ADR provisions, information about its UN number can be found in section 14(1) of the safety data sheet.

Hazard phrases (H-phrases)
are codes usually composed of one letter and three digits that inform about the risks a given substance or mixture can pose.
Information about the H-phrase should be placed on the product label. The list of H-phrases is available in the CLP regulation.
