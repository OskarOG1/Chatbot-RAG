Before you ship your products to the Allegro Warehouse, prepare and submit the ASN. Stages of generating the ASN vary depending on how you pack your products for shipment. You can select:
boxes or pallets
containers
.

If you often ship the same products to the Allegro Warehouse, you can redo your ASN. That option allows you to conveniently create a new ASN  based on the one you submitted to us before.
Learn more
.

How to create an ASN for products that come in boxes or on pallets
Go to the
My One Fulfillment
page and click the [create ASN] located in the
Advised deliveries
tab.
You will see a list of products your offers are linked to. Click the ones you want to ship to the Allegro Warehouse. Select the ones you want to ship to the warehouse and specify their number. You can easily find products by using the search bar.
When you add products previously stored in Allegro Warehouse to an ASN, and we know their dimensions, we will update the
Storage space
section with their details. After we do that, the column
In ASN
will show information about the storage space that the products you deliver to the warehouse under this ASN will take.
If we have not stored those products in the warehouse before, add their volume. To do that, click the pencil icon next to
no data
.
Click [see added products] and make sure that the list contains all products that you want to ship to the warehouse. You can download the list — all you need to do is click [download packing list as PDF]. You can, for example, forward the list to your employees so that they can prepare product for shipment accordingly.
Once you made sure the list is complete — click [next].

You can save the list of products selected for dispatch as a draft anytime — just click [save and finish later]. You will find it in the
ASN list
tab.

Specify how you will pack your products for dispatch. Select the biggest possible way of packing you will use.

You pack products into boxes, which you later place on a pallet. In that case, select
pallet
as the
packing method
.

Depending on the method you selected, specify:
the number of boxes you are going to ship to the warehouse
or
the number of pallets you are going to ship to the warehouse.
Specify whether you will place our One Fulfillment labels on boxes/pallets on your own, or if we should do it for you on accepting products for storage (for example, if the shipment comes directly from the manufacturer or distributor).
If you decide to place the labels on your own — download and print them. Select the format you want to download labels in — PDF or ZPL. Then, click [download labels]. Check
how to place labels correctly
.
Select how you will ship products to the Allegro Warehouse:
own transport
— provide the license plate number of the vehicle, estimated delivery date, and the country of dispatch
courier delivery
— provide the consignment note number for the whole shipment, estimated delivery date, and the country of dispatch. If your products come in several shipments, provide the consignment note of each of them.
delivery from manufacturer
— provide the manufacturer's or distributor's name, estimated delivery time, and the country of dispatch.
In the case of courier delivery — complete the consignment note number(s). This helps us accept your products at the warehouse more efficiently. Provide as many consignment note numbers as the carriers you send to the warehouse.
Click [next].
Check whether the information you provided is correct. If you want, you can download labels or the product list as a PDF.
Click [send ASN]. Done! Now you can ship your products to the Allegro Warehouse.
Information about your ASN will appear in our warehouse system. It will allow us to accept your products for storage.
Until your products reach the Allegro Warehouse, the status of your ASN will be
pending delivery
. You can edit a given ASN until its status changes to
receipt in progress
. To do it, go to the ASN list tab, click the three dots next to a selected ASN, and click [edit].
How to create an ASN for products in containers
You can only ship one container within a single ASN. If you want to ship more of them,
create a separate ASN
for each.
To create the ASN:
Go to the
My One Fulfillment
page and click the [create ASN] located in the
Advised deliveries
tab.
Click the ones you want to ship to the Allegro Warehouse. Select the ones you want to ship to the warehouse and specify their number. You can easily find products by using the search bar.
When you add products previously stored in Allegro Warehouse to an ASN, and we know their dimensions, we will update the
Storage space
section with their details. After we do that, the column
In ASN
will show information about the storage space that the products you deliver to the warehouse under this ASN will take.
If we have not stored those products in the warehouse before, add their volume. To do that, click the pencil icon next to
no data

You can download the list — all you need to do is click [download packing list as PDF]. You can, for example, forward the list to your employees so that they can prepare product for shipment accordingly.
Once you made sure the list is complete — click [next].

You can save the list of products selected for dispatch as a draft anytime — just click [save and finish later]. You will find it in the
ASN list
tab.

Select container.
Specify the number of packagings of the product you will ship to the warehouse in the container.

You ship 500 units of a given product in a container. 200 units are in unit packaging, and the remaining 300 pieces are packaged in bulk packaging — 100 packages, three pieces in each. The total number of packages you place in the container is 300 (200 unit packagings + 100 bulk packagings).

Select how you will ship products to the Allegro Warehouse:
own transport
— provide the license plate number of the vehicle that will deliver products and the estimated delivery time
courier delivery
— provide the consignment note number for the whole shipment, estimated delivery date, and the country of dispatch. If your products come in several shipments, provide the consignment note of each of them.
delivery from the manufacturer
—  provide the manufacturer's or distributor's name, estimated delivery time, and the country of dispatch.
Then, click [next].
Check whether the information you provided is correct. If you want, you can download labels or the product list as a PDF.
Click [send ASN]. Done! Now you can ship your products to the Allegro Warehouse.
Information about your ASN will appear in our warehouse system. It will allow us to accept your products for storage.

pending delivery

When you cannot create a new ASN
According to the Terms & Conditions of the service, we may temporarily suspend accepting certain products for storage at the Allegro Warehouse. If we do that, you will see information in the ASN creator. Next to products for which you cannot create an ASN, we will display the following message:
Produkt został objęty czasową blokadą awizacji (We have temporarily suspended creating ASNs for this product)
.
When you hover the cursor over it, you will see the reason for suspension. It may be due to:
limited storage space
— in that case, try to create the ASN in a couple of days. The limits should not last longer than 72 hours from introduction.
high value of the Out of stock in column for selected products
— it means that, according to the calculations you can check in the
out of stock in
column in the
My Stock
tab, the number of product units in stock is sufficient. We consider the following number of days sufficient:
60 days — for sellers whose businesses are registered in the Asia and Pacific region
30 days — for other sellers.
We cannot dispose of textile products. If you want to ship such products to the Allegro Warehouse, you need to provide an address for withdrawals. Learn more about
withdrawing products from the Allegro Warehouse
.

If you often ship the same products to the Allegro Warehouse, you can redo an ASN.  You can do it in two ways:
in the
ASN list
tab. Select the ASN you want to redo and click the three vertical dots next to it. Next, click [redo ASN].
in the details of a selected ASN. All you need to do is click [redo ASN].

You can calculate the volume of a product as follows:
Product length x product width x product height = product volume
.
However, if you dispatch products:
in boxes
— calculate the volume [height (m) x length (m) x width (m)] of each box in which you dispatch products. Then, add up these values.
on pallets
— calculate the volume [height (m) x length (m) x width (m)] of each pallet on which you dispatch products. Then, add up these values.
Keep in mind that in the
Storage space
section, we display the volume in m3. Therefore, provide the volume for those products in this unit.
