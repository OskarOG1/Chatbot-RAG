How to sign in with a Google, Facebook, or Apple account
If you registered your Allegro account with the same email address as your Google, Facebook, or Apple account, you can link them together. This will allow you to quickly sign in to Allegro using the [continue with Google], [continue with Facebook], or [continue with Apple] button.

The [continue with Apple] option is only available in the Allegro app for iOS for now.

How to enable sign-in with a Google, Facebook, or Apple account
Open the
sign-in page
and select [continue with Google], [continue with Facebook], or [continue with Apple].
Sign in to the selected service and confirm you want to link the accounts. Use the same email address you registered your Allegro account with.

How to sign in with a passkey
A passkey is a secure sign-in method that does not require you to create a separate password. You will sign in to Allegro the same way you unlock your device — for example, with a fingerprint, face scan, or PIN.
How it works
When you set the passkey, we will create a secure connection between your Allegro account and the device you use. During the sign-in, you just unlock the device with the set sign-in method, confirming your identity that way.
Most of the verification takes place locally on your device. All we receive is the confirmation that you succeeded.
We do not access your data, such as PIN, fingerprint, or face scan
. You get improved security and convenience.

If you wish, you can use other sign-in methods on a chosen device — even if you have enabled the passkey sign-in.

When you can use the passkeys
To sign in with a passkey, you need:
an up-to-date version of one of the popular browsers, for example, Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Opera, or Microsoft Edge
a secure program to store passkeys, such as Apple Keychain or Google Password Manager — such programs are often installed by default by the device manufacturer.

If your passkey storage program uses the cloud, you will be able to sign in with this method on multiple devices.
For programs that do not use the cloud, you can create separate passkeys for different devices.

How to enable signing in with a passkey
Go to the
Passkeys
tab. You will find it in your account settings, in the Safety section.
Click [create passkey].
You will see a new window with a few options to choose from. They can differ depending on the operating system and the device you use. To create a new passkey, follow the instructions in that window.
If you managed to create a passkey, you will see it in the
Passkeys
tab. There, we will display information about when the passkey was created, and which program on your device has access to it.
Done! From that moment, you can sign in to Allegro the same way you unlock your device.
What you gain using a passkey with two-factor authentication
When you sign in with a passkey, we do not need any additional confirmation. We will not ask you for it within the
two-factor authentication
— even if you have enabled that option on your account.
However, we will not change the two-factor authentication settings on your account. If, after providing the passkey, you want to sign in with a different method, we will ask you for a confirmation, as always.
How to disable the passkey sign-in and remove the passkey from the device
To securely disable that sign-in method, remove the passkey both from your Allegro account, and your device.
If you remove the passkey only from your Allegro settings, your device can still suggest it as an available sign-in method, even though it will no longer work.
How to remove a passkey from your device
Detailed instructions depend on your passkey storage program. You will see its name in the
Passkeys
tab. Examples of such programs include Google Password Manager and Apple iCloud Keychain.
After checking the program's name, search for it on your device. Go to its settings and remove the chosen passkey for signing-in to Allegro.
How to remove the passkeys from your Allegro account
Go to the
Passkeys
tab.
Click [remove] next to the chosen passkey on the list.
In the new window, click [yes, remove] to confirm your decision.
