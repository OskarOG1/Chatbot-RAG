How to dispatch a parcel through Ship with Allegro
Step 1: Select the order you want to dispatch
Open the
Orders
tab and click
[dispatch parcel]
next to the selected order. We will redirect you to the dispatch form. We will complete the details provided by the buyer.
If you want to dispatch parcels for several orders simultaneously, mark them in the
Orders
tab and select the
[send multiple]
option on the green bar at the bottom of the page. You will be redirected to the dispatch form for the first selected order.
At the top of the form, you will see the basic order information:
the buyer's login
ordered products — if there are several of them, you can expand a detailed list with the costs
the delivery option selected by the buyer
additional information from the buyer.
If you dispatch parcels for several orders simultaneously, we will hint to you which of the orders the details concern — for example,
1 of 2
.
Step 2: Check the delivery and dispatch details
Based on the delivery details provided by the buyer in the order, we automatically complete the first three sections of the form:
Dispatch under the agreement
— the
agreement you will use to settle the parcel
— we will automatically select the agreement with the carrier chosen by the buyer
Delivery
— the details of the buyer and the address the carrier will deliver the parcel to
Dispatch
— your details and the address that will appear on the label — we will automatically provide the information from your
address book
or
your account information
.
The appearance of those sections and available options will differ depending on the delivery option the buyer has chosen.
Check if all the details are correct. You can also edit them — for example, provide different parcel recipient's details or change the pick-up point.
Next to the selected delivery options, you will see an additional section
Decide how you will send parcel
. You need to check an option — otherwise, you will not dispatch the parcel.
Step 3: Provide the dimensions and number of shipments
In the
Shipment
section:
select the shipment type —
parcel
or
envelope
enter its weight and dimensions or select the proper size. You can also use our hints on parcel weight.

The proposed weight is just a suggestion. Make sure it is the same as the actual weight before dispatching the parcel.

We will automatically calculate the parcel size and dimensional weight. If the dimensions of your parcels exceed the maximum values, we will display them in the form in red and mark them as an error. You will not dispatch parcels through Ship with Allegro until you correct their dimensions.
We show you the estimated parcel cost based on the size and weight you provided. You can check them in the
Calculated fee for delivery section
.
The costs we calculate in this section are based on the information from the form. We also take into account the shipping price list for the selected delivery option and Allegro Smart! conditions. If you change a value in any of the fields, we will update our calculations.
If you are a
seller from Poland
, this section shows
gross amounts
.
If you are a seller
from outside Poland
, this section shows
net amounts
.

The cost displayed in the new section is a prognosis calculated based on the information you provide and the shipping price list for the selected delivery option. If the final parcel weight (factual or dimensional) or dimensions are different, the delivery fee may change. What is more, at the stage of dispatching a parcel, we cannot foresee certain fees, as they depend on the parcel's shape or packaging type, which is confirmed only by the carrier. This applies to services like Non-standard parcel.
You will be able to check the final cost later — in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab.

If the dimensions you enter differ from the actual parcel size, we will add the price difference to your
Settlements with Allegro
.
In the
Shipment
section, you can also provide the information we will place on the label:
your reference number — for example, the warehouse, order, or invoice number
additional information — for example, the parcel's contents or instructions for the courier.
Step 4: Enter the additional parcel protection and payment on delivery values
You will see the
Additional Parcel Protection
section beneath the parcel dimensions. We will display the
Payment on delivery
section only if the delivery option you dispatch your parcel with comes with that payment method.
In those fields, we will automatically enter the order details:
in the
Additional Parcel Protection
field — we will enter the value of purchased products
in the
POD amount
field — we will enter the sum of the purchased products' value and the delivery cost.
You can edit the default amounts. We enter all the amounts
in the currency of the marketplace the buyer placed their order in
. The insurance amounts of DHL and DPD parcels are an exception — we always provide them in PLN.

We will sum the insurance and POD amounts you provide for all the shipments from a given order.
Correct the amounts we entered automatically
if you want to dispatch the order in several shipments.
Do not enter the entire amount next to each shipment — do it according to the value of the products dispatched in a given shipment. You can check your calculations in the summary at the bottom of the form.
Learn more
.

If you dispatch a
shipment with payment on delivery
, you can also check there how you will receive the money from the buyer.
In the case of
orders from the marketplace you are registered in
(allegro.pl), with delivery within Poland — the money from the buyer will be transferred to your bank account.
In the case of
orders from the foreign marketplaces
(for example, allegro.cz) — we will add the payment to your funds in Allegro. You can check it in the
Funds and Operations History
tab. You can use that tab to easily pay out funds.
We can transfer the payment from the buyer only to the bank account you have
added and verified
in the
Payout Settings
tab. If you have added and verified several payout accounts, you can select one of them from a drop-down list.
We will verify the account number also when you dispatch parcels through the Ship with Allegro API. If you provide an unverified account number, you will see a message:
This delivery option requires the verified IBAN
.

The only carrier whose parcels you can dispatch through Ship with Allegro under your own agreement is InPost. In this case, you will settle directly with the carrier. They will transfer the money from the buyer to the bank account you have provided in your account settings in the InPost Parcel Manager. That is why you do not need to provide us with the bank account number in the form or when you dispatch through the API. We will remind you about it in the dispatch form.

Step 5: Select additional services
For selected delivery options, you can order additional services, such as delivery at specific times or delivery of parcels with non-standard shapes. Click [show more additional services] to expand the full list of services available for the selected option.
You may be charged extra fees for additional services
, according to the
price list
. Select only those services you want to order for a given parcel. You can also check the detailed cost of additional servicec selected for your parcel in the
Calculated fee for delivery
section.
Step 6: Save the shipment or add another one
If you want to dispatch
more than one shipment for the same order
, click [add another shipment] at the bottom of the form. We will display a new section where you can enter the details of another shipment from the order — just as you did with the previous one.
Once you add all the shipments from the order or you want to dispatch
just one shipment
— you can confirm the order and dispatch it. To do that, click [save] at the bottom of the form. In the next window, click [order shipments].
Done! If you dispatch parcels for multiple orders, we automatically redirect you to the dispatch form for the second order. In other cases, you will go to the
Summary
section in the dispatch form. There, you will see the summary of shipments you have just managed to order. In the table, we display:
tracking number of the ordered shipment
selected delivery option
sender's details
recipient's details.
When you hover over the
Sender
or
Recipient
field for a given shipment, you can see the full address.
You can now download the labels for the dispatched shipments.

You will see an error at the top of the form. Correct it and try to dispatch shipments again. If you want, you can remove the problematic shipments in bulk.

How to download the shipping label
You can download labels and consignment notes:
at once, when you finish the dispatching of shipments — in the following part of the dispatch form
later — in the
Ordered parcels
tab.
If you want to download the documents at once — after ordering the shipments, go to the
Summary
section in the dispatch form. Select the parcels and click [download documents] in the green bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.
What documents you can download
You can download:
only the labels
shipping labels with the summary —
check the details
only the consignment notes — if the selected carrier has made them available
all the documents (labels and consignment notes together)
DPD Poland and DHL are the carriers who have made consignment notes available within Allegro Standard. For parcels handled by other carriers, you can only download labels.
We generate the labels
in the format you select in the
Ship with Allegro settings
— you can download them as individual PDF, ZPL, or EPL files.
If you want to download:
all documents (labels and protocols together)
several labels in different formats,
you will download one ZIP file with all the selected documents.
How to order a courier pick-up
To order a courier pickup, select the parcels you want to dispatch with it. You can conveniently do it in bulk and order a courier pickup for multiple parcels at once:
Go to the
Shipments and pickups
tab and select
Ordered parcels
.
Select the chosen parcels and click [order pickup].
This will help you order as many pickups as you really need, and your pickup order will contain all the parcels the courier should collect.

If you select Allegro One same-day delivery, we will remind you to order the courier pick-up right away. This way, the parcel can reach the buyer on time.

If you want to order the courier pick-up at once — after ordering the shipments, go to the
Summary
section in the dispatch form. On the list, select the shipments for the courier to pick up from you, and click [next] at the bottom of the page.
We will direct you to the
Request pick up
section of the dispatch form.

If you dispatch the Poczta Polska and Packeta parcels through this tool and prepare them for shipment, you can hand them over at any Poczta Polska office. If you have a contract with Poczta Polska on scheduled arrivals, you can hand the parcels over to the courier.
No matter how you dispatch your Packeta parcels — Poczta Polska will forward them, and Packeta will deliver them to buyers abroad.

In the
Request pickup
section, select the date and time for the courier to pick up the parcels. We will also display the courier pickup address, which is set as the default address in the in Ship with Allegro address book. You can edit it if you want.
If at that moment you do not want to order a pick-up for any parcel, in the Pick-up date section, select
Without pick-up by courier
.
Once you select the correct options for all the shipments, click [request pick-up] to go to the summary.
You will find all the courier pickups ordered through Ship with Allegro in the last 180 days in the
Ordered pickups
section of the
Shipments and pickups
tab.
What you will see in the summary
From the
Summary
of ordered shipments, you can go back to the order list or the ordered shipments list.
In the
Summary
, you will see the pickup status for each selected parcel. You can also download the documents for the selected parcels again.
If you click [back to list of orders], you will terminate the dispatch process, and we will redirect you to the
Orders
tab.

Why can I not recall a parcel that has already been dispatched?
If a parcel you dispatched is already being processed or in transit, it cannot be recalled. You can wait for the parcel to be delivered to the buyer. If the buyer fails to collect it, the parcel will be returned to you.

In the case of parcels dispatched:
within your own agreement with the carrier — you can file a complaint to the carrier
without your own agreement with the carrier, based on our agreements —
you need our power of attorney
to file a complaint.

This happens in 3 situations:
if, in the
Delivery Settings
tab, you select the
buyer can enter their own delivery cost
option as the method for calculating delivery costs
if the offers in which the customer places an order do not share any delivery options
if the customer orders products from different offers that share only the
In-store pickup from seller
delivery option.
In that case, you have to agree on the delivery cost with the buyer.
Example:
Offer 1 has the following delivery options: Allegro Paczkomaty InPost and Allegro Kurier24 InPost.
Offer 2 has the following delivery options: Paczkomaty InPost and DPD Courier.
Those offers do not share any delivery options, so we will display the
Delivery and price to be agreed with seller
option.
If you do not want to receive orders with no delivery option and cost, enable at least one delivery option that will be shared by all your offers. That way, the buyer will not need to enter the delivery cost on their own.

If the buyer asks you to change the delivery address, you can do so only when dispatching the parcel. If you want to redirect a parcel to another address after dispatching it,
contact us

If you have already ordered the pickup, you cannot change its address.

Pickup Address
