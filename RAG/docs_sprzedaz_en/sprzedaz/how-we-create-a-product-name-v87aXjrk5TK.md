In the Allegro Product Catalog, we want to gather and describe all products available on Allegro. This way buyers can find exactly what they are looking for more easily. We display it:
on the product list
in the Other offers of product section.
Why you should complete parameters correctly in your offers
In some categories, we create the product name based on the offer parameter values.
If you complete parameters correctly, you have a tangible impact on the product name.
This way, information in your offers will be more useful to buyers.
What we take into account while creating a product name
A good product name should:
be written in lowercase
be grammatically correct (for example, have proper conjugation)
not contain punctuation marks or special characters
not contain the EAN.
What the rules of creating a product name are
The full product name in the Allegro Product Catalog contains:
fixed phrases related to the product category and the Type parameter
— we display them at the beginning of the name in the Catalog. The phrases depend on the category a given product is listed in and/or the parameters' values.

The product name:
Roof Ventilation Chimney
Krono-Plast KFP-2-1 fi 125 mm
This product is listed in the following category: Home and Garden > Construction Work and Equipment > Roofs >
Roof Ventilation Chimney
The fixed phrase:
roof ventilation chimney

The product name:
Smartphone
Xiaomi POCO X3 Pro 8 GB / 256 GB black
This product is listed in the following category: Phones and Accessories > Smartphones and Cell Phones > Xiaomi > POCO X3 Pro
The Type parameter:
Smartphone

— they are the same for all products in one category. Here, we use values from the select parameters of a product from the Catalog.

We are working to make the offer title and product name consistent and clear for the buyers. Within the Product Name catalog, we gather information on the product names used on Allegro and check whether they accurately describe products. That way, we select the most applicable name for a given product. This means that we will not always create the product name
based on the parameters
.
