If you sell
plastic single-use packaging, for example, food and beverage containers
—
you are obliged to charge an environmental fee
from end consumers.
The end consumer
is the one who buys the packaging alone or products packed in it for their own purposes and does not resell them.

The Act of April 14, 2023, on changes of the act on the obligations of entrepreneurs concerning the managing of specific waste and the product fee
— available in Polish.
The Regulation of December 7, 2023, of the Ministry of Climate and Environment on the fees for plastic single-use packaging
— available in Polish.

Check how you can add the environmental fee to your offers
You can sell your products both to the buyers who are end consumers and those who are not. That is why, you can list two offers with the same product:
For business customers
— without the environmental fee
. In the offer description, provide the information that the price does not include such a fee. If buyers order products for their own purposes (being end consumers), they should place an order in the offer for individual customers and ask for an invoice.
For individual customers — with the environmental fee added to the price
. In the offer description, provide the information that the price includes the fee.

In the offer description, you can provide information on the environmental fee and specify for whom the product is intended — it complies with
our Terms & Conditions
. If you provide a number or a link to another offer, you violate our rules and we have the right to remove such an offer.

Under
our Terms & Conditions
and the
price list
, we will charge the
sales commission
from the price that includes the environmental fee.
