Legal basis:
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 of the European Parliament and of the Council
.

Under the regulations, if you publish a photo generated or modified using artificial intelligence in a manner specified by the AI Act, you must inform recipients about it — so that they can tell whether the image or video is real after seeing it for the first time.
The new law does not ban the use of AI — it only imposes a transparency requirement, meaning you must label such content in specific situations
Which photos may be subject to the new requirements
These requirements apply to images generated or modified by AI that might appear authentic (so-called deepfakes).  These may include, for example:
photos generated from scratch — for example, when a product does not exist yet because it is made-to-order.
photos of a real product with an AI-generated background
virtual models — AI-generated characters presenting the product.
What it means for you
