From August 19, we will no longer be adding the
GREAT PRICE
badge to offers. We will focus on developing a single pricing program —
Best price guarantee
.
You can be awarded the GREAT PRICE badge in offers with the lowest prices only on allegro.pl. Under the Best price guarantee program, we additionally compare prices of products in your offers with prices in selected online stores.
Why we are doing it
Our research shows that two similar badges are confusing for buyers. They do not see the difference between GREAT PRICE and the Best price guarantee — they believe both highlight offers with the lowest price exclusively on allegro.pl.
Best price guarantee brings customers concrete benefits. This badge assures them that the offer on allegro.pl has both high quality and the lowest price among selected online stores — which means they do not need to look for the product anywhere else. The GREAT PRICE badge does not guarantee that. Additionally, offers with this badge are often not price-competitive on the market.
Our primary goal is to provide buyers with the most attractive prices and thereby build their trust in the Best price guarantee program.  This can translate into higher sales for your offers with this badge.
What will happen with offers with the GREAT PRICE badge
On August 19, we will stop displaying this badge in offers. If you want to increase your offer visibility:
check
how your offers can get the Best price guarantee badge
and
learn what the Top Offers are and how to make your offers represent products
.
