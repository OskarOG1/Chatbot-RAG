Basic fees
We charge the basic fee for each product unit we handle within the service. The fee includes:
accepting the product for storage
picking and packing orders
environmentally friendly packaging, filling and protection
dealing with customer queries regarding orders.
We charge the basic fee the moment we dispatch products from the Allegro Warehouse to buyers.
The basic fee depends on the product volume. We calculate it based on the three longest product dimensions: width x length x height. We do it when we accept the product for storage at the warehouse for the first time.
We use the above values to determine the product's price list category — from A to F.

Discrepancies upon accepting products for storage at the Allegro Warehouse
There are three types of fees for discrepancies:
related to quality
— they apply to products sent to the Allegro Warehouse but not declared in the ASN.
Example
: the seller declares 5 flower pots in the ASN, but delivers 5 pens instead.
related to quantity
— they apply to products sent to the Allegro Warehouse in a quantity greater than that declared in the ASN. We do not charge a fee for a lower number of product units.
Example
: the seller declares 30 sweaters in the ASN, but sends 55 instead.
related to non-compliance with Terms & Conditions
— they apply to products sent to the Allegro Warehouse that we cannot accept for storage under the Terms & Conditions.
Example
: the sellers sends products that exceed the maximum permitted volume.

Product withdrawal
A fee for withdrawing products from the Allegro Warehouse is

. 
We add shipping costs to the fee — based on an invoice from the carrier. Shipping costs depend on the carrier's price list and the weight, volume, type, and value (in the case of insured parcels) of the products we send back to you.
We will charge the fee on the first Monday after sending the products to you. If Monday is a holiday, we will charge it the next business day.
