You can offer business customers discounts on your entire assortment. You can set create the order discount in the
Order discount
tab or through the Allegro API.

You do not bear any additional costs.

What the order discount is and how it works
With the
order discount
you can set discounts on your entire assortment within one cart if the buyer makes a purchase worth at least an amount you specified.
Within the order discount, you can set any number of thresholds at which the discount applies. The lowest threshold you can set is 2%

You give a 5% discount for a single order worth 10,000 PLN. For an order worth 25,000 PLN ― you give a 7% discount. For an order worth 50,000 PLN ― a 10% discount, and so on.

We add a discount in the buyer’s cart.
The order discount
does not include offers in which you have set a
wholesale discount
— a discount on a specific number of the selected product's units. Thanks to that, you can be sure the buyer will not get a double discount.

You have set the
order discount
for orders worth 6,000 PLN or more.
A buyer adds two products from your offers to the cart:
a scooter for 5,000 PLN
a bike for 2,000 PLN — in that offer, you have also set a
wholesale discount
that reduces the price for the purchase of at least 4 units.
The total cart value is over 6,000 PLN. However, the order discount only includes the scooter's value — 5,000 PLN — so less than the discount threshold. The buyer will not get a discount.

Who may benefit from the discount
Only
the sellers on their business accounts may offer the order discount.
Only
the business customers ― buyers signed in to their business accounts ― may benefit from the discounts.
How we display to buyers information about the discount
We display information about the discount:
on the store page
when the buyer adds the product to cart
in the seller's offer.
How to set the order discount for business customers
Open the
Large order discount
tab.
Click [add  discount].
In the
amount from
field, enter the threshold value for a buyer to be eligible for a discount. In the
discount
field, enter the rate of this discount.
Click [save the discounts]. Done!
