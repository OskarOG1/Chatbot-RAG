What pre-made shipping price lists are
They are a simplified way of creating
shipping price lists
in a few easy steps. All you need to do is specify the country of dispatch and sale, and select the template. We will automatically complete the remaining information:
we will create a shipping price list containing appropriate delivery options, for which we will always automatically set
maximum delivery prices
if we make any changes to the delivery options, we will update the shipping price list automatically — based on the setting you choose (country of dispatch, assortment type).
The benefits of pre-made shipping price lists
Your shipping price lists are always Smart!
— the pre-made shipping price lists always meet the Allegro Smart! conditions in every marketplace.
Your delivery rates are always up-to-date
— in the shipping price lists, we automatically set the maximum delivery prices. If the rates change — we update them.
You always offer the latest delivery options
— if we enable a new delivery option that meets the conditions of your shipping price list, we automatically add it.

How to create pre-made shipping price lists from scratch
Go to the
Delivery Settings
tab.
Click [add a new shipping price list] in the Shipping price lists section.
Select the country from which you will dispatch parcels. You can only choose one country of dispatch. If you want to dispatch products from different countries — create separate shipping price lists for each of them.
At this stage, you can also select
email delivery
.

We will redirect you to the current shipping price list view if you:
select a country of dispatch other than the one you provided upon account registration
select email delivery
select a non-standard shipping price list.

Your offers will always be visible in your local marketplace — the one in which you registered your account.
Decide whether you also want to sell abroad:
in the International markets section — select other Allegro marketplaces
in the Other countries section — select up to 20 other European countries.
If you select a country of dispatch other than the one you provided upon account registration — we will redirect you to the non-standard shipping price list form.

Select the type of the shipping price list depending on your assortment.
Small assortment
— select this type if you want to dispatch parcels:
with a courier
to parcel lockers
to pick-up points.
Medium assortment
— select this type if you want to dispatch standard parcels using courier delivery options.
Large assortment

oversized parcels

The dimensions and weight provided for each type represent the

How to edit the pre-made shipping price lists
Go to the
Shipping price lists
section in the
Delivery Settings
tab.
Look up the shipping price list you want to edit and click [edit].
We will redirect you to the Settings tab.
Enter all changes and go to the Summary.
Save changes. Done!
How to add the pre-made shipping price lists to offers
You can add the pre-made shipping price lists to new and active offers as usual. Read
our article to learn more
.
How to remove the pre-made shipping price lists
Go to the
Delivery Settings
tab and look up the shipping price list you want to remove.
Click the three dots on the right and select [remove shipping price list].
Click [remove]. Done!

Once you remove a given shipping price list, we will not be able to restore it.

If you added a shipping price list to offers
If some offers are assigned to the shipping price list you want to remove — select another shipping price list to replace it and click [remove]. You can replace a shipping price list only with one that contains delivery options to the same countries.
See what to do if you
added a shipping price list to more than 1,000 offers
.
Linking offers with another shipping price list may take up to several hours.

Example 1:
You want to remove a shipping price list that you added to offers. It contains delivery options to Poland, Czechia, and Slovakia, which means your offers are available in foreign marketplaces. If you want to replace that shipping price list, we will show you only the other ones that also contain delivery options to Poland, Czechia, and Slovakia.
Example 2:
You want to remove a shipping price list that you added to offers. It contains only delivery options within Poland. If you want to replace that shipping price list, we will show you only the other ones that also contain delivery options within Poland.
