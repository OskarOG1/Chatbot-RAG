Thanks to shipping price lists, you do not have to complete delivery details in each offer. You just need to create a price list that you will later easily and quickly add to a selected offer.
You can create up to 250 price lists and add them to offers based on the assortment. Name each price list to make it easier to distinguish them. Remember also to set up the way you calculate delivery costs for offers with different shipping price lists. That way, your customer will see the correct cost when making the purchase.
What price lists you can create
You can choose between two price list types: pre-made and custom.
When to select the pre-made shipping price list
If you want us to automatically complete information in your price lists and keep them up to date.
Learn about the benefits:
Your price lists will always be Smart!
— pre-made shipping price lists always meet the Allegro Smart! conditions in every marketplace.
Your delivery rates will always be up to date
— in the shipping price lists, we automatically set the maximum delivery prices. If the rates change — we update them.
Your delivery options will always be up to date
— if we enable new delivery options that meet the conditions of your shipping price list, we automatically add them.
When to select a custom shipping price list
If you want to manage the price list and update it yourself.
If you want to set delivery prices yourself or offer buyers free delivery at your expense — outside of the Allegro Smart! program.
If you want to offer buyers delivery options that you
settle directly with a carrier
.
If you offer
email delivery
or dispatch heavy and oversized products.
If you want to dispatch from a country other than your registration marketplace.

How to create a new shipping price list based on an existing one
You can add new shipping price lists with the [create similar] option. That way, you do not need to add all delivery options to the new shipping price list from scratch.
How it works
In
Delivery Settings
, find the shipping price list you want to use to create a new one.
Click the three dots on the right and select [create similar].
Name the new shipping price list and edit the details according to your needs.
Click [save]. Done! Now you can add the shipping price list to your offers.
How to set the delivery cost in different currencies
If you have a
business account
on Allegro, you can sell both on allegro.pl and in foreign marketplaces.
Depending on the dispatch and delivery place you choose, you will see appropriate delivery options in your shipping price lists. You set the delivery cost in a given currency. If the delivery option is available in more than one marketplace, you need to complete prices in each currency separately.
If you want to set the delivery price in two currencies, you can use the Currency calculator. Thanks to it, you will calculate the delivery cost of a given option in different currencies.
Click [set the price] to set the recalculated price in your shipping price lists. It is possible if the recalculated price is not higher than the maximum price you can set in the price lists for a given delivery option.
We publish the data in the Currency calculator based on the European Central Bank's current rate.

How to remove a shipping price list template
If you want to remove a shipping price list template that you do not use anymore:
Go to the
Delivery Settings
tab and find the price list you want to remove.
Click the three dots on the right and select [remove price list].
Click [remove]. Done!

Once you remove a given shipping price list, we will not be able to restore it.

If there are offers linked to the shipping price list you are trying to remove — first, you need to select another price list that will replace the old one, and then click [remove]. We can replace a given shipping price list only with one that contains delivery options to the same countries.
Example 1:
You are trying to remove a shipping price list linked to offers. That shipping price list contains delivery options to Poland, Czechia, and Slovakia. It means that your offers are available in foreign marketplaces. To replace that shipping price list — we will only show you your other shipping price lists that contain delivery options to Poland, Czechia, and Slovakia.
Example 2:
You are trying to remove a shipping price list linked to offers. It contains only delivery options within Poland.  To replace that shipping price list — we will only show you your other shipping price lists that contain delivery options to Poland.
Linking offers to another shipping price list can take up to several hours.

When you create a price list, you select the delivery options you want to make available to buyers. Then, you complete the following fields:
First unit — the parcel fee that will be charged to the buyer when they buy at least 1 unit.
Maximum quantity — the number of units you can ship within one parcel. In this field, enter the number of units you are actually able to pack within one parcel.
Surcharge for subsequent unit — the amount that we will add for each subsequent product shipped within the same parcel.
Examples
The delivery fee for the first unit is 10 PLN, you can ship up to 5 units within one parcel, and you set a surcharge of 1 PLN — in that case, when the buyer orders:
1 unit — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN
2 units — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN + 1 PLN = 11 PLN
3 units — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN = 12 PLN
5 units — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN = 14 PLN
6 units — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 10 PLN (another parcel) = 24 PLN
7 units — the delivery fee will be 10 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 1 PLN + 10 PLN (another parcel) + 1 PLN = 25 PLN.
The delivery fee for the first unit is 10 PLN, you can ship up to 5 units within one parcel, and you set a surcharge of 0 PLN — in that case, when the buyer orders:
1–5 units — the delivery cost is 10 PLN
6–10 units — the delivery cost is 20 PLN
11–15 units — the delivery cost is 30 PLN.

The maximum parcel weight is another value we take into account when determining the delivery cost. In the price lists, you can set the weight to a value ranging from 5 kg to 700 kg. You can do it in the
Delivery Settings
tab or through the
API
.
It is up to you whether you want to provide the maximum parcel weight.
You can set a maximum parcel weight for the following delivery options:
Allegro DPD Courier
Allegro DPD Courier payment on delivery
Allegro DPD Pickup
Allegro DPD Pickup payment on delivery.
When you set both the
maximum quantity
and the
maximum parcel weight
, we will take them into account. If an order exceeds one of those values, we will charge the buyer another parcel costs.
We take into account the maximum parcel weight in the delivery cost only if you complete the
Weight (with packaging)
parameter in a given offer. If you do not do it, we will calculate the delivery cost based on the maximum number of units in a parcel alone.
Example:
You sell dog food, whose weight with packaging is 2 kg. In the delivery price list, you set the following parameters for the Allegro DPD courier delivery option:
the price for the first unit in a parcel — 12.99 PLN
the maximum number of units in a parcel — 4
the maximum parcel weight — 5 kg.
The customer buys 3 units of dog food from the offer, which together weigh 6 kg. The order exceeds the maximum parcel weight you have set as 5 kg. The customer pays 25.98 (2 x 12.99 PLN) for the delivery of that order. We charge them for another parcel — despite the fact that the maximum number of units in a parcel is 4.

Combining price lists and delivery costs allows the automatic calculation of the delivery cost if the customer buys in a few of your offers. We will combine them if in all of the offers from which the customer buys products, you enable at least one common delivery option:
Combining costs
: if there is a common delivery option, but not all of its parameters are the same in all of the offers, we calculate delivery costs for that option according to the seller's settings.
Combining price lists
: if all of the parameters of the common delivery option are the same, we consider that all of the offers have the same price list.
Make sure that you enable at least one common delivery option in all of the offers and set the rule of calculating delivery costs for various price lists. As a result, the customer will see proper delivery costs upon every purchase.
