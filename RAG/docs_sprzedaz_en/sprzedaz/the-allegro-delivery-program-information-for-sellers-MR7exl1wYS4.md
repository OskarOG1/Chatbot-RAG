Within
Allegro Delivery,
we are responsible for the entire delivery process and take comprehensive care of its speed and quality. You are not responsible for the transport of the parcel or the consequences of its possible loss or destruction. How we determine parcel weight
We compare its actual and dimensional weight. We consider the higher value.
Actual weight
— how much the parcel weighs when placed on a scale.
Dimensional weight
— depends on the parcel's dimensions.
We calculate it with the following formula: [height (cm) x length (cm) x width (cm)] / 6,000.
If a parcel exceeds the maximum weight or dimensions — we will add a surcharge according to the
price list

Allegro Delivery parcel return
The parcel may be returned to you if the customer does not collect it. In that case, we will not charge you for the return, and the parcel will be sent to the address you provided in the account settings.

If we charge an extra fee the moment we deliver the parcel to the buyer, we will also add it when it is returned to you. It does not apply only to payment of delivery.

How to file a complaint about an Allegro Delivery parcel
You can file a complaint about
every parcel
within Allegro Delivery through the complaint form in the
Shipments and pickups
tab. Select the order you want to file a complaint about, open the order details and click [complain about delivery].
This way, your complaint will be immediately linked to the order it concerns, and handled more quickly.
The form will guide you through filing a complaint and suggest what information you need to include.
If you no longer have an Allegro account or cannot sign in, use this
complaint form

your details

mailing address

the list of attached documents

damage report

No matter how you file the complaint, you will receive a confirmation email once it reaches us. We will update you via email about its status. If you want to ask additional questions, click [contact us] under this article.
We usually address complaints within 7 days. However, sometimes it may take up to 30 days. We may also ask you to provide additional documents or information.
For accepted complaints, we usually pay out claims within 3 working days.
You can appeal our decision. Send an appeal as a reply to our email with the complaint decision within 14 days of its receipt.
For what reasons you can file a complaint about the service
You can do it if:
the parcel is lost (we consider it lost if it has not been delivered within 30 days from the date of dispatch).
we charge you additional fees you have not agreed for.
the parcel is damaged in transit.
we provide the service incorrectly.
we do not provide the service on time (if the buyer collects the parcel, they can file a complaint due to delays).
Who can file a complaint and when

A complaint regarding a parcel can be filed by its sender or recipient.
The sender can do it in every case.
The recipient can file a complaint when:
the parcel was successfully delivered — they picked it up from a courier, pick-up point, or parcel locker
the sender transfers the right to pursue claims to the recipient.

As a seller, you can file a complaint against the service up to 12 months from dispatching the parcel.
If, upon parcel receipt, the buyer finds a defect or damage unnoticeable on the outside — they have 7 days to file a complaint about such a parcel. In other cases, they can file a complaint against the service up to 12 months after dispatching the parcel.
