The
My Assortment
tab is your hub for offer management, in which you can:
look up specific offers
— using the search function, filters, or sorting
quickly change the price and number of units
— you can edit them directly on the list of offers in this tab
find the most important information about your offers
— such as price, unit number, basic statistics, or status
bulk edit sales policy and complete important details about a product
, such as dispatch time or safety information
share your offers in foreign marketplaces
— you can easily share offers in all or selected marketplaces by bulk editing a shipping price list
manage the offer's visibility
— you can relist or terminate offers, and exclude or restore them in the foreign marketplaces
check the offer's details
— in them, you can find information like fees or the offer's participation in
campaigns and programs
use promotion options
— you can enable Features or Flexible Features in offers
go to the buyer's view of an offer and other key tabs
, such as the listing form
manage offers within the
One Fulfillment
service
— you can look up offers that we handle within One Fulfillment, edit them, or convert a regular offer into a One Fulfillment offer.

We display more detailed statuses of offers shared in additional marketplaces. You can filter offers by them:
all
for sharing
— an offer that does not have a delivery option to the given marketplace in the price list. Add a relevant option or change the price list to make it available.
submitted for sharing
— an offer that has been submitted for sharing but is not active in your registration marketplace. If you want this offer to be visible in a foreign marketplace, list it on allegro.pl
sharing in progress
— an offer that has been submitted for sharing and we are currently checking if it meets all the conditions of displaying it in the given marketplace
shared
— an offer that we are currently displaying in the given marketplace
rejected by Allegro
— an offer that has been submitted for sharing, but you must correct it so that we can display it in the given marketplace
excluded by you
— an offer that you have withdrawn from the given marketplace.

How to see the offer from the buyer's perspective
Click the offer title — we will display your offer's page that the buyer will see in a separate tab. This feature allows you to see what your offer exactly looks like and improve it to boost its attractiveness.
How to edit offers in the My Assortment tab
You can manage your offers either individually or in bulk. You can implement most changes in bulk, which allows you to adjust your offers much more quickly. You can edit up to 1,000 offers at the same time.

Some editing options may differ between One Fulfillment offers and regular ones due to the characteristics of One Fulfillment by Allegro. For example, in One Fulfillment, you cannot change the number of units — we update it regularly in accordance with the inventory.

How to edit offers in bulk
When you select at least 1 offer from the list, you will see a green bar with options at the bottom of the page. They will allow you to manage your offers in bulk, and edit things like:
availability
— list, relist, and terminate offers, as well as restore them in the foreign marketplaces or exclude them from there
sales policy
— basic ones, such as price, unit number, dispatch time, and shipping price list, and the other ones, for example, complaints terms, warranty information, invoicing options, VAT rates, offer tags
product
— such as size charts, manufacturer details, safety information
promotion
— manage features.
To edit offers in bulk, for example, the price:
Check selected offers.
On the bulk edit bar, select the desired option — in this case,
Sales policy
and then
Price
.
Complete the information on the overlay and click [save].

How to create a label and add it to offers
Select the relevant offers.
Hover the cursor over the label icon and select
Edit labels
. Enter a name for the new label and select one of the available colors. Click [add].
Hover over the label icon and select
Mark offers
.
Done! The label you have created is available as a filter.
You can also see the created label in the offer details. Click [more] next to the selected offer to see its details.
How to edit a label
When you click [edit labels], you will see a list of your labels. To change a label's name or color, click [edit] next to it.
The changes you introduce will appear in all offers to which you have added the chosen label.
