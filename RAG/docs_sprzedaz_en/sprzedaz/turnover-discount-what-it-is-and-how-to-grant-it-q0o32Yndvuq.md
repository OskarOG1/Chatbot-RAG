You can use the turnover discount to set discounts on your entire assortment
for business customers on business.allegro.cz
. This means you can only add it to the offers that
qualify for sharing
on Allegro Business in Czechia, and it will only be visible to Czech business customers. This discount will not be available to buyers on allegro.pl, allegro.sk, or allegro.hu.
How the turnover discount works
You set this discount for your entire assortment. You decide on the
discount percentage
and the
purchase threshold
— that is,
how many Czech korunas
the buyer needs to spend on your products in a month to qualify.
If you set the discount and the buyer spends the specified amount at your store the following month — they will be able to purchase products from you at discounted prices in the month after that.
If the buyer reaches the required threshold again, they will be able to benefit from the discount again in the following month — and so on.

On March 5, you set a 5% discount for buyers who make purchases for at least 4,000 CZK (but no more than 60,000 CZK). Throughout April, a buyer spends a total of 4,000 CZK on purchases from you. Between May 1 and May 30, the buyer will have a 5% discount on your entire assortment.

The discount will be applied in the cart. It cannot be combined with the wholesale discount for businesses. If a particular offer meets the conditions for both the turnover discount and the wholesale discount — only the wholesale discount will apply.
Who can benefit from this discount
Only sellers with a business account whose offers we share on allegro.cz can set the turnover discount.
Only entrepreneurs on business.allegro.cz — that is, Czech customers with a business account — can benefit from the turnover discount.
Where buyers will see the discount information
We display the information about the discount:
on the product list
when the buyer adds a product to cart
in the seller's offer.

The discount badge is
free of charge
. You do not bear any additional costs for using the badge.

How to set a turnover discount
Go to the
Turnover Discount
tab.
Click [add turnover discount].
Specify the spending threshold — between 4,000 and 60,000 CZK, and the discount percentage — between 2% and 50%.
Click [next].
We will display a summary. At this point, you can go back to make changes. If everything is correct, click [save discount].
The discount always takes effect on the first calendar day of the following month — you cannot change it.
How to edit or disable the turnover discount
Go to the
Turnover Discount
tab.
Click [edit] or [remove] under a given discount. If you edit or remove the discount, it will still be applicable until the end of the monthly cycle.
