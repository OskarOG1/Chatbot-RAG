On August 6
, we will end the
discount coupons program for buyers
.
From May 4, you will not be able to create
new coupon campaigns or add new discount coupons to your offers. Until this time, you can still create new coupon campaigns in your offers.
All coupon campaigns created before May 4 will end according to your planned schedule — after 90 days at most.
How you can attract customers to your offers
Allegro customers love Smart! Coins, and you can create
coin campaigns
. Customers will be able to get extra Smart! Coins for purchases in your offers.
Learn more about
coin campaigns
. See also
how you can promote your offers with Smart! Coins
.
