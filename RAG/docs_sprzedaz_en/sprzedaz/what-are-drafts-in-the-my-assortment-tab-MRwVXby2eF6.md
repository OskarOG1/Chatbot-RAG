titleIf you close the page while listing an offer, you will not lose the data you entered – we will save it as a draft.
All you have to do to create a draft is enter the offer name and select a category in the
listing form
. The drafts are autosaved – every time you introduce a change while listing, the draft is updated.

You
cannot
transfer saved drafts to other accounts, even if you use
linked accounts
.

In order to continue listing the offer, open the
listing form
again and select a draft.
You can find your drafts in the
My Assortment
tab. The drafts are available on every device and every browser after you sign in to your account.
The draft will disappear when you finish listing the offer. If you need to list more offers based on this draft – go to the offer in the
My Assortment
tab and click Wystaw podobną (List similar).

We store drafts up to 120 days from the last edit. That means if you do not edit a draft for 120 days, it will be deleted automatically. You can edit, for example, the title, the category, the product, the description, or the image.
We will not store a draft longer if the only thing you edited is the number of units in an offer
.
