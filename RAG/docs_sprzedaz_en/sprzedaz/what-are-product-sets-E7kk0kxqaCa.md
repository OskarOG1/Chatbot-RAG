A product set can consist of:
multiple units of the same product – in that case, it is called a
multipack
different products.
A customer can only buy the entire product set.
You can manage your product sets in the
My Assortment
tab. To search for them, use the
sets in offers
filter.

A flexible bundle consists of various offers. A customer can buy the entire bundle or choose a product and different offers from the same category. You can read about flexible bundles in
this article
.

How to list multipacks (multiple units of the same product)
If you want to sell
multiple units of the same product within one offer
, use a
multipack
.
Go to the
listing form
.
In the product search, select the product you want to sell.
Decide how you want to handle the selected product – on your own or through One Fulfillment.
Click [complete the form].
In the
Products in offer
section, select
Multiple units in a set
.
Complete the number of units in a set.
Complete the remaining fields of the form. Finally, click [list product]. We will mark your offer as a
multipack
.
How to list sets of different products
If you want to sell
different products within one offer
– go to the
Products in offer
section of the
listing form
and select
Single unit
. The first product you select will be your
main product
.
Complete parameters and click [add another product] to create a set. We will display the
Add another product to the bundle
field. Look it up by its name or code.
When you find the right product, click [add to bundle].
You can add another product or – if your set is ready – click [continue with X products] and complete the form.

You can determine the
Condition
parameter only for the
main product
. We will automatically set the same parameter value for all other products in the set. You cannot edit it. What is more, you can complete parameters only for the main product. We will not display the parameters of additional products in the set.

How we display the product sets to buyers
On the search result list
Buyers can use the
Bundles
filter. When they select
show only bundles
, we will display available product sets (multipacks and sets of different products) on the search result list. We mark product sets as
Set of X units
.
We group the same multipacks on the list and show only 1 multipack. That way, buyers can make their choice more easily. At the same time, your customers can quickly access the list of all offers with the same multipack and compare them. All they need to do is click the [see X offers] button next to a single offer or a multipack – then, we will display only a list of single offers or multipacks.
We group
sets of different products
on the list by the
main product
in the set.
On the offer page
On the offer page, in the
Multipack
section, we display offers with multiple units of the same product. In the section, we display available options:
a single product, including its price
a maximum of 3 multipacks, including the price and the percentage discount compared to the price of a single product.
We display multipacks on the offer page
only if it is cheaper or price-neutral for the buyer to buy more units
of the same product. It means that if the multipack is more expensive (per unit) than a single product currently being viewed — we will not display it.
This rule applies to both your multipacks and those of other sellers
.
We will display multipacks from the same seller first.

Prices and percentage discounts in the Multipack section do not include the deposit.  We display the deposit amount as before — right below the product price.

Buyers viewing your offer will see all possible multipacks for that product that meet the display criteria. If you do not have them in your assortment, we will display other sellers' multipacks that meet the display criteria in your offer. When a buyer clicks on such a multipack, we will redirect them to the product page of the other seller.

We can automatically mark such active offers with the buy now option as product sets. We will do it only if you list offers in selected categories and we are sure you list multiple units of the same product. We will let you know about any of such changes by email.
Product sets allow buyers to buy from you quicker and more easily. They can see right away that the offer is a set and the number of units you are selling.
