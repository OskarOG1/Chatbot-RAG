With such an account, you can sell and buy products you are interested in. You can also rate a seller and a product. Your transactions are safe and legal. Junior account protects you from consequences of unintentionally breaking the law — for example, by buying or selling a product that is not intended for minors.
Once you turn 18, we will convert your account into a regular one.
How to register a Junior account
If you are at least 13 years old, but not 18 yet, make sure that your parents (or legal guardians) agree to your Allegro account.
In the
registration form
, check the box that you are not 18 yet and enter your birth date. Your account will get the Junior status.
What restrictions apply to a Junior account
You can use the basic Allegro functionalities but
before you turn 18, your access to some services will be limited
.
Junior account has a limit of 50 PLN for listing fees
. If you exceed this amount, pay your bill in the Settlements with Allegro tab to continue listing offers.
For safety reasons, as a Junior account holder, you cannot:
share an account with your spouse
become a Super Seller
take advantage of installments
buy or sell in the following categories:
Real Estate
Erotica
Cars
Motorcycles and Quads
Other Vehicles and Boats
Tobacco Accessories
Weapons
Air Guns
Stun Guns
Pepper Sprays
Fireworks
OTC Medication
Investment Products
Collectible Gold Coins
Non-Prescription Veterinary Drugs
Alcohol
Alcohol-free
Alcohol Accessories
Energy Drinks.
We will lift those restrictions after converting your account into a regular one. We will do it when you turn 18.
