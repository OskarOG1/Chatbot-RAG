When you list an offer and, for the Condition parameter, you:
select New — after activating the offer, you will not be able to change this value
select a value other than New — after activating the offer, you can change this value to any other value except New.

What values can the Condition parameter have
Below, you will find the simplified definitions. You can read their full versions in the
Terms & Conditions
.
New
— this value describes a brand new product that is complete, free from any faults or defects, and has not been used, opened, or repackaged. Such a product must be in its original packaging that has not been opened or damaged, with intact security features or manufacturer's designation (such as seals on boxes or tags on clothes). Such a product must not have any traces of use.
New with defects
— this value describes a brand new, complete, and unused product. It may have minor visual defects (such as scratches, dents, or minimal damage) that do not affect its functionality. A product described as New with defects should be in its original packaging, with intact manufacturer's designation — such as seals on boxes or tags on clothes. The packaging may have visual defects (for example, be dented or damaged).
Used
— this value describes a product that has been used. The product may have traces of use, but it is fully functional and works as intended. A product described as Used may lack some parts or accessories — for example, screws (in the case of furniture) or a headset (in the case of a phone).
Retreaded
— this value is used to describe only the condition of tires. It applies to tires that have been regenerated — by replacing the worn tread and applying a new layer of tread. Such tires are not damaged or more than 5 years old. They must be checked using pressure tests.
Certified refurbished
— this value describes a product with minimal or no traces of use. The product has been professionally checked, cleaned, and refurbished by the manufacturer or distributor and complies with the requirements described in the specifications. The product is in a new or replacement packaging and has original or new accessories.
Seller refurbished
,
Remanufactured
, or
Refurbished
— each value describes a product that has been restored to a usable condition. The product has been checked, cleaned , repaired, and is fully functional. It has minimal or no traces of use. The product may (but does not have to) be sold in the original packaging. It has original or new accessories.
Ex-display
— this value describes a product that has been displayed in a store. Such a product is complete, with possible minor traces of use, but fully functional and working as intended. It does not have to be sold in its original packaging.
Repacked
— this value describes a brand new, complete, and unused product, free from any faults or defects (such as scratches, dents, or minimal damage), that has been repackaged.
New without tags
— this value describes a brand new, complete, and unused product, free from any faults or defects. Such a product does not have the manufacturer's tag.
After returns
— this value describes a product that has been sold before and returned. Such a product is complete and may only have slight traces of repackaging or use. It does not have to be sold in its original packaging. It can be sold in replacement packaging or unsealed original packaging.
For parts
or
Not working
— this value describes a product or its element that may not work as intended and is not fully functional. This value applies to products with defects, requiring repairs, or missing essential parts.
Incomplete set/Incomplete kit
— this value describes a product that does not have all the elements included in the original set or kit. Such a product has minimal or no traces of use. It does not have to be sold in its original packaging.
Salvaged/Disassembled
— this value describes a product that has minimal traces of use — related to, among other things, assembly, transport, or preparation for sale. Such a product may only have minor traces resulting from disassembly. It does not have to be sold in its original packaging.
Needs refurbishment
— this value describes a vintage product with apparent traces of use or aging of material that can be removed or repaired. The product's appearance does not impact its functioning. A product with this value does not have to be in its original packaging.
Does not need refurbishment
— this value describes a vintage product that does not have apparent traces of use or aging of the material. Such a product has not been refurbished, nor does it need to be — its appearance does not differ noticeably from the original condition. The product is functional and can be used as intended. It does not have to be in its original packaging.

Parameter values may vary depending on the category in which you list your offer.
