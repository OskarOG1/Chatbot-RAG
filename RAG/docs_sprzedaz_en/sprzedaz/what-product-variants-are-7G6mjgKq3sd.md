Which offers we display product variants in
We display them
in offers with products for which we have product variants in our Allegro Product Catalog
.

What if you list an offer in which we display product variants
If other sellers have a product variant you lack — we may display it in your offer.
If you sell a product variant other sellers lack — we may display it in their offers. The more variants of a given product you have, the more likely we are to display them in the offers of other sellers.
Featuring offers will work as usual — your featured offer will be high up on the list of offers. When buyers open it, they will also see product variants from other sellers — if they offer a product variant you do not sell.
How buyers see product variants
Buyers see all available product variants on your offer page. If you do not have them in your assortment, we display other sellers' variants in your offer.
When the buyer switches from your offer to the product variant offered by another seller, we display the following message:
The seller and the offer terms have changed for the selected variant
.
Buyers can decide whether they want to see other sellers' product variants in your offers. We will display the
Show variants from other sellers
option above product variants in the offer. Once they disable it, they will see only your products.
How we combine variants
If a given product is available in several size variants, we show one variant for each color on the product list. It includes all available sizes of this product. We do not display a single Size parameter value, but a range of sizes. 
We display all available product variants when the buyer clicks [compare X offers]. Then, we show the list of all offers linked to this product, in all sizes.

You sell shoes of a popular brand in several sizes and the following colors: black, white, and red. On the product list, we display the color variants of this product — black, white, and red — separately. We provide the range of sizes: 36–41. The buyer clicks [compare 70 offers] next to the selected color variant and sees all offers with shoes available in sizes from this range.

How you can improve your product variants
In the
My Assortment
tab, you can check the
product variants
for each offer and suggest changes to them. This way, you can improve the quality of product variants and the entire Allegro Product Catalog.
You can suggest changes when you want to:
extend the variant group with the additional products that you offer
indicate duplicated variants in a group
report incorrect variants in a group.
If your offer does not include any product variants, you can suggest them to us.
You can find detailed information about the tool that serves for suggesting product variants in a
dedicated article
.
