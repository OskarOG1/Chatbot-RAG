In the Allegro Product Catalog, we gather all products available on Allegro along with their edetails — such as parameters, images, or descriptions. This way, buyers can quickly find the product they ar searching for and easily compare all the offers with that product.
We regularly verify the links between offers and products to ensure their high quality. This allows us to provide buyers with clear and trustworthy information.

According to
Appendix 10
to our Terms & Conditions — as the seller, you are fully responsible for correctly linking your offer to a product in the Allegro Product Catalog.

We want buyers to be sure that your offer matches the exact product they are looking for. If your offers are incorrectly linked to a product from the Catalog, we can unlink them. We will then end your offer automatically. We will let you know about it by email.
We can unlink your offer from the Allegro Product Catalog if, among other things:
the offer title indicates a different product than the one presented
the basic parameters you provided in your offer differ from the product's details in the Catalog. You can check them in the Basic parameters section of the listing form.
the photos of the goods in the offer differ from the photos of the product
you provided a different number of units in the offer than in the product
you offer the goods in a color version not included for that product.
We can also take into account:
GTIN (EAN, ISBN, ISSN, or others) — a unique number that indicates the product itself
and not its owner
. This way, the same product can be linked to offers from different sellers.
information on whether the product's and offer's categories are in a
set of similar categories
.

Upon entering the Product proposal, the User shall lose the ability to edit the data and content added to the Product specifications card. The User may, however, report to the Company any errors and mistakes that took place while entering the Product proposal, using the relevant functionalities on Allegro."
