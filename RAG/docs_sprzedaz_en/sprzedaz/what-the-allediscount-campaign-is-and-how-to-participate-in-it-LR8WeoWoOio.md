AlleDiscount is a recurring campaign within which you can offer select products at discounted prices. When we start accepting submissions for a given edition, we send you a notification on your Allegro account. Click the bell icon in the top right corner of the page to read it.
We will inform you about the details of the promotional campaigns in the
News
.
How the AlleDiscount campaign works
You submit selected products from the
List of Goods
for the campaign.
We lower the prices of those products in your offers automatically.
Your offers are marked with the badge, for example, Allegro Days. Their lowest price from the last 30 days before the discount is also crossed out.
The customer buys the product at a discounted price.
The customer pays you the reduced price — we pay you reconciliation up to the product price from before the automatic reduction.
You do not bear any additional costs —
you can participate for free
.
What you can gain
More attractive prices in your offers ― without additional costs or losses in profit.
Offers positioned higher in the search results — when buyers sort by price: lowest first.
Higher sales ― buyers often choose a given offer because of the lower price.
Your offers have higher chances of being granted special badges — for example,
Best price guarantee
or
GREAT PRICE
.
Who can participate in the campaign
You can participate in the campaign if you:
have an active business account
have achieved
a sales quality rating
at least at the Neutral level
have set payouts
have paid all invoices from us
.
Your submission means that you accept the Terms & Conditions of the campaign.

You can check them
here
.

What the List of Goods is
For each edition of the AlleDiscount campaign, we will publish a new List of Goods that you can submit. You will find it on
this
page.
In the List of Goods, you can also find:
Maximum Price
for each product  — that is the highest price that your product can have on submitting it to the program
Minimum Discount
expressed in percentages — we will reduce the price of a given product at least by that amount. The maximum reduction is always 60% of your price.
How to submit offers for the campaign
Go to the
Campaigns & Programs
tab and select the campaign with
AlleDiscount
in its name.
Next to each offer you want to submit, enter your
suggested price
. It is the value to which you decide to lower the price.  Next to products for which we require a discount of less than 25%, we automatically enter the suggested price for you — but you can change it manually.
Check
what the maximum price you can enter is
.
Select offers you want to submit ― you can select up to 200 offers at once.
Click [submit offers].

We will display an additional message next to offers where you provide a promotional price lowered by more than 25%. We will also show the number of such offers in summary — we will mark it red.

If we accept your submission, its status will change. If — for some reason — your offer gets rejected from participating in the campaign, you will also find that information in the
Campaigns & Programs — Manage participation
tab.
Once the program is over, submitted products will go back to their original prices automatically.
How you can manage your offers
Within the AlleDiscount program, you can:
withdraw offers from the campaign
edit offers that do not meet the program requirements (change: the Buy now option, the number of units, the VAT invoice option) and submit them again. If you edit the offer to obtain the higher discount in an artificial way, we will exclude your offer and your account from a given AlleDiscount campaign edition.

All you need to do is withdraw the offer from AlleDiscount, edit it, and submit again.

How to resubmit your offer for the campaign
Go to the
Campaigns & Programs — Manage participation
tab.
You will see the
declined
status next to the offers that have not qualified to the promotion.
Click the three dots next to the offer status and select [resign].
Go to the
Submit
tab to resubmit selected offers for the campaign.
When resubmitting your offer, take into account the new lowest price from the last 30 days.
How we calculate the discount rate
We take into account the price you provide and the market prices of a given product. No matter what the final reduction is, we will pay you full reconciliation up to the price you suggest when you submit your offer.

Your current price in an offer is 105 PLN.
You reduce it to 100 PLN to meet the requirements from the List of Goods (Maximum Price for that product is 100 PLN).
We reduce it to 90 PLN — with the Minimum Discount.
A buyer purchases the product for 90 PLN.
You get 90 PLN from the buyer and a 10 PLN reconciliation from us.
We charge you the sales commission based on the price you set when submitting the offer — that is 100 PLN (the price suggested by you).

Note that a discount for that product may have been from 10% (Minimum Discount) to 60% (the Maximum Discount for all the products from the List). That is why the discounted product may cost between 90 PLN and 60 PLN ― your revenue remains the same.
We will return the amount by which we reduced your price to your Allegro bank account when a customer buys your product. You will find it in the
Settlements with Allegro
tab as the
operation type
―
Reconciliation in the Allegro Prices Program
.
