Allegro Days is an event where we promote discounted offers — on the Allegro home page and in the
Deal Zone
. Check how it works and what you can gain.

During Allegro Days, we also launch the
AlleDiscount
campaign, where we reduce prices for you! Your offers are more attractive without extra costs — we pay you back the difference between the price set by you and the reduced price customers pay.

How the Allegro Days campaign works
Allegro Days is a cyclical promotional campaign. During Allegro Days, we also promote offers with discounted prices. We display a special
allegro days
or
-%X
badge next to such offers. Buyers see them in the promotion sections of the home page.
You can submit your offers even 2 weeks before the start of a given edition of the campaign. We will let you know about the start of the campaign in advance — in the
News
. You can submit your offers until the last day of a given edition.
Badges in offers within the campaign
The offers you submit for Allegro Days can get:
the standard
allegro days
badge
the
-X%
badge — the percentage of the discount you grant in the offer.

You submit an offer for Allegro Days and lower the product price by 25%. Buyers will see that offer with the
allegro days
badge or the
-25%
badge on an orange background.

Who can participate
You can participate in Allegro Days if you:
have an active business account
have at least
Neutral
sales quality
have set payouts
are not behind on your payments of invoices from us
you list offers where you sell products from the List of Goods that we share every time before the start of the campaign.
What you gain with Allegro Days
We will mark your offers with a special
allegro days
or
-%X
badge — buyers will see it on the offer page and the offer list. Additionally, we will display offers that qualified for the campaign on the
Allegro home page
and in the
Deal Zone
.
As soon as we charge the additional commission for a sale made in the campaign, we will
refund you 40% of its value
. We will return the money to your Allegro funds. You will receive the remaining 60% in the form of
Feature packages
(19.90 PLN/10 days). The number of packages will depend on the discount you set in your offer.
You can accept packages in the
Discount Center
— they will be valid for 3 months from when we grant them.
Where to check your Allegro Days campaign results
If your offers participated in the given edition of the Allegro Days campaign, you will find the results on the
campaign summary page
. If your offers did not participate in the campaign, we will display a message with that information.
What offers you can submit
For the Allegro Days campaign you can submit active offers that:
have products from the List of Goods, which we share every time before the campaign start
have products with a price that does not exceed the maximum price specified on the List of Goods
have the
condition parameter
value: New
are listed in the
buy now
format.
Additionally, you have to
reduce the offer price by at least 5%
from the lowest price of that offer from the last 30 days. You can set the price while submitting a given offer to Allegro Days.
You can submit offers with the
Medical device
parameter selected. It is possible when you sign the paper version of
this declaration
(available in Polish) and send it to us with the phrase: medical devices to the address: Allegro sp. z o.o., ul. Żelazna 51/53, 00-841 Warszawa.
You can submit an offer that is a part of a bundle. Find out
when we display offer bundles in campaigns and programs
.

How to submit offers for Allegro Days
You can do it:
in the
Campaigns and Programs
tab — select Allegro Days campaign with the correct date and provide the promotional price for selected offers
through the API
.
Once we check the submitted offers, we will automatically change their prices for the ones you declared. The
allegro days
badge will be displayed for a maximum of 72 hours (until the end of a given Allegro Days edition) — unless you choose a shorter time or resign before the campaign ends.
Your submission means that you accept the
Terms & Conditions
of the campaign. You can also find them in the tab where you can submit offers.
Learn more about how to manage your offers in campaigns and programs
.
How to submit offers in multiple marketplaces
We have added a feature to the
Campaigns & Programs
tab that allows you to submit your offer to Allegro Days in Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary simultaneously. The offer must meet the campaign conditions in the marketplaces where you want to submit it.
Besides submitting the offer to the campaign in other marketplaces, you can also manage its promotional price. We will convert it according to the European Central Bank's exchange rates. You can easily increase or reduce the promotional price. Just enter the percentage by which you want to change the price, and the system will recalculate it.
To submit an offer to campaigns in Poland, Czechia, Slovakia, and Hungary simultaneously:
go to the
Campaigns & Programs
tab
select the offers you want to submit to Allegro Days
click [submit offers]
you will see the
Submit these offers to other markets
overlay — select the additional marketplaces where you want to list your offers within the campaign
finally, click the [submit to selected markets] button.
If you do not want to submit offers to other marketplaces, click [skip] on the
Submit these offers to other markets
overlay. If an offer that you want to submit to the selected marketplaces does not meet the conditions in one of them, you can submit it manually. In that case, select a marketplace in the
Campaigns & Programs
tab and submit your offer to the campaign.
Learn more about how to manage your offers in campaigns and programs
.
What fees we will charge
We will charge you an
additional campaign sales commission
for the qualified offers. This additional commission is always 60% of the product sales commission.

You are selling a humidifier worth 300 PLN. The buyer selects the 0 PLN delivery. You list the product in a category with a 4% product sales commission. The 4% sales commission of 300 PLN is 12 PLN. The additional
campaign sales commission
equals 60% of the product sales commission — 7.20 PLN (12 PLN x 60%).

At the same time, we will grant you an equivalent of this additional commission in the form of a Features package.
Learn more
.
When we can exclude your offers from Allegro Days
In some cases, we can exclude previously qualified offers from the campaign. We can do so if:
your actions on Allegro cause harm to other users or our platform
you have outstanding payments for at least 2 settlement periods
you violate the provisions of the Allegro Terms & Conditions or the Allegro Days Terms & Conditions
we
block your account for selling illegal products
.
We can check if your offer is still attractive and meets the requirements of the Terms & Conditions at any time. If it is not that popular anymore — its price is no longer attractive compared to the prices of other offers on the market and Allegro — we may exclude it from the campaign.

The Allegro Days campaign cannot be combined with any other promotion. Learn more about the campaign in the
Terms & Conditions
.
