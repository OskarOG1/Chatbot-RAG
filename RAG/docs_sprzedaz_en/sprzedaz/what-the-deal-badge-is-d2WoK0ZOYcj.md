Within the
Deal
badge, we display the percentage of an offer price discount. We calculate it in relation to the lowest price of a product in that offer in the last 30 days before the discount. That price is displayed crossed out.
If you share your offers in the foreign marketplaces, the lowest price from the last 30 days will be
different for each marketplace
where you share that offer.

In offers that we display to entrepreneurs, we do not determine a separate lowest price from the last 30 days. It means that, for example, on business.allegro.cz, we take into account the price from allegro.cz. On business.allegro.pl, on the other hand, we take into account the price from allegro.pl.

What offers we grant this badge to
We automatically grant the
Deal
badge to offers that:
include
a new product
— we do not grant the Deal badge to products for which the
Condition
parameter is not available
have had
their product price reduced by at least 5%
, but
no more than 60%
, compared to the lowest price in that offer in the last 30 days in the given marketplace
have been active on Allegro for
at least 24 hours
— we can determine the lowest price in the last 30 days only after that period
are not listed in one of the
categories excluded
from the campaign
have not been excluded from the campaign because we found them unattractive
— to determine that, we mostly take the price and sales performance into account.
How we determine the lowest price
We check the lowest prices of a given product on Allegro and in the market over the last three days. We calculate the median based on this data. To get the badge, your price must be equal to or lower than the median of the lowest prices from the last three days. We update the prices once a day.
We exclude offers if their price after the discount is higher than the median of the lowest price of that product from the last 3 days
. We determine the product price median once a day. We also check the offer's sales results, popularity, and buyers' interest. We exclude some offers because we want to offer buyers attractive products at reasonable prices and ensure high quality of the Deal badge.

For the given product, we take into account the price median from both offers for entrepreneurs and customers with a regular account. It means that even if your discounted offer is the only or the cheapest offer of that product for entrepreneurs —
we might not grant it the Deal badge
if its price greatly differs from the prices of the same product for customers with a regular account.

Other
restrictions on promoting offers in a given marketplace
still apply. It means that the badge will not be available, among others, in offers of sellers who have been
barred from participating in campaigns
, or in offers with
medical devices on allegro.pl
, for which sellers have not completed the required consents.
How long we display the Deal badge
We will display the Deal badge in your offer for a maximum of
30 days
. If the offer no longer meets the badge conditions due to a change within this period, we will stop displaying the badge.

You update the price
of your offer to 10 PLN. The lowest price of this offer in the last 30 days is 11.10 PLN, so the discount equals about 10%.
On April 5,
we automatically grant this offer the Deal badge
for 30 days — until May 5.
On April 15,
you update the price
of this offer to 9.80 PLN. The current lowest price of this offer in the last 30 days is 10 PLN, so the discount equals 2%.
Your offer no longer meets the conditions of the Deal badge, so
we stop displaying it
even though 30 days have not passed yet.

You can also disable the Deal badge on your own. To do that,
contact us
.
In what categories we do not grant the Deal badge
The list of categories in which we do not grant the badge differs between marketplaces. Click a headline to see the excluded categories for a given market.
