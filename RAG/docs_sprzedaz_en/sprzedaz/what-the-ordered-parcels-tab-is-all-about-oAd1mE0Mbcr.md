In the
Shipments and pickups
tab, you can find all parcels you dispatch with
Ship with Allegro
in all marketplaces: allegro.pl, allegro.cz, allegro.sk, and allegro.hu, as well as courier pickups.

Ship with Allegro is a free tool that you can use on your business account. It allows you to conveniently dispatch parcels to your customers, print out labels, and order a courier pick-up.
Learn more
.

What you can find in the Ordered parcels section
In the
Ordered parcels
section, you can browse through
all the parcels you dispatched through Ship with Allegro within the last 180 days
. Next to each parcel, we will display:
tracking number
date of creation (dispatch)
delivery option
details of the sender and receiver
amount of money charged on delivery — if the buyer selected the POD delivery option (we will display
No
next to parcels paid for in advance)
information about courier pick-up — next to parcels for which you cannot order pick-ups through Ship with Allegro, you will see the status
not applicable / dispatched at a point
information about parcel cancellation.
When you click [details], you will also see:
delivery status  displayed on a timeline
amount of money to be paid on delivery
information about the ordered courier pick-up
parcel collection date
pick-up request ID
order number — click it to go to details in the
Orders
tab
parcel details that you completed in the dispatch form:
type — envelope or parcel
dimensions and weight
reference number
additional services ordered.
Use the buttons at the bottom of the window to order a courier pick-up or print documents for a given parcel. Check
how to do it for multiple parcels at the same time
.
What you can do in the Ordered parcels section
This section allows you to:
easily look up a specific parcel, check its status, and details
download a report detailing parcels
manage multiple parcels at the same time, for example:
order a courier pick-up — also for different carriers
print documents: labels and protocols.
You can also switch to other
linked Allegro accounts
and manage orders on each of them.
How to search for parcels
Above the list of parcels, you can find a search bar and filters that will make searching easier.
Enter the recipient's details (first and last name, company name, or street), order number, or parcel number in the search bar.
You can also use the filters:
date from
and
date to
— you will see parcels dispatched in a specified period of time
delivery option
— you will see only parcels dispatched with selected delivery options
pick-up
— you can see parcels with or without requested pick-up or all parcels together.

If you are using the
pick-up
filter, you will not see parcels with the status
not applicable / dispatched at a point
on the list. Those are parcels for which you cannot order a courier pick-up with Ship with Allegro — for example, parcels dispatched with Poczta Polska delivery options.

You can also use fast filters to check the list of canceled parcels and parcels dispatched at the pick-up point.
How to download a report detailing parcels
To download the report, select parcels and click [download report file] at the bottom of the screen.
A new window will appear. There, you can choose the data you want to include in the report. You can also select the file format — PDF, CSV, or XLSX.
How to manage multiple parcels at the same time
In the Ordered parcels section, you can perform the following actions with multiple parcels:
order a courier pick-up for different carriers at the same time
print documents — all types together or labels and dispatch protocols separately.
To do it, check the parcels and select [request pickup] or [print] on the green bar at the bottom of the page. You can select up to 100 parcels at the same time. You cannot select canceled parcels.
You can also download documents directly in the parcel details.

We generate labels in the format you select in
Ship with Allegro settings
— you can download them as individual PDF, ZPL, or EPL files. If you want to download:
all documents (labels and protocols together)
a few labels in different formats
you will download one ZIP file with all the selected documents.

What you can find in the Ordered pickups section
In this section, you can browse
all courier pickups ordered through Ship with Allegro in the last 180 days
. In this section, you will find:
pickup ID
carrier's name
number of shipments assigned to a pickup
filters to browse pickups by: order date, carrier, pickups ordered on a given day.
Expand the details of a given pickup to check, among other things:
list of shipments
courier pickup address
additional parcel protection
dimensions and weight of the shipment.
