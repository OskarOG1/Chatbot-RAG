The Performance index is the assessment of your One Fulfillment activity that we calculate based on data from five areas. We sum up the points you have earned in each of them and determine your result. It shows you the areas you are doing well in and the ones you need to improve.
When we display the Performance index
We display it if you use the service.
If you are just getting started in One Fulfillment by Allegro, we will start displaying the index 14 days after accepting your first products at our warehouse.
What constitutes the Performance index
You can score a maximum of 100 points. We calculate the Performance index for 5 areas of your activity.
Area
Maximum number of points you can get
What we check
Rotation in One Fulfillment
Maximum number of points you can get
40
What we check
We check whether your products have not been in the Allegro Warehouse for too long. To calculate the average stock rotation, we divide the average stock over the last 14 days by the average sales from the last 14 days. This result is also influenced by the sales dynamics and your customers' purchase decisions.
Active offers
Maximum number of points you can get
40
What we check
We check whether all of your One Fulfillment offers are active. You fully use the sales potential if you list offers for each product stored at the warehouse.You will earn the maximum number of points if all of your One Fulfillment offers are active.If an offer ends because your products have sold out, we will not lower your score. You can improve your result every day. All you need to do is activate offers for all products stored at the Allegro Warehouse.
Products without offers
Maximum number of points you can get
10
What we check
We check whether you have created offers for all products we are storing at the Allegro Warehouse.  If you have, we will grant you 10 points for this area. You can improve this result every day. All you need to do is activate offers for all products stored at the Allegro Warehouse.
Correct ASN
Maximum number of points you can get
5
What we check
We check whether the products you ship to us have an ASN with the correct status: pending delivery. You will earn points if you send us ASNs before you ship products to the Allegro Warehouse. See how to ship products to the Allegro Warehouse.
Compliance of delivery
Maximum number of points you can get
5
What we check
We check whether the products you ship to the Allegro Warehouse comply with the ASN. We check this for the last 4 shipments delivered to the warehouse. You will earn the maximum number of points if there are no discrepancies in all 4 ASNs.
