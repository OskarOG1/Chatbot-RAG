If you want to promote your offers in other marketplaces, use
Allegro Ads
.

Who can buy promotion options
The promotion options are only available:
on
business accounts
with
at least Neutral
sales quality level.

If the sales quality level on your account drops below Neutral — you have
7 days
to improve it before we disable Features in your offers.

In what offers you can buy the promotion options
You will only add them to offers that meet all of the conditions below:
Condition
parameter value:
New
,
Digital product
,
Collectible product
, or
Investment product
format:
buy now
the offer is
not a set consisting of different products
(for example, you cannot feature a shampoo and a conditioner listed as a set).
What promotion options are available
Feature and Flexible Feature
If you select one of those options when listing your offer in the
listing form
, it will be placed higher than the offers of other sellers, who have not used that option. It will appear on the list of promoted products in a given category — if it is also the Top offer of that product — and in the search results (except when searching by relevance).

If your offer is also linked to the product from the
Allegro Product Catalog
— it can become the
Top offer
and
represent the product
. That way, more customers will notice it.

If you choose:
Feature — we will be promoting your offer for
10 days
Flexible Feature — we will be promoting your offer for
1 day
.
Promotion on the category page
If you select this option, your offer will be featured on the page of the category you list your offer in. It will be placed in a special gallery of promoted offers. The gallery contains an offer thumbnail, price, and title. From there, buyers will go directly to your offer page.

You can manage
promotion on the category page
in the
listing form
, in the Promotion options section. You cannot do that in the
My Assortment
tab.

Your offer will be placed in a pool from which products are selected at random to be displayed on the category page. That is why the display duration and the number of page views depend on how many sellers decide to use this option.

We display up to 12 offers on one screen in the gallery. The offers change automatically when you refresh the page. When you click [see more offers], we will redirect you to the list of all offers promoted in a given category.
Many people browse category pages — this is the landing page for those who click the category name on the Allegro home page. That is why that promotion option is so attractive.

Go to the
Home and Garden
category and check how the promoted offers are presented on the category page.

Check the detailed
rules of promoting offers on the category page
.
How to use the promotion options
You can enable promotion options:
in the Promotion options section of the
listing form
— when you list offers
in the
My Assortment
tab or by editing an offer — if you want to promote an already listed offer
through the
API
.
You can check the exact promotion fees for a given offer in the summary before you list it.
How to enable Promotion options while listing offers
Go to the
listing form
.
Complete the information on the products you sell.
In the Promotion options section, select the one that fits your needs best:
Feature
Flexible Feature
.
Here, you can also check the benefits that come with each of the promotion options. To learn more, click on the arrow and read what each of the benefits means:
Chance to sell
— how high the sales will be in a given offer
Displaying offers higher on the list
— your offers will be more likely to appear higher in the search results than the non-promoted offers
Priority in the Top offer selection
— we will prioritize your offer when selecting the offer to represent a given product. If your offer becomes the one representing the product, we will display it above other, non-featured offers in the search results.
Possibility to appear in Google
— featured products have a chance to appear in the Google network (search engine, YouTube, display ads) as part of the paid promotion carried out by Allegro
Your offer with zero rates
— you can enable payment in installments in your offer. If the product price is lower than 1,000 PLN, we will display a special badge
Fee charged per day
— if you select the Flexible feature, we will charge the fees every day.
Managing the promotion options
In the
My Assortment
tab, you can:
enable or disable Feature or Flexible Feature at any time:
in a given offer
in several offers simultaneously — select the offers and click [promotion] on the bottom bar
check what offers you are currently promoting. We will display a special badge next to them, and by clicking on [details], you will be able to see more information about the option and planned changes in the offer promotion
change the promotion options in your offers
disable the promotion options for expired offers — that way, you will decide whether to continue promoting them after relisting.
You can also make changes to offer promotions through the
listing form
when you edit offers.

When disabling the promotion option, decide whether you want to do it instantly or at the end of the settlement period — in that case, you will fully use the promotion.

Why it is worth promoting offers
Our research shows that you increase the chance to sell a product even seven times.*
You can present your offers and products on top of the search results (except when searching by relevance).
We consider featured offers first when selecting the
Top offer — the offer representing a product
.
You can grant your customers access to extended periods of 0% installments.
Learn more about the
benefits of featuring offers
. Go to the
My Assortment
tab if you want to enable the promotion.
* The average number of transactions per 1 featured offer vs. the average number of transactions per 1 non-featured offer, Allegro internal data, 2020.

Payout of funds in the case of payouts with installments
In offers with the Feature or Flexible Feature option, we enable ZERO Rates. Buyers who purchase in such offers can pay for products in installments (you receive the entire amount at once).
To pay out the funds from sales in offers where the buyer has chosen payment in installments, you need to have the Przelewy24 option enabled in your Allegro Finance balance. If you still have not enabled it —
check how to do that
.
