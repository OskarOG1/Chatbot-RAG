Why you should set the same-day dispatch time in your offers
If you have the same-day dispatch time set and you meet the specified conditions, we display a special badge in your offers —
delivery tomorrow — buy before XX:XX
.

In some cases, instead of information about the delivery on the next day, buyers will also see information about delivery the day after tomorrow or on a specific day. It may result from the given carrier's shipping time exceeding 1 day.

The badge makes your offers stand out
. In addition, your potential customers know that when they buy in a given offer, they can expect to receive their order on the next working day. This may encourage them to buy from you.
But that is not all! Check what you can gain thanks to the fast dispatch.
You will increase your offer visibility
. We display offers with an earlier estimated delivery time higher in the search results. They also have a higher chance of
becoming a Top offer
.
You will achieve higher scores in sales quality
. If, in your offers, you declare immediate dispatch or dispatch within 1 day and you dispatch orders from those offers on the day of purchase, you can get up to 200 points for
your sales quality
(160 points in the
Fast dispatch
metric and 40 points in the
On-time dispatch
metric).
You will have an advantage over other sellers
. Fast dispatch and delivery is one of the factors buyers consider while making a purchase. That is why if we display your same-day dispatch time longer than for other sellers — customers are more likely to buy from you.
Buyers can look up your offers in a fast and easy way
. To do it, they can use the
Delivery time
filter and select
delivery by tomorrow
.
You will get a special badge
. If you have great results in the
Fast dispatch
metric in the sales quality, we will grant you a
special badge
. It will inform customers that if they buy from you, they can expect to receive their order on the next working day.
You can get a bonus in the Allegro Diamond Partners program
. With the same-day dispatch, you can meet one of the
program objectives
and receive, for example, a package of Features.
What conditions you need to meet so that we can display the same-day dispatch time in your offers
Customers expect the delivery information to be as accurate as possible. That is why we can only display the
delivery tomorrow — buy before XX:XX
information in offers where:
you declare to dispatch the parcel immediately or within 24 hours
and
you have enabled at least one next-day delivery option.

You can check the information about the delivery time for specific carriers on
this page
.

Apart from that, you also need to meet the following conditions:
in the
Ordering time settings
tab, you provide the same-day dispatch time — for specific carriers and days of the week
you have fulfilled at least 10 orders delivered by
integrated carriers
for whom you have set the same-day dispatch time
you have dispatched a minimum of 95% of the orders your customers placed and paid for
in the last 28 days
on the same day
your delivery time has not been extended due to delays in dispatching orders from the previous day.
To help you adjust offers to those requirements, we display a section with recommendations in the
Delivery Settings
tab. We suggest offers for which you can shorten the dispatch time. We do it on a regular basis — each Thursday. If you want to use our suggestions, you can edit the dispatch declaration in a quick and easy way.
To do it:
Go to the
Delivery Settings
tab.
In the
Increase your sales — get more badges with quick delivery
section, click [set the dispatch time].
You will see a list grouped according to the declared dispatch time and a recommended change of the dispatch time for each group.
Select the offer group and click [edit dispatch time].
You can also download a file with a list of offers for each recommendation. It will help you check the offers for which it is a good idea to shorten the declared dispatch time.

You specify the dispatch time in the
listing form
However, you can change it at any time:
separately for each offer
in multiple offers at the same time
.

If you set the same-day dispatch time in the

To learn more, go to the

The report will be saved on your device.
Open it and go to the Orders spreadsheet.

How to set the same-day dispatch times for specific carriers

Set the same-day dispatch times as late as possible — that will give you an advantage over other sellers and you will increase the chances that buyers will choose your offers. However, pay attention to:
setting the same-day dispatch time that will give you enough time to prepare parcels and hand them over to the courier
adapting the same-day dispatch time to the workflow of your business
setting the same-day dispatch time no later than the cut-off times carriers specified for the parcel collection. That applies to the
cut-off times for your location
, as well as the pick-up times you have set with the carrier.

You can set the same-day dispatch times in the
Delivery Settings
tab — in the
Ordering time settings
section. To make it easier, select the delivery market at the top of the tab. That way you will only see the carriers that carry out delivery in that market.
If you select
Poland and other countries
as the delivery market, you can see two sections:
Allegro Delivery
and
Other carriers
. Keep reading to learn how to set the same-day dispatch time in each section.
Check
how you can quickly and conveniently request a later courier's pick-up time

The Allegro Delivery section
In that section, you will set the same-day dispatch times for the delivery options available in the Allegro Delivery program.
Delivery with
Allegro One (Box) — same-day delivery
and
Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) — next-day delivery
is carried out by Allegro One Courier in selected cities. That means you will set the same-day dispatch times for those delivery options only if they are available for your location. Therefore, select the city you dispatch parcels from to enable time settings for those delivery options.
Next, to set the same-day dispatch time:
Check the delivery option for which you want to set the same-day dispatch time. You can select:
Allegro One (Box) — same-day delivery — carried out by Allegro One Courier in the selected cities and their surrounding areas
Allegro One (Point, Box, Courier) — next-day delivery — carried out by Allegro One Courier in the selected cities and their surrounding areas
Allegro One (Point, Box) — with that delivery option, you can set the same-day dispatch time for deliveries carried out by DPD
ORLEN Paczka
DHL.
Select the days for which you are setting a given time. You can set the same time for each day of the week:
from Monday to Friday
or
from Monday to Sunday
. If you want to set a different time for each day, click
Custom
and select the days for which you want to set a given time. If you want to set the time for other days, click
Set remaining days
.
Click [save changes]. Done!
The Other carriers section
In that section, you can set the same-day dispatch time for carriers that carry out delivery outside the Allegro Delivery program.
To do it:
Select the carrier and set the same-day dispatch time.
Select the days for which those times apply. You can set the same time for each day of the week:
from Monday to Friday
or
from Monday to Sunday
. If you want to set a different time for each day, click
Custom
and select the days for which you want to set a given time. If you want to set the time for other days, click
Set remaining days
.
Click [save changes]. Done!
