Weekly Smart! deals
is a permanent program that allows you to offer selected products at attractive prices to buyers with an active Smart! service.
What you can gain with the new program
a transaction rebate in the amount of
30% of the sales commission
in offers you submit for the program
we will grant your offers the following badge:
Smart! Only
we will promote the Smart! deals offers on Allegro — for example, in the Deal Zone.
What the program is all about
You can submit offers with products from the
List of Goods
at any time.
We will select a
possible display time
for your offer. This means your offer may not start participating in the program right after you submit it, but later — within 7 days of submission.
You can check the status of the offer you submitted and its planned display date in the
Manage Participation
tab.
Your offer will be in the program for up to 7 days
from the start of participation
— or less if it sells out before then.
Only buyers with an active Smart! Service can make purchases in offers within the program.
We
do not charge
any additional fees for participating in the program.
What the List of Goods is
The list contains products you can submit for the program at a given time.
We display several Lists of Goods — the one that applies this week, and the ones from previous weeks. Each list is appropriately described. We upload new lists on Mondays, usually around 10 a.m.

Who can participate in the program
You can join the program if you:
have an active business account
have at least a Neutral
sales quality level
are not behind on your payments of invoices from us
have read and accepted the Terms & Conditions of the program.
What offers you can submit
For the program, you can submit active offers in which:
you list a product from the List of Goods
you set the
Condition parameter
value to new
the product price does not exceed the maximum price specified on the List
you reduce the product price by at least 5% from the lowest price in that offer from the last 30 days before submission
you enable Allegro Smart! delivery.
You can submit an offer that is a part of a bundle. Find out
when we display offer bundles in campaigns and programs
.
How to submit your offers for the program
Go to the
Campaigns & Programs
tab and select the Weekly Smart! deals program.
Select the offers you want to submit.
Click [submit offers].
You can check the status of the offer you submitted and its planned display date in the
Manage Participation
tab.

Check the Terms & Conditions of the program
here
.
