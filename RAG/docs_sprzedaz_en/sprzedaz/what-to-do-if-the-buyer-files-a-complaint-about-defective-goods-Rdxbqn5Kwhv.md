As a seller, you are liable within
the statutory warranty (for non-consumer purchases)
or
the lack of conformity of the goods with the agreement (for consumer purchases)
if your offer description does not inform about defects or the product’s non-conformity with the agreement.
How much time you have to address the complaint
You have
14 days
of filing a complaint by the buyer to consider it. If you do not do it within that time frame, the complaint is deemed upheld by you.
Consumer Sales
In case of a lack of conformity of the goods with the agreement, the buyer has the right to file a complaint. As stipulated in the
Allegro Complaints and Returns Policy
, they can file a complaint through the complaint form.
Filing the complaint by clicking [file a complaint or issue] in the purchase details and completing the relevant form —  describing the issue and indicating how they prefer you to handle the complaint:
the buyer can select repair, replacement, repair or replacement, or — if the defect is significant — withdrawal from the agreement and a full or partial refund
the repair or replacement should be carried out within a reasonable time frame and be free for the buyer
if making the goods conform to the contract in the way the buyer chose is impossible or overly costly, you can:
replace the product when the buyer demands fixing it
fix the product when the buyer demands replacing it.
The buyer may apply for a full or partial refund when:
the non-conformance of goods is severe enough to justify an immediate withdrawal from the agreement or lowering the price
you refuse to make the goods conform to the agreement due to it being impossible or too expensive
you will not make the goods conform to the agreement
you are unable to make the goods conform to the agreement in a reasonable time or without undue inconvenience
despite your attempts to make the goods conform to the agreement (by fixing or replacing them), the non-conformance is still present.
If you accept the buyer's complaint and decide to refund them — you click [accept and refund payment] we will automatically insert the buyer's details and refund amount in the complaint chat.
Ensure the details are correct and click [refund payment].
The limitation period of claims
Check the key deadlines.
The complaint claim may expire after 6 years.
Within 2 years of delivery, the seller should be able to prove that the goods conformed to the contract at the moment of their delivery to the buyer.
Complaint chat
We created a separate chat for handling complaints to make the process more convenient. You will find the chat in the complaint details in the
Complaints and Discussions with buyers
tab. We will automatically start a chat between you and the buyer if the buyer selects at least one of the following reasons when filing a complaint:
product inconsistent with the offer — for example, its description, images, or parameters
product delivered with damage
defect identified during use
missing product elements — for example, a screw
my reason is not on the list.
Three days after submitting a complaint, the buyer can ask our consultant for help in the complaint chat.

In the complaint chat, you can also report the buyers' messages that contain:
threats — messages saying that you or your relatives will get hurt if you do not fulfill the buyer’s expectations
pornographic content.
To do that, use the [submit] option available next to the message you want to report. In the form, choose the reason for the report and confirm that you are sending it in good faith. You can also add a more detailed description of the situation.

How you can manage complaints
You can find all the complaints in the
Complaints and Discussions
with buyers tab. They are marked with a special badge —
Complaint
. To manage them:
Go to the
Complaints and Discussions with buyers
tab and select a complaint.
Make sure that the buyer provided all information necessary to process the complaint.
If the description lacks information or images that could help you process it, click [ask for additional information]. Enter the additional information you need and confirm you want to send the message. You and the buyer will see your message in the chat, below the steps of handling a complaint.
Step 1
: Select how the buyer should return the product. Click [without product return] if the buyer does not need to send the product back to you. If you click [schedule a return], specify how you will collect the product from the buyer.  We recommend taking this step within the first 3 days after the buyer files a complaint. This allows them to quickly deliver the product that the complaint concerns to you for inspection — if necessary. If the product complaint concerns an Allegro Smart! parcel and the time to withdraw from the agreement has not passed yet, you will see the Free return via Allegro Smart! option. When you select it, the buyer will be able to manually generate a free return label.
Step 2
: Make a decision regarding the complaint within 14 days of receiving it — by clicking [accept claim] or [reject claim].
If you accept the complaint, you can change the buyer’s preferred solution to another one.

We will not count closing a complaint in the complaint chat towards your sales quality
. Go through all the steps to handle complaints efficiently. You have 14 days to decide on the complaint.  If you do not inform the buyer of your decision within this time, they will be able to apply for a refund from the Allegro Buyer Protection program. We will add that compensation to your bill.

When the buyer sends the product back to you as part of the complaint
The product should come properly secured. The buyer does not need to send the product back in its original packaging or enclose a receipt.
What if you reject the customer's complaint
If you reject the customer's complaint:
refer to the specific parts of the customer's complaint. Indicate which ones are unfounded and why.
rely on factual arguments that are supported by evidence, such as expert opinions, technical documentation, or conversation transcripts
refer to the law or provisions of the agreement — in the justification, clearly state the legal or agreement-related basis on which you reject the complaint
be clear and precise — use simple language that the buyer can easily understand
respond to all of the buyer's claims, regardless of whether they relate to the product's non-compliance with the agreement.

If you refer the buyer to the warrantor, such as the manufacturer, this is not equivalent to you having processed the complaint.

If you reject the buyer's claims, according to the
Polish Act on consumer rights
(available in Polish),  you must return the product to them at your expense.
If the buyer could not send the product they complained about back to you because:
you did not provide information about how to send it back
you did not provide a return label
they received the return label from you too late to respond to the complaint within 14 days — for example, on the 13th day of the complaint's time limit
you cannot reject the complaint for this reason.
What if the buyer receives a product they have not ordered
If you accidentally send the buyer a product different than the one they have ordered, they should file a complaint due to the product's non-conformity with the agreement. Talk to the buyer and agree on:
how they can return the incorrect product — you can ask the customer to send the product back or leave it with them
how you will send them the correct product.
Complaint decision and return
If the product purchased by the customer does not comply with the agreement, is damaged, or has a defect, and the customer wants to file a complaint — you, as a seller, can expect the product to be sent back or made available for inspection. You cover the costs of repair or replacement, including, in particular, the postal fees and the cost of transport, labor, and materials.
Filing a complaint is free for the buyer — regardless of the decision you make. You are obliged to refund the buyer for all the costs they bore in regard to the transaction — for example, the total cost of assembly or installation of the device. The buyer should make the defective goods available to you so can fix them. As a seller, you are obliged to collect the product from the buyer at your expense — you can, for example, provide the buyer with a return label or collect the product on your own.
