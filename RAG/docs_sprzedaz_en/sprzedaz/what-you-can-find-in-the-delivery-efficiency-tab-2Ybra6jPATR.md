How the option: disable deliveries faster than my declaration works
This option allows you to change the estimated delivery time we display in your offers to better suit your preferences.
You can use it if you dispatch parcels faster than declared and do not want us to take it into account in the estimated delivery time. When you click [disable deliveries faster than my declaration], the estimated delivery time in your offers with declared dispatch within 2 and 3 days will not be shorter than what you declared.
You can use this option at any time. When you do that:
we will change the estimated delivery time in your offers within two hours
the new way of calculating the estimated delivery time will be valid for 10 days
. After that time, we will calculate the estimated delivery time using the standard method. In the
Delivery efficiency
tab, you can regularly check how many days remain until the end of that 10-day period. You can enable that option again after the 10 days are over.
you can change your mind and disable that option at any time
the new way of calculating the estimated delivery time will apply to all your offers with declared dispatch within 2 and 3 days. You cannot enable that option only for selected offers.
this change will not affect your sales quality. You still need to dispatch orders according to your declaration to gain points.
What you can find in the Delivery efficiency report
You can download the Delivery efficiency report in an XLSX file. It contains two tabs —
Statistics
and
Orders
.
The Statistics tab
In this tab, you can quickly and conveniently check the basic and most important data related to order processing. We use this information to award you sales quality points and to check whether we can display information about same-day dispatch in your offers. See below how to interpret that data.

On-time dispatch

No

On-time dispatch
metric in your sales quality.
Next to
Counted for Sales Quality
— in the cell to the right (D16) — you will see the number of all orders we currently include in the
On-time dispatch

The Orders tab
In this tab, you can check detailed information on the dispatch and delivery of orders that buyers placed with you. It contains all orders placed until the previous day, for which the dispatch declaration or the expected delivery time displayed in the offer at the moment of purchase expired within the last 28 days. We take into account the later of those dates.
This means the report contains orders from a period longer than the last 28 days.
Check what the data in each of the columns means
Link to order
— contains the order ID. Click it to go to the order details.
Date of order
— the day the buyer placed the order.
Time of order
— the time the buyer placed the order.
Date of payment
— the date and time the buyer paid for the order.
Declared dispatch time
— the dispatch time set in the offer at the moment of purchase. If the order consists of multiple offers, you will see the longest of the dispatch times declared in those offers.
Date of declaration
— the date and time by which you need to dispatch the order so that we consider it dispatched on time.
Order counts towards Sales Quality?
— check whether we include a given order in sales quality. If we do not, you will also find the reason for the exclusion here. It will be one of the following:
return
delivery option not taken into account in Sales Quality —
learn more
canceled order
order not taken into account due to the carrier's technical problems
order not taken into account due to not being scanned by carrier.
Evaluation start date
— the date from which we include the order in sales quality. It is the day following the declared day of dispatch or expected delivery time displayed in the offer at the moment of purchase. We take into account the later of those dates. If we do not include a given order in sales quality, you will see
order excluded
in that column.
Tracking number added?
— check whether a tracking number was added to a given order.
Order dispatched on time?

Yes

On-time dispatch

No

On-time dispatch

Not yet evaluated (until the date in the H column)

Fast dispatch

Not yet evaluated (until the date in the H column)

Date of addition of tracking number

Dispatch date

Delivery date

Tracking number (addition)

learn more

Carrier (addition)
— the carrier responsible for completing the order. We automatically add carrier information to the order, or you do it manually when you add a tracking number to the order.
How to use the Orders tab
Use filters to quickly find the information you need or conveniently browse the data. For example, use them to quickly check the list of orders that:
we include in your sales quality
have a negative impact on your results in sales quality metrics
we have excluded from your sales quality.
have not been sent as declared
have not been delivered by the estimated delivery time displayed at the moment of purchase.
What you can learn in the Statistics section
The
Delivery efficiency
tab also contains a statistics section. There, in the part called:
On-time dispatch
— you can check the number and percentage of parcels that you dispatched as declared or that were delivered by the expected delivery time. We grant you points in the
On-time dispatch
metric based on this data.
Fast dispatch
— you can check the number and percentage of parcels that you dispatched on the day of placing and paying for the order, or delivered no later than indicated by the longest
shipping time
for the selected delivery option, counted from the moment of paying for the order. We include only those parcels whose orders consist of offers with declared
immediate
or
1-day
dispatch.
At the bottom of the tab, you can find supporting statistics. We do not grant points for sales quality based on them. However, you can find them useful — for example, they indicate how well you handle dispatching orders.
In the section called:
Tracking numbers added
— check the number and percentage of parcels for which you managed to add a tracking number
Delivery time
— check the percentage of your orders that the buyers received:
on the day of purchase
the day after the purchase
2 days after the purchase
3 or more days after the purchase.
