If you offer delivery options that you settle directly with us
You will not get the payment for POD parcels directly from the buyer or the carrier. Our payment operator — Allegro Finance — will process it.
We will add the payment for the POD order to your current balance in the
Funds and Operations history
tab. You will see the
Payout settings in PLN for Allegro Standard POD parcels
section. To manage the payments, activate the payouts in the
Allegro Standard POD payments
section of the
Payout Settings
tab.
You will receive the payment for the order within 2 working days after the buyer collects the parcel. You will find the money in the
Funds and Operations history
tab — for orders from allegro.pl and other marketplaces. We will transfer the payments daily to your bank account. We will also inform you about it in the
Payment on delivery
section when you dispatch the parcel with Ship with Allegro. You cannot change the frequency of payouts or order automatic or on-demand ones.
You can get payments from several buyers within one transfer. It will be the total amount for all the orders from a given working day.
The title of the transfer with buyers' payments is as follows:
Wypłata Allegro Finance sp. z o.o. Id (payout number) login
.
You can find the payment information in the
Orders
tab. In the details of a given order, in the
POD details
section, you will find:
the amount paid by the buyer
the bank account where you can find the payment
the first and last name of the bank account's owner.
